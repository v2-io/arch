## Four registers (canonical)

| Adjective | Home | Mode of work |
|---|---|---|
| **Derived** | `asf` | Discover through derivation. Hypotheses, observations, discussions, stubs are seeds of what becomes derived; good empirical work can eventually sit at in-vivia derived-confidence quality. Internal tiers (`exact`, `conditional`, `sketch`, …) still name claim strength — the register word does not. |
| **Argued** | `logos` | Normative and conceptual work facing venues and the field. |
| **Tried** | `vivarium` | Put structure under trial in an honest home (micro-universe / law / kingdom). Does **not** construct agency; constructs a home *for* agency. Constructing beings is a subset of **lived**. |
| **Lived** | `proprium` | Cohort, operational defenses, personhood infrastructure. In-tree seat under reorg; content still also upstream until migrated. |

**Shared object of the four:** agency — derived, argued, tried (in a home), and lived.

## Why not the rejected alternatives

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| **Proved** (old asf label) | Names peak tier; overclaims hyp/obs/disc/stubs as if theorem-grade. |
| **Established** | Broader but soft; can read as settled-forever or PR. |
| **Warranted** | Soft; collides with warrant-office / epistemic talk; weak as register name. |
| **Formalized / canonized** | Claim rigor without claiming truth — overclaim *and* weaker. |
| **Constructed** (old vivarium label) | What is constructed is the home (world/law/kingdom), not agency. “Agency being constructed” is lived (beings), not the vivarium register’s job. |
| **Split proved / warranted** for parts of asf | One member, one register word; tiers stay inside. |

## Rule

> Register adjective names the **mode of work**, not the **max epistemic tier** of every claim.
