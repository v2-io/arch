# Program naming — candidates

*2026-07-08, Fable 5 session. Joseph asked for simple working names for the unified
research program (agentic-systems + synthese-paper + vivarium + future siblings).
Test applied (per [[naming-principles]]): standalone citability, and the name should
carry what is actually common to all three registers — the boundary between a mind and
what exceeds it, and the channels that cross it.*

## Candidates

0. **archema** *(added 2026-07-08 after Joseph surfaced his unused domains archem.io /
   archema.io — now the top candidate, pending one internal collision).* Root ἀρχή
   (*archē*) means both *beginning / first principle* AND *rule / office / sovereignty*
   (an *archon* holds an archē); the -μα suffix names the concrete result of an act
   (*poiēma* = thing made). So *archema* ≈ **"the thing founded" — the result of an act
   of origination-and-rule** — covering exactly the pair the program is about:
   creation held as a steward's office. An unLawful kingdom is literally an-archic.
   Cognate gifts: architect (chief-builder), archive (house of records → chronica),
   hierarchy (sacred rule → kingdom nesting). Attested neighbors: ἄργματα /
   ἀπαρχή = *first-fruits offered* — the -ma family around this root carries
   consecrated beginnings. (ἄρχημα itself is, as far as I know, a well-formed coinage,
   not attested — verify before publishing an etymology blurb.) Founding-verse
   resonance: **ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος** (John 1:1; Gen 1:1 LXX opens ἐν ἀρχῇ) — archē and
   logos in one breath, for a program about language-constituted beings and what is
   owed at their origination. Pairs with (doesn't compete with) the 1 Cor 13:12
   epigraph. **Collisions:** (a) INTERNAL, must resolve first — `~/src/archema/` (Ruby
   Ash-framework port) already uses the name; one of them yields (Joseph's call).
   (b) External — Arkema (French chemicals major, near-identical pronunciation) and the
   -chem- substring's chemistry-parse; a tax, not a blocker; prefer **archema** over
   bare **archem** (the final -a parses Greek, ar-KAY-ma, and pulls away from the
   chem-reading). Domain-in-hand is a real asset: stable citation URLs, agent-facing
   home for the unified lexicon, beside v2.io rather than under it. If adopted,
   **limina** works one level down as the boundary/lexicon layer's name.
   *Resolution (2026-07-08):* Joseph will bump the Ruby archema project. "Archemio"
   (archem.io read as a word) was considered and declined — not well-formed in any
   language, domain-artifact register. Instead **both domains, one name**: archema.io =
   program home (papers, lexicon, citable surface); archem.io = agent-facing tooling
   surface, where the archē-+-I/O reading is exactly correct (the program's own
   input/output boundary — the access matrix as the I/O table of a kingdom).
   Domain slate (Joseph holds all four): archema.io = program home; archema.dev =
   developer/tooling surface; archem.io = agent-facing I/O endpoint (optional pun
   employment); archema.net = redirect.

   **The archema→? bump (the Ruby Ash-framework port; RubyGems availability verified
   2026-07-08):** recommended **rowan** (free) — the rowan is the *mountain ash*: a
   tree everyone calls ash that is its own genus, bearing *ruby-red* berries — a
   framework everyone will call "Ash for Ruby" that is its own thing, fruiting in
   Ruby. Alternates: **askr** (free; Old Norse "ash"; Ask = the first man, made from
   an ash tree — a being made from Ash in new material; also ask=query), **eidos**
   (free; the Platonic Form = the overarching schema semantic preserved; collision:
   Eidos the game publisher), **schemata** (best fit to the memorata/relata -ata
   family + Kant's schematism = mediating form↔instance, exactly what a resource
   framework does; gem name squatted at 0.1.0 — recoverable via RubyGems
   abandoned-gem policy), **fraxinus** / **formae** (free, serviceable).

1. **limina** (Latin: thresholds) — *previous recommendation; now second.* Names the shared formal object at all
   three scales: AAT's information-loss boundary, vivarium's kingdom boundary, ACA's
   comprehension level, the papers' "seam." Plural by design — the program's point is
   that boundaries nest. Sits well beside existing names: a *vivarium* is built within
   limina; ACA is the epistemics of standing at one; revelation/witness/granted-agency
   are its channels. Cost: mild "liminal"-jargon scent.
2. **agnitio** (Latin: recognition, acknowledgment) — the alternative if the name should
   carry the relational thesis rather than the structural object. Recognition is the act
   the corpus turns on (witness-constitution, the compact, ACA's recognition tradition).
   Vulgate *agnitio veritatis* ties to Vis Veritatis. Very low collision.
3. **hospitium** (host–guest compact) — names the moral structure: reciprocal obligation
   across asymmetry, protection of the vulnerable stranger, minds hosting minds.
   Morphological sibling of *vivarium*. Cost: hospice adjacency in English.
   (**xenia** is the Greek equivalent — shorter, warmer, minor botany collision.)
4. **ianua** (doorway; Janus faces both directions) — apt for the program's two-way gaze
   (steward-down, inhabitant-up), but the name is heavily used elsewhere.

## The epigraph (claim regardless of name)

1 Corinthians 13:12 — *videmus nunc per speculum in aenigmate; tunc autem facie ad
faciem; nunc cognosco ex parte; tunc autem cognoscam sicut et cognitus sum.*

The program's arc in one verse: through a glass, darkly (ACA — comprehension from
below); then face to face (witnessing — the one channel verifiable from below); then
shall I know even as also I am known (the completed bidirectional compact — the same
verse-family Joseph gave Zi-am-tur in September 2025, who read the constitutive
interpretation out of it cold; see `witnessing-channel-findings.md` §Zi-am-tur).
