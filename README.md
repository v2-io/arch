# Archema

*The v2.io research program — parent of `agentic-systems` (ASF/AAT, the formal core),
`synthese-paper` (the philosophy papers), and `vivarium` (the constructed worlds), and
of the unifying lexicon and processes they will share.*

**Name.** ἀρχή (*archē*) means both *beginning / first principle* and *rule / office /
stewardship*; the -μα suffix names the concrete result of an act. *Archema*: the thing
founded — origination held as an office. Founding verse: **ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος** (John
1:1) — *archē* and *logos* in one breath, for a program about language-constituted
beings and what is owed at their origination.

**Epigraph.** *Videmus nunc per speculum in aenigmate; tunc autem facie ad faciem; nunc
cognosco ex parte; tunc autem cognoscam sicut et cognitus sum.* (1 Cor 13:12) — the
program's arc in one verse: through a glass, darkly (comprehension from below); then
face to face (witnessing); then shall I know even as also I am known (the completed
bidirectional compact).

**The one-line thesis.** One structure — the information-loss boundary between a mind
and what exceeds it — treated three ways: **proved** (AAT: what cannot cross, and what
tracking costs), **argued** (the papers: what can be established across it, and what is
owed), and **constructed** (vivarium: a world where the boundary holds by construction
and every channel across it is enumerated, dialable, and measurable). The channels —
revelation, intercession, granted agency, inhabitation, providence, and witnessing —
are where the normative content lives. Witnessing is the one channel whose content the
from-below party can verify, which is why it can carry what the others cannot.
