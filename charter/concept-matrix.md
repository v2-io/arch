## Reading the matrix

Three cross-cutting identifications deserve to be seen whole rather than per-row:

1. **One modal cut, three dresses.** Certificate-vs-zero (GUC Boundary 1), certification-vs-occurrence (ACA §6), Lawful-as-revelation-only (taxonomy F6.3): in each, the *reality* of a property and its *certifiability* come apart, and the program's ethics consistently attaches to what can be established (occurrences, costs borne, content-matches) rather than what would require the forbidden survey.
2. **One persistence template, three substrates.** $\alpha R \gt \rho$ (mismatch), $\mathbb E[\varrho_{\text{rg}}] \gt \mathbb E[\rho]$ (identity), and vivarium's realizability gate (tempo vs awareness-scale): the same inequality-shape governs tracking reality, remaining someone, and hosting someone — with the middle one's compensation channel provably relational.
3. **One choice, four formalisms.** Truthification vs strategic self-coupling (M4), covenant vs self-legislation (spike §8), honest-receiver vs truth-death (witnessing channel), readable vs corrupted residuals (ascent gating): the agent's stance toward truth is the single free variable on which reachability — by evidence, by witness, by mercy, by ascent — jointly depends.
