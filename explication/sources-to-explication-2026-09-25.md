## The open question (Joseph, verbatim)

> The big question before me right now is if, for philosophy work in particular, the relevant essays + submitted papers etc. *ARE* the logos (a name for a subset of explication), or whether they keep their own directory and have something that is more robust and ours that is the primary underlying substrate for any and all papers.

## The principle (Joseph, verbatim)

> With the existing papers scattered all over the place there was a lot of development that happened at the time of the paper coming together that then (ostensibly) got fed *back* to ASF etc. and integrated back upstream. That hasn't worked out so well. I realize now that a much more principled and effective process would be to make sure that upstream is *always* the source of truth and that explication never asserts anything or derives anything or (possibly) argues anything that isn't already upstream. As the papers are refined and receive feedback, the feedback is implemented/integrated upstream as needed *first*, and *then* the paper is modified to reflect the refined truth.

Note: this revises `CHARTER-DRAFT.md` §6 ("Develop in whichever register moves fastest; land on the receiving register's own grounding; back-propagate") and bears on §0's logos row and §3.2 ("The papers argue at the ports"), which treat logos *as* the papers. The charter is itself unratified.

Obviously that still allows a more mathematical treatment, which we will make as correct and meaningful as possible, to give insights and nuance that we backport to the paper's ideas [and vice versa some more]."*
