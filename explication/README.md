# Provisional Pipeline Parts

A clean, universally sequential publishing pipeline that untangles your steps. It perfectly blends the strict abstract deadlines of CS/AI with the traditional journal steps of Philosophy.
## 01_Targeting (RFPs & Venue Fit)

* What lives here: Lists of open Calls for Papers (CFPs/RFPs), conference deadlines, journal scopes, and rough notes mapping your ongoing projects to specific venues.
* The Action: Choosing where the paper will go before you finish tailoring the text. [1] 

## 02_Prepping (Manuscript Prep)

* What lives here: Active drafts, data plots, code frameworks, and text files.
* The Action: Writing the core argument and formatting it to match the target venue's style guide or page limits.

## 03_Vetting (Internal Review & Preprints)

* What lives here: Co-author feedback comments, advisor edits, and your polished preprint files.
* The Action: Circulating the paper to your trusted network and uploading it to [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) (for CS/AI) or sharing a working draft with a reading group (for Philosophy).

## 04_Submission (The Gateway)

* What lives here: The final metadata text file containing your exact title, abstract, keywords, and blind manuscript PDF.
* The Action: Completing the early "Abstract + Title Submission" deadline (crucial for CS conferences) followed by the final full paper upload. [2] 

## 05_In_Review (External Evaluation)

* What lives here: A holding area for papers that are out of your hands.
* The Action: The paper passes through Desk Review (editor checks if it is a fit). If it passes, it moves into Double-Blind Peer Review (sent to anonymous experts). [3, 4] 

## 06_Revisions (The Pivot Point)

* What lives here: Reviewer feedback sheets, your response letters, and new revision drafts.
* The Action: Writing a conference rebuttal or executing a deep journal "Revise and Resubmit" (R&R) overhaul. [5] 

## 07_Production (The Finish Line)

* What lives here: Accepted, "Camera-Ready" final versions, copyright forms, and published PDFs.
* The Action: Final formatting tweaks before the venue publishes the piece. [6]
