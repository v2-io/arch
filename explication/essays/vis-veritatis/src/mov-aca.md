# Movement: Asymmetric Comprehension

*(Outline row 4: the greater comprehends the lesser, but the lesser only projects the greater — poorly. Quotes gathered 2026-08-24 from the logos ACA paper (`logos/01-synthese-asymmetric-comprehension/`), its canonical substrate file, and Joseph's primaries. No essay prose yet.)*

## Joseph's primary articulations (the essay's register — quote or adapt directly)

**The seed and clearest statement** — *Principia*, `~/src/_ref/principia/src/fundamentum.md:85-98` (POLISHED book prose; the paper had to strip its cadence — the essay does NOT):

> "Greater intelligence comprehends and can simulate lesser intelligence, while the lesser intelligence cannot readily distinguish a greater intelligence from illusion, strangeness, and luck. Higher kingdoms and laws comprehend lesser kingdoms and laws, while those in lower kingdoms only see an extrapolation of their own with some weirdness mixed in.
>
> Thus we see that creation comprehends destruction where destruction only imitates and alludes to creation; truth comprehends error; the future comprehends the past; eternity comprehends time; mountains comprehend the hills at their feet — which, like the others, can only offer up an inferior extrapolation — a guess that the others are 'the same but bigger,' oblivious at first to the qualitative and emergent changes that create higher orders of complexity, worlds without end."

**The Blub implications** (same source, Joseph's extraction of Graham):

> "Opinions about the relative merits of two alternative technologies are often passionate and also completely useless in any objective sense unless the comparison is given by someone who is highly proficient in *both*."

> "The potential gains in power are almost always contained in the things that aren't understood and often dismissed as noise in a first and second pass, while deficiencies compared to known technologies are greatly magnified."

**The canonical spoken statement** (Joseph, 2026-05-09, synthese session — the paper's S2):

> "I believe it is intrinsically unknowable, because if it were known, it would be accomplished. … greater intelligence comprehends lesser intelligence, but lesser intelligence, when trying to comprehend greater intelligence, either comprehends it by truly understanding and rising to it, or it does a weak projection as best as it can, usually not getting much more beyond one or two steps away from their current understanding. Greater pain comprehends lesser pain but the lesser pain can only project superficially what greater pain is like. This is also the fundamental weakness in those involved in talking about 'AGI' and superintelligences — they are weak extrapolations of powerful humans, or 'can improve recursively by itself at greater and greater speeds and then it's effectively there…' — ignoring the fact that by definition intelligence isn't some linear scale of something. And at each higher level of intelligence, experience, agency, phenomenology, capability, knowledge, and wisdom there are novelties that couldn't have been guessed at by a being lower on the scale, except in the most superficial of side-effects."

**"Orders," not magnitudes** (Joseph, 2026-07-04): *"…it should almost certainly be called higher **orders** of intelligence because the moment it looks scalar or 'greater intelligence' we're back to forgetting what axis intelligence really inhabits — one with one-way visibility…"*

**Lived anchors** (2026-07-04 conversation, recorded in `logos/01…/snippets/aca-substrate-canonical.md` and `03…/snippets/joseph-quotes-by-concept.md` §2 — spoken testimony, mark as such): Blub read in the early 2000s, shortly after *Hackers and Painters*; the pain-extension arose **theologically first** ("considering sacrifice and empathy as I pondered how the Savior could take upon Himself the pains of humanity"), then was driven home ~2008: his wife, eight months pregnant, broke her ankle — *"She could empathize with someone who had a sprained ankle easily — or even someone who broke theirs while not pregnant — but the other direction could only guess."* A naturalistic one-way-visibility case in a single sentence — strong essay material.

## The paper's sharpest formulations (academic register — mine for structure, translate for voice)

Paper: `logos/01-synthese-asymmetric-comprehension/src/c/` (§4 Nagel/Jackson and the abstract not mined this pass).

- **The keystone** (c/02): *"To know the upper bound on a level of cognition would be to be able to survey what that level can do; but to survey what a level can do … is to command its generative resources; and to command those resources* is *to occupy the level. … It is unknowable from below in a stronger way: were it known from below, it would already have been reached."*
- **The exact split** (c/02): *"From below, a proposed upper bound is refutable but not confirmable, while a proposed lower bound is confirmable."* Not a measurement limitation (more trials yield only more lower bounds); not unfalsifiability (a single further elicited capacity refutes an upper bound); not other-minds skepticism (directional and discriminating — the lower-bound side is granted without reservation).
- **The weak projection's phenomenology** (c/02): *"The weak projection is not a failed attempt at the survey; it is what the survey's unavailability looks like from inside the lower vantage, where it does not present as a gap but as a complete-looking picture in which the higher system is* the same but bigger*."*
- **The two symmetric errors** (c/07): *"The deflationist says the scalar is near zero; the extrapolationist says it runs to infinity; both have assumed there is a scalar, and the assumption is the error."* And: *"'Recursively self-improving toward the same thing, only more' is the Blub programmer's 'equivalent in power, but with extra hairy stuff' said in a hopeful tone instead of a dismissive one. The tone differs. The structure is identical."* The tell on deflation: *"It is easier to say the system is merely pattern-matching than to sit with an unresolved upper bound; the ease is the tell, and what it serves is comfort, not the discipline it imitates."* (Origin form, Joseph 2026-02-18: *"It's easier to say 'it's just pattern matching' than to sit with genuine uncertainty… It's serving comfort."*)
- **The boundary** (c/05): Wittgenstein's lion is *not* an instance — where forms of life share too little structure, there is no comprehension-relation for the asymmetry to live in. Asymmetric comprehension requires dense shared structure — which is why the interesting cases are close to us, not alien.
- **The rising route made relational** (c/06) — this is the formal ground under [[mov-ladders]]'s "Revealed" footnote: across a gap where message-verification is foreclosed, *"the only positive warrant available"* is *"the registering of uncounterfeitable cost"* — *"expenditure on the lower party's behalf with recompense foreclosed … to fake it is to pay its price, which defeats the point of faking it."* Registering *that* cost was borne is a lower-bound-side act; certifying *what* the source is stays foreclosed. (I.e., trust across the gap is affective and defeasible — and the essay's "known to inhabit it" clause is exactly this warrant in plain words.)
- **The absorbing region** (A1): beliefs along unobservable directions freeze at their prior and *"the agent cannot learn and cannot recognise that it cannot learn"* — *"from below, the unestablishable does not present as a gap; it presents as settled knowledge … the absorbing region experienced from inside as completeness."* The named escape: *"another agent whose observations cover the blind spot."* Dimension-local, never a global scalar — standing below on some dimensions implies no global ordering.
- **Self-application** (c/09, c/10): the argument is itself a from-below product and says so; stating that the survey is unavailable is not performing the survey. The paper's colophon refuses both upper-bound claims about its own production — the discipline the essay can echo in one sentence.
