## Working Notes

*(2026-08-24 session — the term discussion behind the footnote, so it doesn't have to be re-derived.)*

- **The question:** whether "Revealed" (the fifth source in 3.2, beside Derived / Argued / Tried / Lived) needs a more clinical term. Vivarium's LEXICON.udon was read whole as the estate's existing structural treatment.

- **What the lexicon already holds (§7, kingdom/participation ontology):**
  - *Revelation* is defined non-mystically as a channel classification in an access matrix: **"the sole noumenal-VISIBILITY channel from inside"** — with degrees along four named dimensions: throughput, latency (*gradual "line upon line" vs on-demand oracle*), receptivity (*ignorable/rejectable → decays*), comprehension-capacity.
  - Its siblings complete the channel set: Intercession (sole noumenal-*mutation* channel from inside), Granted Agency / Inhabitation (endo lawful action), comprehension (learn law from data — *never exact*), illusion (exo shows a law-violating phenomenon while law is unchanged).
  - META — knowing the frame at all — comes only by *"Revelation / correct guess"* for an endo agent; and *"revealed-or-correctly-guessed META lets an endo agent begin to conform itself toward higher law."*
  - The clinching structural argument, in `realized-kingdom`: *"Gödel: a system cannot prove its own consistency from inside — 'Lawful' is a noumenal property an endo agent cannot self-verify, exactly N-VIS = Revelation."* I.e., there is a class of truths (frame-truths, consistency-of-law, META) **provably unreachable by the other four sources** — reachable only through an agent with native access choosing to disclose. Access-matrix one-liner: *"the WHOLE distinction lives on the noumenal row — exo = native, endo = mediated."*
  - The moral-center note ties it to the wider program: *"'Dad reveals' is a structural role, not a metaphor."*

- **Term candidates considered:** (1) keep **Revealed** with a structural gloss — the lexicon has already de-mystified it, and any substitute is the same referent in a lab coat; (2) **disclosed** — most clinical near-synonym preserving the asymmetry (a discloser with access the receiver lacks; mild Heidegger baggage); (3) **testimony** — slots into analytic epistemology's canonical source-taxonomy (perception / memory / inference / testimony) but is ordinarily peer-to-peer and would need "from native access" as a standing qualifier; (4) imparted / received — too soft, loses the specificity. **Landed (Joseph, 2026-08-24): keep "Revealed" + footnote;** the full access-matrix machinery stays home in the lexicon, at most a pointer for the reader who wants it.

- **Why Joseph's footnote formulation is *stronger* than the lexicon's for a cold reader:** the N-VIS/Gödel form ("some truths only arrive this way") is true but unfalsifiable-sounding. His form makes revelation **verifiable in retrospect** — taught the shortcut by someone *known to inhabit* the higher understanding, and *once inhabited, self-proved*. That is exactly the ACA's **rising route** (the lesser comprehends the greater by truly rising to it, vs. the weak projection): **you can't survey it from below, but you can climb and then see** — the Blub shape. The claim converts from "trust me" into "here's a path; the proof is at the top," which a skeptic can engage without any faith commitment.

- **Load-bearing dependency inside the essay:** *"known to inhabit it"* is doing real work — it leans on movement 2's trust-calibration material (credibility of those who have embraced truth *at personal cost*, vault `# truth` §1). The footnote quietly requires that movement; keep them consistent if either moves.

- **Held in reserve (not in the footnote):** the Gödel/N-VIS argument converts the fifth source from confession-of-faith into a structural-necessity claim — *if* truths of that class exist, this is the only path they can travel. One sentence of it could enter the essay body later if the footnote proves too thin under readers' pressure; the primary is `vivarium/LEXICON.udon` §7.2 (`realized-kingdom`) + §7.5 (access matrix).

- **Adjacent caution (belongs to 2.4, noted here so it isn't lost):** the lexicon's terms carry carve rules and a cross-project collision ledger ("law" ≠ probability's "law of X"; "regime" deliberately fuzzy, consumer-relative; kingdom/regnum/regula cognate structure).
