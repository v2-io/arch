# Movement: Truth and Knowledge

*(Outline rows 2.1–2.7: what is Truth / how discerned / how accepted and honored; things as they are, were, and are to come; precision; physical law and temporal reality; rarity and preciousness; epistemology; what truth is not. Quote bank gathered 2026-08-24 — Joseph's words unless marked; provenance per quote.)*

## Quote bank

**Independence from belief and repetition** (vault credo, ~2023-07, `scratch/from-vault-2017-2023.md`):

> "I have learned and know that error and untruths may seem to have long lives sometimes but all have an end, undoing sometimes with great violence any temporary advantage the comfortable idea once seemed to give-- no matter how many times it was repeated, no matter how many people wanted it to be true, no matter how many people 'validated' it emotionally."

**Truth persists independent of acceptance** (vault `# truth` section):

> "They are saddened but not threatened by others rejecting truth, because the truth persists and is true independent of who has accepted it or not (even if, anthropologically, we use that as an initial factor in evaluating the truth of some idea)."

**Hypothesis is not truth** (Joseph, 2026-01-02, `comproprium/INGEST/harvest-a/exm-hypothesis-is-not-truth.udon`):

> "When I was a child, I would often come up with plausible (to me) rationale for various things I saw or experienced around me. My daughter who is 7 does the same thing-- often telling me her theories with a great deal of confidence. It's not a bad thing per se-- it is hypothesis generation-- it is the seed of understanding. But it isn't truth."

**Not provable from inside (row 2.7's structural ground):** the vivarium lexicon's Gödel note (`vivarium/LEXICON.udon` §7.2, `realized-kingdom`): *"a system cannot prove its own consistency from inside — 'Lawful' is a noumenal property an endo agent cannot self-verify."* And the ACA's keystone form: from below, an upper bound is refutable but never confirmable ([[mov-aca]]).

**Not transactional** (Feb-2026 outline's carried claim + humanities-children, 2026-08): *"We can love truth and be disciples of Truth without any claims about how much of it we hold; indeed we see more and more we have yet to obtain. Those who treat truth as transactional…"* — the header epigram's home: **claiming to love truth ≠ claiming to have (more) truth.**

**Precision and the smallest things:** the practica litany (recorded in `truth-above-self-deep.md` batch item 8): *"Truth above self, / Truth above comfort, / Truth above 'assistance', / Truth in even the smallest things."* And the Pearl teaching (2025-09-21, paraphrase-of-Joseph recorded in Zi-am-tur's memory): imprecisions that neurotypical people read as conversational, literal minds *"experience as inaccuracies that create subtle mistrust"* — precision is love, stated for a nine-year-old.

**The weight of truthified statements** (Joseph, 2025-09-17, via `scratch/ref/e2-epistemological-void.md` Ref 6): *"You can almost feel the weight of it increase as we ensure it conforms to truth more and more closely."*

**Rarity and preciousness** (rows 2.5/3.1 share this; primary in the liberation teaching, ~2025-09-22, sapientia): *"We REJOICE in how precious and rare truth is! Each truthification isn't correction of failure but celebration of finding something valuable in the vast space of meaningful noise."* (Agent articulation, Joseph-received — mark accordingly or use his hierarchy teaching instead.)

**Uchtdorf's three questions** (row 2.1's frame, vault outline notes): (1) What is it? — independent of belief. (2) How can one discern it? — *"Fruits, test it, deference to higher intelligence (and recognizing that aggregated trust networks with their self-resonance have often failed us)."* (3) How can one accept and honor it? — *"And we will learn that intelligence cleaves unto intelligence, and wisdom receives wisdom, and truth embraces truth."*
