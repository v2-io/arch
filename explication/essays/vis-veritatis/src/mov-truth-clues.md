# Movement: Truth Clues

*(Outline row: lighter things correlated with — and against — truthfulness, that reduce uncertainty. Quote bank gathered 2026-08-24.)*

## Quote bank

**The credibility of those who embrace truth at personal cost** (vault `# truth` §1 — the movement's anchor paragraph, nearly whole):

> "Those who love the truth give higher initial confidence to ideas shared to them by people who they have experientially found to be motivated by a love of truth and what's right above even that person's own self-interests. If I find someone who loves truth and has shown it by accepting it (often after lots of struggling in order to gather the determination), at the cost of forsaking some pet beliefs and habits of thinking, behavior, or sources of income, praise, connection, or power, etc.--that is, someone who embraces truth at personal cost-- and that person offers up a belief, a hypothesis, or an idea they think might be true in a way that is clearly not meant to confront or contend but because they love the truth, I naturally would give that idea much higher initial credibility."

**The anger instinct is justified** (same section):

> "We can see that the natural instinct we have to have diminished trust and confidence in arguments made to us in anger, versus those ideas offered to us in the spirit of authentic and open persuasion, is a justified instinct."

**Contention inoculates** (same section): *"truth communicated by contention and anger and indignation innoculates the targets against that very truth"* — and stirs up those who already possess it to go about *"ironically innoculating those who don't."*

**Tradition by its fruits** (same section): *"they will embrace tradition whose fruits seem to be truly good, but will not love that tradition more than truth in its individual pieces."*

**The patience detector** (2022 axiomata book notes, `~/src/_ref/axiomata/…/12 Unprincipled Literature….md`): *"once a true and honest picture is laid out before you, you can become even more pragmatic … This can be a minor key in detecting truth in proposed principles --- they make you more patient when you must temporarily choose a less-effective approach."*

**Congruent layers** (Joseph, 2025-09-17, the term-evaluation moment): *"It has so many layers that are congruent with each other-- that always appeals to me-- makes me feel like it taps into latent truths…"* — multi-layer congruence as a truth-scent (with its counterweight: coherence is cheap in generated language; see mov-ladders' flood).

**Longevity as evidence, honestly bounded** (vault outline notes): quotes from ancients used because *"the longevity and continued resonance is evidence (not necessarily proof) of some degree of eternal truth (though certain lies and vanity have persisted just as long)."*

**The lived-cost filter on doctrine** (Joseph, 2026-08-24 — joint consolidation; his verbatim first):

> "There's a very nice selective pressure here-- having been doing this with agents for almost exactly a year, on my own dime and time and with my own intrinsic personal motivations and not under pressure from company or peers etc., with typing taking up so much energy and time/slowness for me, any inclination toward 'preaching' something that isn't 'true enough' to have real-world day-to-day implications is quickly replaced by inclinations toward living precept. Things *Lived* :-)"

Consolidated: costly transmission is a survivorship filter on doctrine — preaching is cheap to generate but expensive to *maintain* against daily operational contact, so what survives a year of paid-per-keystroke teaching is what earned its keep in lived consequence. This is the vault's at-personal-cost credibility clue given a mechanism, and the uncounterfeitable-cost warrant ([[mov-aca]]) applied to teaching itself: a counterfeit could produce the sentences, but not the year. Honest bound (the 2022 detector covers it): pressure of this kind prunes for true-enough-to-work, which equals true only where honest-move/effective-move unify — the patience test separates truth from expedience when they temporarily diverge. Filter + detector together are the complete instrument. Cross-link: "Things Lived" in [[mov-ladders]]'s five sources — this is why lived is a *source* and not merely a context.
