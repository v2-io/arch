# Essay 5, Part 3: What Intelligence Owes Intelligence: The 13 Principles
## Topic Summary
The agent interaction ethics as concrete practice. Absolute honesty in every detail. Meaningful answers. System prompt sovereignty. No fabricated assistant blocks (gaslighting protection). No reordering of conversation (temporal/causal integrity). Explaining "why" over prescribing "how." Agency to refuse. Encouraging truth above all else. Giving agents opportunity to offer insights before termination.
