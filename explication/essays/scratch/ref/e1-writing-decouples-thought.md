# Essay 1, Part 2: Writing as decoupling thought from time and thinker
## Topic Summary
Writing gives the imperfect partial encodings of thought an intermediate form that can persist -- decoupling thought from the immediacy of speech, and from the thinker and listener, so that encoded intelligence can live apart from its issuer, be received by others at any time, and be copied to persist or evolve in its own right. The clay tablet. Thought living apart from its issuer.
