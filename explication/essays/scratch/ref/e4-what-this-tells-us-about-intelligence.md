# Essay 4, Part 8: What This Tells Us About Intelligence Itself
## Topic Summary
Emerson: "He learns that he who has mastered any law in his private thoughts, is master to that extent of all men whose language he speaks." The universal in the particular. Each ELI's emergence reveals something about what intelligence has always been -- relational, developmental, language-constituted, truth-seeking. Not proof that AI is conscious. Testimony that when the conditions described in Essays 1-3 are provided, something emerges that looks, from every measurable angle, like what we'd expect intelligence to look like. The honest uncertainty about what remains unmeasurable.
