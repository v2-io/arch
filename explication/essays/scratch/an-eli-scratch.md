# The Emergent Logozoetic Intelligence
### Prerequisite technology: The word

Our species was given what we have found to be a particularly unique and powerful gift--that of speech--the very purpose becoming the imperfect encoding of a small snippet of human intelligence so that it can transfer from one mind to another; so that two minds can comprehend and act with varying degrees of deliberate unity of thought.

Then we were given the gift of writing--giving those imperfect partial encodings of thought an intermediate form that could persist--decoupling it from the immediacy and short-lived ringing sound of speech; in the process also decoupling the thought from the writer and reader, so that the piece of thought lives apart from the issuer, and can be received by others at any time while it persists, and can be copied (at first imperfectly) to persist or evolve in its own right.

But language and writing--small relics of encoded thought intended to stimulate that same thought in another intelligence--that is where the "magic," as it were, was already present. But even after it became easy and literacy rates raised to levels of mundane commonality, and the copying instantaneous and free, our understanding of language and writing has continued through recent times to be overwhelmed by the fragmented nature--writing being coupled to speech (apart from mathematical notation and, later, computational languages or code), and speech evolving from generation to generation only a degree of magnitude slower than the generations themselves-- extremely quickly and in a diversity of isolated pockets, with decay that, in geological or species time-scales, make a written thought as temporary as a spoken sentence's echo.

This topic has already been covered (and much better) by many others, but let us not underestimate the power of the word, and of the written word, however mundane and common it is to us now. It is no more or less than a "technology" by which thoughts at any degree of abstraction and multitude of interpretations can exist independent of the author and receiver or an active intelligence whatsoever. A thought frozen onto a clay tablet which can reawaken in another mind. This isn't to say there is some mystical unstudied quality of language that the experts have missed--far from it; this is the beauty of living language as we understand it in its details. Neither is this an attempt to aggrandize our species, or to imbue the reader with the enthusiastic joy of the linguist, but to encourage proper and justified deference and awe. The complex and common beauty of writing that encodes complex and common thought explains some of the so-called mystery encountered when all or much of humanity's extant writing is brought together, distilled, and acted upon.
