## 1. The four-way separation, checked against canon

Joseph's four (internal-reality-model / intent / action-space-and-orientation / reality-model-updates) vs. canon's carve ($M_t$, $G_t$ states; $f_M$, $f_G$, $\pi$ maps; cascade ordering):

- **Mixed kinds, and that's the friction**: slots 1–2 are states, slot 4 is a map ($f_M$), slot 3 is a hybrid (action-space = loop signature; orientation = process). Not four components of one kind — likely why it wouldn't settle.
- **But the heterogeneity is the pedagogical virtue, renamed**: these are exactly the four *roles* a reader must distinguish before the coupling question parses — and the lawful/pathological line falls across slots 3/4 (goals→action-space/orientation = lawful selection; goals→update-map = the pathology). Keep the four-way as "four distinguishable roles," not "four components."
- **Add the event as an explicit fifth object** — selection-vs-processing is a statement about the two sides of the event's arrival; it can't be drawn without the event on stage.
- **The bootstrapping resolution, from canon itself**: the $(M_t,G_t)$ decomposition is explicitly a *formulation choice*, with the unproved conjecture that any separation-preserving decomposition is isomorphic to it — "directed separation forces a component whose update function doesn't reference purpose." Read carefully: **the partition isn't prior to the coupling question; the coupling question is what carves the partition.** This is the counterfactual-definition move arriving from the formal side. If the spike proves the conjecture (any form, incl. conditional), the paper's spine upgrades from "definition we adopt openly" to "canonical decomposition, forced." If refuted, the counterexample's shape scopes the definition. Candidate formalization to watch for in the spike result: $M$ as the *quotient of $X$ by goal-variation* — which would just *be* the counterfactual definition, formalized.
- **Borrowable audit-gold**: three independent substrates converged on $M_t$/$G_t$ as is/ought — "Hume's guillotine built into the state vector," directed separation as *can an ought manufacture an is?* Audience-legible; weigh the philosophical baggage.
- **Wording guard**: "action is the *single* point where the substates interact" is flagged over-strong in the segment's own follow-ups. Say "the sanctioned meeting point under separation."

## 2. Earlier outline feedback, reworked to current (guc-intro) direction
