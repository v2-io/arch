## 1. The four-way separation, checked against canon

- **Mixed kinds, and that's the friction**: slots 1–2 are states, slot 4 is a map ($f_M$), slot 3 is a hybrid (action-space = loop signature; orientation = process). Not four components of one kind — likely why it wouldn't settle.
- **But the heterogeneity is the pedagogical virtue, renamed**: these are exactly the four *roles* a reader must distinguish before the coupling question parses — and the lawful/pathological line falls across slots 3/4 (goals→action-space/orientation = lawful selection; goals→update-map = the pathology). - **The bootstrapping resolution, from canon itself**: the $(M_t,G_t)$ decomposition is explicitly a *formulation choice*, with the unproved conjecture that any separation-preserving decomposition is isomorphic to it — "directed separation forces a component whose update function doesn't reference purpose." Read carefully: **the partition isn't prior to the coupling question; the coupling question is what carves the partition.** This is the counterfactual-definition move arriving from the formal side. If the spike proves the conjecture (any form, incl. conditional), the paper's spine upgrades from "definition we adopt openly" to "canonical decomposition, forced."
