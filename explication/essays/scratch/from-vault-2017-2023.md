### 2. Remember: Cognitive load
*(From Sweller, van Merriënboer & Paas[^1])*

1. *Intrinsic*: Load inherent to the material itself (its element interactivity / irreducible complexity).
2. *Germane*: Load devoted to actually building schemas, i.e. the useful part of the effort.
3. *Extraneous*:  Load imposed by poor presentation/design that contributes nothing to learning. Avoid, identify, isolate, and remove.

>  This tip is a combination of writing and sound design experience, but it’s one I use for every single show I create:  when cutting together an interview or sequence, do your first draft. Then, close the session and make a list of what  happens in that content from just your memory

Those who love truth more than self are willing to subdue ego and comfort in order to accept greater truth when presented and evidenced, and are also careful to not to adjust beliefs that might actually be rationalization or justification for self-validation, convenience, self-justification, and so forth in the service of appetites, passions, and base biological feedback. [e.g., running and heartrate monitor and self-rationalization]

Those who love the truth recognize those biases and biologies that bias us toward self-preservation at the expense of truth, that can alternately bias us toward more initial truth, and it seems to be true that some widely distributed cognitive biases that have, anthropologically, preserved and multiplied us, have done it by helping us accept or abide by certain truths in the absence of more evidence and in certain circumstances, but that they may in other circumstances bias us away from greater truths. [e.g., accepting short-term gain instead of great longer-term works much better when "real life" is in more effect and chaos guarantees that we can't forecast and extrapolate nearly as much as we would like; but it (comparatively) hurts us when longer-term stability is probable (experiments often [and appropriately] ignore or control for this real-world variable). The ideal solution is fundamentally unknowable unless we have full knowledge of the future, but even having a reasoned estimate of the likelihood of those future returns being realized gets one past self-preservation biases that might serve us well in immediate short-term high-consequence with lots of unknowns and chaotic situations, and allow us to preserve self and others (and knowledge of truth) better.] That is, instincts and emotions are recognized by appropriate degree for the incidental/anthropological/biological truths that they may make available to us-- a gift for chaotic times from our fathers and mothers throughout history-- but are made subservient to more information and understanding that may be available to us in a specific context or situation-- they serve us instead of us becoming slaves to them. They are our "priors."

For many, many generations the children of men were subject to becoming captive, whether to hunger or hatred


In many places in the world it is still the same, but in others we see the rise of instruments of great noise and confusion. These instruments of communication have the capacity for high fidelity and to propagate truth and allow for deeper connections among the children of men, but are not in themselves truthful or uplifting. That is, they are easily corrupted. The hubris is the same, the feeling that we know just about everything except some details around the edges. Such an abundance of information and opinion, indulgence and selfishness, indignation and enmity will surely lead to such promising potential being dithered and dried up-- men and women becoming slaves to substance, thoughtlessness, vanity and uncivilized impulse. Evil and falsehood is easily magnified and self-justified in our time.
