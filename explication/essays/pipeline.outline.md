## Shared conventions

- **Voice.** Joseph's, on his best days — "elevated but not academic, precise but not jargon-heavy" (his own tone note, Feb 2026 outline). Each essay standalone; a reader who meets only one comes away with something complete.
- **Provenance discipline.** Joseph's verbatim prose is the primary and is carved, never retyped. Agent glosses in the evidence files (deep-pass commentary, dossier notes) are *locators only* — they do not enter essay prose. Where a source is an agent's rendering of Joseph's idea, the row says so.
- **Style substrate.** `scratch/from-vault-2017-2023.md` carries Joseph's own pre-agent guidelines (cognitive load; R.E.A.L.; register proportional to gravity; progressive disclosure vs. the deliberate withheld reveal — *"the 'lede'… isn't disclosed as 'the answer' until all of the preparatory work is done, because in this way the revealing of the knowledge has an emotional impact"*). These govern here over any default essay conventions.
- **src/ atoms.** One movement per file, slugged `mov-<slug>.md`; ordering lives in the outline, never in filenames. Frontmatter: `slug`, `essay`, `state` (proposed / gathered / drafted / joseph-voiced / settled), `voice` (joseph-primary / joseph-reworked / agent-drafted).
- **scratch/ and scratch/ref/** remain the quarry: the Feb 2026 dossiers (link-healed 2026-08-24, Joseph-voiced primaries pulled to `scratch/ref/primary-sources/`), the Emerson references, the vault extracts.
