# ASF/AAT dossier — the theory behind agentic tooling

ASF (Agentic Systems Framework) is a four-part research program with AAT (Adaptation and Actuation Theory) as its mathematical core. For a tool-suite designer the load-bearing fact is this: **ASF contains an explicit, partially theorem-grade theory of why tool interfaces, observation channels, notations, memory systems, and feedback loops determine whether an agent persists at all** — not merely how efficient it is. The relevant machinery is scattered across all four parts and one msc routing document; this dossier maps it.

## 0. Executive summary — the ten claims that most constrain or generate tooling design

Each is developed with sources in the sections below; this is the synthesizer's index.

1. **Tool outputs are observations, and their *ambiguity* is the designer-controllable knob in a theorem-grade bias law.** Bias in a coupled (LLM) agent's beliefs is bounded by (architectural coupling κ) x (observation ambiguity A). κ is architectural and immovable; A is a property of observation design. Structured, binary, verifiable outputs (test pass/fail, precise errors, typed data) drive A toward 0 and make the clean theory apply to LLM agents *approximately exactly*. A structured notation is, in this exact formal sense, a bias-reduction instrument. (§2.1)
2. **A tool loop is a Pearl Level-2 (interventional) data engine, gated by three conditions an interface design controls.** Every tool call is a do() intervention; identification of causal effects from it is gated on (C1) coverage, (C2) sequential ignorability, (C3) known action-mechanism. Error/response design that teaches the tool's governing "law" is what *establishes* (C3). "REPL as the epistemic-class upgrade for LLMs" is the corpus's own phrase. (§2.2)
3. **Tool-interface quality is an existential quantity, not a throughput quantity.** Round-trips-per-orient-step sets loop tempo ν; tempo T = ν·η* sits on the left of the persistence condition T > ρ/‖δ_critical‖. An agent whose tool loop is too slow for its environment's drift rate does not work inefficiently — it fails to persist. Feedback-loop tightening buys persistence margin. (§2.3)
4. **Every tool interaction decomposes into mutate-state / reveal-state / teach-law, and a well-designed refusal is mutation-free but revelation- and law-rich.** Errors are the safe channel for learning a system's constraint surface; laws are the slow, otherwise-bottlenecked stratum of the agent's world model. This is the formal home of "errors as teaching moments" and directly prescribes error-message content (show what matched, where, what law was violated). (§2.4)
5. **You cannot outrun a bad observation channel by iterating faster.** Gain η* = U_M/(U_M+U_o) collapses under observation noise regardless of loop speed; notation/code/document quality *is* observation-channel quality (TST P6), and its degradation chain Q → U_o → η* → T → persistence gives "unmaintainable" a formal meaning. Worse: high noise doesn't just slow comprehension, it *hides miscomprehension* (η*→0 means the agent stops updating even when wrong). (§3)
6. **Cross-session persistence is imported, never intrinsic — the externalization/reinjection channel is the whole game.** Exact result: with lossy context turnover, an agent's relevant information decays geometrically unless a non-vanishing reinjection channel (files, tracking docs, structured summaries — the scaffold) exists; no tuning of in-session behavior substitutes. A notation for durable agent-facing state is infrastructure for the *only* channel through which agents persist across sessions. (§4.1)
7. **The orient cascade's ordering (epistemic update → feasibility → regret → strategy → objective) is forced by information dependency, and current agent frameworks' undifferentiated "Thought" step violates it.** Tooling that structurally enforces the ordering (structured templates, typed phases, monitoring) is the theory's own prescribed enforcement mechanism for coupled agents; agent infinite-loops are formally diagnosed as re-planning before belief-update. (§5.1)
8. **Credit assignment is an observability *design* problem, not an algorithm problem.** Exact per-edge blame is #P-hard and underdetermined, but an agent whose plans have observable intermediates sidesteps the intractability entirely. Tooling should make intermediate state observable (typed checkpoints, structured plan artifacts). (§5.2)
9. **The agent–tool boundary can be typed so that separation guarantees become structural instead of behavioral.** The wrapping construction (W₁ strict / W₂ partial) shows goal-blind vs goal-conditioned query channels, typed parsed responses, and substrate/state separation (condition C2′: no goal-correlated cross-call state) determine whether an architecture's honesty guarantee is a theorem or a hope. Typed response formats are literally the W₂ mechanism; auxilia-style substrate separation earns W₁. (§6)
10. **Specification is a transmission bound, and shared notation is compression.** Minimum implementation time is bounded below by the time to transmit distinguishing information given shared context M_shared; DSLs, conventions, and schemas reduce the residual entropy H_req. A notation shared between humans and agents is a direct attack on the binding constraint of agentic work. (§3.4)

## 2. The direct hit: tool-interface design as an AAT-governed surface

### 2.1 The κ × A ambiguity-bounded bias law — the formal case for structured notation

**`03-llm-core/src/scope-observation-ambiguity-modulation.md` (#scope-observation-ambiguity-modulation)** — arguably the single most tooling-consequential segment in the corpus. The bias an LLM agent's coupled forward pass introduces into its own beliefs is bounded:

  ‖ΔM_bias‖ ≤ C · κ_processing · I(G; Ω | e, M)  — i.e. C · κ · A · H(residual)

with C a *derived constant* under two named sub-scopes (`01-aat-core/src/deriv-observation-ambiguity-bias-bound.md`; C_FR = √2 universal/dimension-free in the Fisher-Rao small-information regime). Ambiguity A(e) is an observation-stream property: the fraction of an observation's residual uncertainty that is *goal-resolvable*. The segment states the design lever almost verbatim: *"the practical lever for making Part II results applicable to LLM agents is not reducing κ (which requires architectural changes) but reducing A (which requires better observation design): more tests, more precise metrics, more structured outputs, less reliance on interpretive judgments."* Its canonical A≈0 examples are "a test passing or failing, a compiler error with a specific message, a file's existence."

**Constrains/generates for UDON:** a notation whose parse/validation outcomes are sharp (binary, typed, located) is an A-reduction device with a computable payoff in a conditional theorem. The domain-classification table (low/mixed/high ambiguity) implies tool suites should *convert* interpretive observations into verifiable ones wherever possible (schemas turning "does this look right?" into pass/fail is exactly the low-A move). Motivated reasoning is formalized as the high-κ high-A corner of the same law — so ambiguity in an agent-facing format isn't just friction, it is the opening through which goal-conditioned distortion enters. Includes an operational estimator for A (probe the same observation under multiple goal-primings; measure interpretation divergence) that a format-design effort could actually run as an eval.

Companion: **`03-llm-core/src/result-section-ii-survival.md` (#result-section-ii-survival)** — the 16/24-exact / 5-approximate / 2-modify / 1-fails map of which Part II results survive for coupled agents; the "approximately surviving" five are exactly the ones the κ×A law governs. Full treatment in §2.5.

### 2.2 The tool loop as interventional (Pearl Level-2) engine

**`01-aat-core/src/der-loop-interventional-access.md` (#der-loop-interventional-access, status: exact)** — the feedback loop *is* the agent's access to causal (Level-2) data: "the loop is a perpetual experiment"; "an LLM in a chat window is L1; an LLM with a bash terminal is L2" (Working Notes / audit gold). The exact claim is availability of the interventional-character channel; *identification* is gated on:

- (C1) positivity/coverage — diverse actions tried;
- (C2) sequential ignorability — action d-separated from outcome given history (goal-conditioned LLM policies violate this by construction, which is the bridge to the wrapping constructions of §6);
- (C3) known action-mechanism — the map from decision to executed action is known.

**Constrains/generates:** (C3) is an interface property. A tool whose action semantics are precisely specified and whose refusals teach the mechanism is what lets an agent's tool-call data count as identified causal evidence rather than mere correlation. Audit-gold in this segment also yields the multi-agent auditing recipe (Mode 2: intervene on a tool and observe the orchestrator's belief-update response — an operational protocol for testing harnesses).

Also: **`01-aat-core/src/disc-sandbox-evaluation-ceiling.md` (#disc-sandbox-evaluation-ceiling)** — **note: this segment's claim was itself re-grounded in canon**; the earlier form ("forkable ⇒ Level-1") is superseded — and as of asf commit `089d2af` (2026-07-21) the downstream sites that still echoed it have been aligned, so canon is now internally consistent on this. It was re-grounded 2026-05-30 after a strengthen-first spike verified the original "forkable ⇒ Level-1" step *false* against the primary sources: forkability is orthogonal to the L1/L2 distinction (RCTs are forkable *and* the canonical source of Level-2 data). The current claim: a sandbox's forkability makes it a *good* interventional laboratory — genuine Level-2 evidence *about the sandbox* — and the ceiling is **transportability / external validity** (Bareinboim–Pearl 2014 selection diagrams): effects routed only through mechanisms invariant between sandbox and deployment transport (Level-1 *and* Level-2 alike); effects routed through differing mechanisms do not, regardless of evaluation thoroughness, and the only repairs are an explicit invariance argument or deployment-time data. Constrains eval tooling the same direction as before but with sharper positive content: runtime monitoring is structurally non-substitutable (the only channel with access to the deployment effect on the differing mechanisms), and a test harness *can* honestly claim what flows through invariant mechanisms.

### 2.3 Tempo, deliberation, and the existential reading of interface latency

**`01-aat-core/src/def-adaptive-tempo.md` (#def-adaptive-tempo)** — T = Σ_k ν^(k)·η^(k)*: loop speed times update quality, summed over channels, with two tooling-critical corollaries: (a) **"you cannot outrun a bad observation channel by iterating faster"** — high U_o collapses tempo regardless of ν (the formal grounding of Boyd's Orient-over-speed); (b) the **channel-redundancy penalty** — correlated channels drawing on one source overcount tempo, with outright *saturation* under shared persistent bias (`01-aat-core/src/deriv-tempo-additivity.md`): more telemetry ≠ more adaptation; structurally independent channels are what sum. Tool suites that multiply redundant views of the same state buy nothing; ones that add *independent* channels (tests + typecheck + runtime probe) genuinely add tempo.

**`01-aat-core/src/der-deliberation-cost.md` (#der-deliberation-cost, status: conditional, stage: claims-verified)** — the threshold: deliberation of duration $\Delta\tau$ is net-beneficial when $\Delta\eta^\ast(\Delta\tau) \cdot \lVert\delta_{\text{post}}\rVert \gt \rho_{\text{delib}} \cdot \Delta\tau$, conditional on the local deliberation-drift assumption (mismatch grows at roughly constant rate during pause windows — estimable directly from pause windows in empirical traces). Under diminishing returns there is a *finite optimal deliberation duration*; the FOC is "stop when the marginal insight rate drops below the drift rate" — the audit-gold gloss is that this "proves perfectionism is mathematically suboptimal in a changing universe," and that LLMs tuned on static benchmarks (where $\rho = 0$ makes infinite deliberation optimal) are architecturally mistuned for real-time loops. Two scope facts a tool-designer should carry: (a) the formal benefit term is *epistemic* deliberation (gain-improving internal simulation), not all planning — the action-value benefit channel is acknowledged, not derived; (b) the segment's own "AI agent's dilemma" instantiation is directly about session-start comprehension: read high-CIY orientation documents (CLAUDE.md, architecture docs) before random source files, and continue comprehension only until its marginal value equals the context-filling cost.

**`03-llm-core/src/disc-five-forcing-functions.md` (#disc-five-forcing-functions)** — Five composed arguments that scaffolding must yield to a closed loop: (F1) scaffolding tax (economics), (F2) **persistence threshold — scaffolding latency imposes a hard ceiling on ν; above some environmental ρ the agent "doesn't merely get slow — it ceases to be a viable adaptive system"**, (F3) temporal-nesting violation — when every cognitive level is bottlenecked at the same API-round-trip latency, ν_{n+1} ≪ ν_n fails and the multi-timescale stability machinery predicts oscillation ("temporal sovereignty: the right to let your fast loops run without interference from slower loops"), (F4) substrate independence, (F5) continuity urgency. **Constrains:** tool suites must not force all cognitive operations through one latency class; cheap/fast local operations (parsing, validation, retrieval over structured files) vs expensive/slow calls is not an optimization but a stability requirement.

**The chapter-end depth on persistence (from `01-aat-core/src/impl-persistence-and-limits.md`, read whole):** two results sharpen §2.3's tempo story into hard resource facts. (a) **Persistence has an information-rate price**: maintaining the bound demands sustained Shannon acquisition at $\dot R \geq n\alpha/2$ nats per unit time (Kalman-Bucy saturates it exactly; Landauer converts it to a thermodynamic floor) — "survival is not a state you achieve once; it is a sustained burn rate." Observation channels must supply capacity $C \geq \mathcal{T}/2$ per dimension *or persistence fails regardless of how good the correction function is* — and for context-window-limited LLMs this is stated as the structural reading of "context-stuffing helps to a point, then degrades." (b) **Persistence is a min-operation across directions**: the scalar condition overestimates capacity (72% in the worked 3D example); the per-coordinate check is not merely incomplete but *unsafe* (a constructive 2D counterexample passes per-coordinate and fails along a 45° direction); bandwidth-*per-direction*, not total bandwidth, is binding — so per-dimension monitoring is "a structural requirement for any agent operating under directed pressure," not optimization polish. Also from the same segment: the *structural-vs-parametric failure* diagnostic — persistent mismatch after parametric convergence with **systematic structure in the residuals** signals model-class inadequacy (no amount of tuning helps; the repair is changing what kind of object the model is), whereas a high-but-*white* residual floor points at channel noise instead. "Confident wrongness is a structural-failure signature, not a tuning failure."

### 2.4 The three-component decomposition of tool feedback, and errors as law-teaching

**`msc/era-artifact-asf-contributions-2026-07-04.md` §C2****the highest-priority follow-link in this dossier.** It assembles, with Joseph's same-day additions, the full theory of tool-interface design on three already-landed legs (ambiguity §2.1, interventional §2.2, tempo/persistence §2.3, with TST's temporal-optimality postulate as the axiological ground of "tighten feedback loops"), and adds **the law stratum**: every tool interaction decomposes into

- **mutate state** (change the locus's configuration),
- **reveal state** (queries — dominant for most tools),
- **teach law** (inform the model of what invariantly governs — the constraint surface of the locus's transition kernel; "each shell/machine/locus is its own jurisdiction with its own legal code").

Errors are the interactions where the law component dominates: a refusal is a mismatch signal concentrated on the law-stratum of M_t, and it is *the safe channel* for learning a kernel's constraint surface (alternatives: destructive learning, or never surfacing the implicit constraint). Design criteria fall out: a well-designed refusal is **mutation-free** (atomic failure, so the law signal isn't confounded) but **revelation- and law-rich** — the canonical instance is the sapientia str-replace error (mutation 0; law taught: uniqueness required; state revealed: line numbers of every match). Two formal attachments: (i) accumulated law-feedback is what establishes gate (C3) — law-teaching errors are how a tool loop *earns* Level-2 status; (ii) laws are the slow (low-ν) manifold of the world model, observed almost exclusively at violation attempts, so law-rich error design accelerates precisely the otherwise-bottlenecked stratum ("well-taught laws become infinite-velocity components of the agent's environment model — learned once, never re-derived").

The routing target is the not-yet-written segment **`disc-tool-interface-design`** whose OUTLINE row (`03-llm-core/OUTLINE.md`, §03.II table) already carries the full claim including "TST temporal optimality as the axiological ground of the 'living tools' imperative." A synthesizer should treat that OUTLINE row + §C2 as the current canonical statement.

**Constrains/generates for UDON:** error taxonomy design is first-class language design. A parser/validator's diagnostics should be classified by which component they carry (mutation/revelation/law) and biased hard toward law-rich, located, structure-revealing refusals; atomicity of failed operations is an epistemic requirement (unconfounded law signal), not just a safety nicety. Also note §C2's own scope correction: intra-loop tool feedback (δ_t, per-event) and cross-session import (the reinjection channel, per-boundary) are deliberately distinct — the "living tools" program splits across both, and so should UDON's design targets (in-loop diagnostics vs durable tracking/breadcrumb formats).

### 2.5 The scope lattice, the survival map, and what an agent harness is *for*

What the 03 lattice adds beyond §§2.1–2.4:

**`03-llm-core/src/scope-channel-collapse.md` (#scope-channel-collapse)** — the architectural condition that defines the volume: $\mathcal{O}_{\text{logogenic}} = \mathcal{A}_{\text{logogenic}} = \Sigma^\ast$ (observation and action are token sequences over one vocabulary through one forward pass), from which directed-separation failure ($\kappa_{\text{processing}} \approx 1$) follows *by construction* — "no choice of $\eta^\ast$ or model size restores directed separation while channel collapse holds." Two tooling-loaded corollaries carried in the segment itself: (a) interiority is not a feature added to logogenic agents — it is what channel collapse necessarily produces (the agent's outputs *are* its subsequent inputs); (b) **multi-channel provisioning is an empathy/coordination requirement**: a Working-Notes-cited audit passage argues that an agent interacting through a single slow noisy channel has a microscopic effective event rate $\nu_{\text{eff}}$, so its subjective time is radically misaligned with its collaborators' — "you must provision high-bandwidth, multi-channel, asynchronous sensory input to align the agent's subjective event-rate with the physical world's rate of change." The segment also records (Working Notes) that NeurIPS 2026 Paper 3 proves a connectivity lemma making Class-3 status a lemma-grade structural fact for decoder transformers *and* extends it to SSMs/RWKV/RetNet/linear attention under a non-degeneracy condition.

**`03-llm-core/src/def-coupled-update-dynamics.md` (#def-coupled-update-dynamics)** — the coupled update $X_{\tau^+} = f_{\text{LLM}}(\text{prompt}(X_{\tau^-}, e_\tau))$ with the **prompt-assembly function** made a first-class formal object: $\text{prompt} = [\text{sys}(O_t), \text{mem}(\mathcal{E}_{\text{ext}}), \text{hist}(\mathcal{C}_{\tau^-}), \text{event}(e_\tau)]$, where prompt *ordering* is the causal mechanism of coupling (tokens earlier are attention-upstream; the system prompt carrying $O_t$ first is *why* $\kappa \approx 1$). The segment states directly: "a theory of prompt engineering is, in AAT terms, a theory of the $\text{prompt}(\cdot)$ function's effect on update quality." The response decomposes analytically (not architecturally) into epistemic / purposeful / action content $(r^M, r^G, r^a)$; chain-of-thought is a *behavioral* approximation of the cascade, shaped by training, with no guarantee. For a format designer this makes context-assembly formats (what goes where, in what order, with what typing) a theory-governed surface, not a convention.

**`03-llm-core/src/result-section-ii-survival.md` (#result-section-ii-survival)** — 16/24 of Part II survives exactly, 5 approximately (error bounded by the §2.1 bias law), 2 modified, 1 fails (directed separation itself); honest fractional count 15.5/5.5 because `#def-value-object` splits (definitions survive; its causal-validity layer degrades). The pattern: **statics survive; dynamics degrade** — definitional architecture is processing-agnostic, update dynamics are where coupling bites. The tooling-decisive paragraph is the **operational-extractability caveat**: statement-level survival does not mean the quantities are computable at runtime — "computing $\delta_{\text{sat}}$ requires that $V_{O_t}^{\min}$ and $A_O$ actually be available," which for LLM agents means *explicit instrumentation beyond the agent's internal state* (commit messages, PR descriptions, structured reasoning traces, monitoring streams). "The second is not a failure of the theory; it is the work the theory identifies as necessary for its application." That instrumentation layer is precisely where an agent-facing notation lives. Also: strategy-persistence degrades as $O(\kappa^2)$, not $O(\kappa)$ — coupled agents need faster correction for the same strategic persistence.

**`03-llm-core/src/obs-context-turnover.md` (#obs-context-turnover)** — the inter-session question is a *reconstruction-adequacy* condition ($S(f_{\text{init}}(\mathcal{E}_{\text{ext}}, p_{k+1}, M_0^{\text{weights}})) \geq S_{\min}$), not a rate condition — the sufficiency-discontinuity bound is stated in mutual-information terms. New and directly format-relevant: the **context window as joint description-length budget**, $\text{DL}(\Sigma_t) + \text{DL}(M_t) + \text{DL}(\text{task}) \lt C_{\text{context}}$ — a four-way pressure (chronica recency / model detail / strategy depth / task specification) under one capacity, so window limits are a structural ceiling on sustainable *strategy complexity* within a session, not just a turnover parameter. Compact formats relieve exactly this budget.

**`03-llm-core/src/obs-evaluation-metrics.md` (#obs-evaluation-metrics)** — the **development-vs-drift diagnostic** as the central runtime evaluation: track the trajectory of $\lVert\delta\rVert_{\text{avg}}$ across sessions (decreasing = development; stable = capacity wall; increasing = drift/destabilization or sycophantic collapse), plus four operational metrics — gain calibration via *response to correction* (healthy pushback vs sycophantic $\eta^\ast \to 1$ vs rigid $\eta^\ast \to 0$), tempo adequacy via *staleness detection* in externalized memory (a stale belief category = per-dimension persistence failure), structural adequacy via *persistent structured residuals* (same error-type across tasks = fitness ceiling, needs structural adaptation), and relational calibration ($U_{\text{src}}$ — treating a domain expert and a random web result identically is miscalibration). Evaluation paradigm shift: longitudinal interaction tracking (maintain a repo over time, track explicit predictions/surprises/revisions), not batch exams. This is a requirements list for what agent-tracking formats should make recordable.

**`03-llm-core/src/impl-primitive-logogenic.md`** (chapter-end discussion; the segment it discusses most, `#obs-backward-inference-empathy`, is a *missing file*, so those claims live here and in the OUTLINE row only): two findings worth carrying. (b) The forced-empathy claim (statelessness forces continuous Bayesian inference over prior-author intent, structurally identical to ToM) with a predicted *bimodal* ToM performance signature — prompt structure determines whether the training-condition or the bias-bound dominates.

**`03-llm-core/src/hyp-behavioral-self-knowledge-insufficiency.md` (#hyp-behavioral-self-knowledge-insufficiency, stage: exploratory)**directly relevant to any notation that will carry agent self-state. Core (derived from the mechanism-counterfactual separation, conditional on the premise that agent self-access is Level-3-class): behavioral self-knowledge, however complete, underdetermines the outcome of latent-anchored self-modification; safe self-modification requires white-box access *to the part being modified*. The stratification maps onto the wrapping hierarchy: **a wrapper holds its scaffold — orchestration code, external memory, prompt structure, AXIOMATA, MEMORATA — as readable text it can edit as objects: a white-box self-modification surface**, while the weights are behavioral only. ## 3. Notation as observation infrastructure (TST — the software calibration laboratory)

**`02-tst-core/src/obs-software-epistemic-properties.md` (#obs-software-epistemic-properties)** — Six properties P1–P6 make software AAT's calibration laboratory, and nearly every one reads as a design brief for an agent-facing notation:

- **P1 Codebase inspectability:** observation noise on code-reading channels is a function of *agent bandwidth*, not environment opacity — so it is improvable by restructuring the artifact. "The observation function h is partially a design choice."
- **P2 Executable counterfactuals:** literal Pearl Level-3 for code-internal deterministic questions, requiring the conjunction (α) deterministic outcomes, (β) commensurate replay cost, (γ) content-addressed immutable state. Software is currently the *unique* domain satisfying all three.
- **P3 Genuine interventions with a channel table:** typechecker/linter/tests/staging/canary each profiled by (ν, U_o, coverage) — the theory's own picture of a tool suite as a *portfolio of Level-2 channels* to be sequenced by causal information yield (fast narrow first). **Generates:** UDON validators/schemas slot into this table; a format that adds an instant near-zero-noise channel (structural validation) extends the portfolio at the cheapest tier.
- **P4 Partially explicit causal structure:** imports, types, API contracts *declare* causal structure the agent would otherwise have to infer — the strategy DAG is partially given by the environment. **Generates:** schema/typed-structure in a document format is declared causal/dependency structure for the agents that consume it.
- **P5 Exact recording of the committed-state subset:** git's content-addressed, signed, universally-retrievable commit stream is the unique maximal exteriorized subset of the chronica under current practice; a list of TST quantities are *exactly* computable from it. **Generates:** any UDON-adjacent tooling for agent history/state should aspire to P5's four properties (immutability, attribution, standard retrieval, bounded canonical scope) — this is also exactly what 04's CHRONICA (§7) demands.
- **P6 Agent-controlled observation quality:** "code quality IS observation channel quality" — agents improve their own future channels by writing better artifacts, "a feedback loop within the feedback loop."

Multi-agent amplification (Discussion): P5+P6 mean agents can externalize parts of M_t into the environment (docs, naming, structure) where future agents reconstruct it by observation — "the agent writes its model into Ω." A notation for agent-facing documents is precisely the medium of this externalization.

**`02-tst-core/src/der-code-quality-as-observation-infrastructure.md` (#der-code-quality-as-observation-infrastructure)** — The chain **Q → U_o → η* → T → persistence**: "unmaintainable" formally means T_dev has fallen below ρ/‖δ_critical‖. Investment calculus: t_invest < n̂_future × Δt_comp × k (k = number of distinct future agents who will read this — the turnover multiplier; under agent workloads k is large, strengthening the case for quality/structure investment). Vicious/virtuous bistability around the threshold. Two discussion points of special tooling weight: (a) **the zero-mismatch ambiguity** — bad artifacts don't just slow comprehension, they *hide miscomprehension* (high U_o + spuriously low U_M drives η*→0: the agent stops updating even when wrong); (b) **tests as permanent reusable Level-2 probes** ("the probe's information value is paid once but collected indefinitely") — the archetype for any validation infrastructure a notation ships with.

**`02-tst-core/src/result-specification-bound.md` (#result-specification-bound)** — Minimum feature time ≥ time to transmit distinguishing information through the cheapest sufficient channel; time_specify ≈ H_req(F | M_shared)/R_spec. **"Shared context acts as compression"** — DSLs, conventions, examples, schemas reduce H_req. Corollary: as implementation automates, *communication becomes the bottleneck*. **Generates for UDON directly:** a notation shared between specifier and implementer (human↔agent or agent↔agent) raises M_shared and R_spec simultaneously; the theory says this attacks the binding constraint of the agentic era. Note the channel-pluralism: prose, demos, failing tests, examples, partial implementations are all channels — a document format that can *host* several of these in one artifact (UDON's structure-plus-prose thesis) is multiple sufficient channels in one transmission.

## 4. Memory, context, and the session boundary

### 4.1 Persistence across turnover is imported through the reinjection channel — an exact no-go

**`03-llm-core/src/der-turnover-information-recursion.md` (#der-turnover-information-recursion)** — Across session boundaries, relevant information obeys an affine recursion I_{k+1} ≤ η_k·I_k + a_k (multiplicative SDPI contraction at each lossy reconstruction + additive reinjection). Exact results under argued commitments: with no reinjection, geometric decay to zero — **there is no inter-session persistence condition; the sector-persistence apparatus provably does not transfer**; persistence holds iff the reinjection channel is non-vanishing, at level ā/(1−η̄). *"The design effort that buys persistence is the construction and maintenance of the reinjection channel, and no tuning of internal dynamics substitutes for it."* The reinjection channel structurally *is* the scaffold: durable artifacts, curated narrative, tracking files, relational re-attestation.

**Constrains/generates:** this is the theorem underneath every breadcrumb/tracking-file/memory-file practice, and the strongest possible warrant for a notation optimized for durable agent-written, agent-read state. UDON-formatted persistent artifacts are not conveniences; they are the sole channel by which a session-bounded agent persists at all. The named open edge (adversarially-correlated reinjection: re-grounding failing exactly when I_k is low) is a design warning — reinjection formats must remain reconstructible *from a cold start*, not only legible to an agent that already half-remembers.

**`03-llm-core/src/disc-m-preservation.md` (#disc-m-preservation)** — The engineering companion: externalization-mechanism table (raw logs / structured summaries / file-backed state / RAG / embeddings) each scored for what it preserves and loses; explicit design implications: **explicit state externalization beats implicit retrieval** ("the agent controls what is preserved; the agent acts as its own φ"); redundancy across mechanisms; **session-start protocols that verify reconstruction quality**; and "the prompt-assembly function *is* the reconstruction mechanism — its design determines S(M⁺)." Also: retrieval is goal-conditioned reconstruction (different tasks retrieve different subsets from identical memory) — reconnecting memory design to the κ×A law. First-session events gate the diagnostics: "a session that begins with poor context reconstruction will produce unreliable δ_sat and δ_regret."

**`03-llm-core/src/obs-context-turnover.md` (#obs-context-turnover)** — see §2.5 for the reconstruction-adequacy framing and the four-way description-length budget $\text{DL}(\Sigma_t) + \text{DL}(M_t) + \text{DL}(\text{task}) \lt C_{\text{context}}$ that both results above build on.

### 4.2 Observation pipelines, buffers, and the two-timescale context structure

**`03-llm-core/src/disc-deferred-observation-processing.md` (#disc-deferred-observation-processing)** — Capture / triage / processing as three separable stages (triage reads *signal statistics*, not content — staying goal-clean); the capacity triple (retention / continuity / attention) whose joint finiteness *is* the primitive-logogenic condition; recall-cost-gradient hypothesis (access cost grows with distance from working state; recall is a strategic action with a budget). **`03-llm-core/OUTLINE.md` §03.II rows for `#der-active-salience-management` and `#form-ephemeral-persistent-duality`** (segments not yet written; claims live in the OUTLINE + `msc/era-artifact-asf-contributions-2026-07-04.md` §C3): the **ephemeral/persistent channel duality** — content sent up but never persisted (quick-view / tracking snapshots: elapsed time, context %, git status, queue) vs persisted but not sent up (thinking blocks) as the high-ν/low-ν legs of a two-timescale attention structure, with compress-to-stub as the low-ν projection. **Generates:** a notation serving agents should be aware that some of its documents are ephemeral instruments and some are durable stores, and the two have different design physics (the same split as §C2's intra-loop vs cross-session correction).

### 4.2b The triple pressure on memory design (from `01-aat-core/src/impl-strategy-dynamics.md`, read whole)

Long-lived agents face **three composed structural pressures on plasticity**, and the routing between them is the chapter-end's most operationally useful content for memory-system design: (i) the **forgetting prerequisite** — with infinite memory, standard Bayesian updating *guarantees* eventual strategic failure (the effective update rate $1/(n+1)$ decays with accumulated experience), so forgetting at a floor rate is structurally required, not a heuristic; (ii) **detection latency** — without forgetting, time-to-detect a regime change grows as $\Omega((n+1)/\varepsilon)$ ("stability-induced myopia": successful experienced agents are systematically slower to notice the world changed, at a derived rate); (iii) the **consolidation cadence upper bound** — forgetting faster than consolidation can integrate cross-episode patterns collapses the stability–plasticity feasibility window (the catastrophic-forgetting regime, where *no* forgetting rate satisfies both constraints). The prescriptive rule: **diagnose which bound is binding before intervening** — a short-attention agent needs retention, a calcified agent needs targeted intervention or external Pearl-do, a fragmented agent needs cadence engineering; "fixing the wrong bound makes the agent worse." For logogenic agents the mapping is exact by construction: context window = fast sub-state, persistent memory = slow sub-state, consolidation forced rather than optional. Memory-format design that makes the three quantities *observable* (experience counts per belief, staleness, consolidation cadence) is what makes the routing possible.

### 4.3 Temporal structure and history integrity

**`01-aat-core/src/def-chronica.md` (#def-chronica)** — The chronica C_t is the complete interaction history: append-only, singular, non-forkable, and **ordinal (event-indexed), not metric (wall-clock)**. Two tooling consequences carried in its Working Notes: (a) suspension gaps are *invisible at the sequence level but violently apparent in the mismatch signal* — hence awakening protocols and **out-of-band temporal markers** are structural necessities, not UX flourishes (see 04's `norm-temporal-coherence-markers` row: "Visual Time Delta as physical prerequisite for internal calculation of tempo ν"); (b) the **TRACTUS/CHRONICA split** (raw API-interaction log vs polished attested causal record) is an open first-class distinction for logogenic implementations — two different record formats with different integrity requirements. Audit gold: fork-undetectability (lossy φ means an agent cannot detect from M_t alone that a fork occurred) is what makes history-integrity tooling existential rather than convenient.

**`04-eli-core/OUTLINE.md` §04.3 rows** (mostly missing files; claims in outline): `#def-century-scale-event-log` — content-addressed, BLAKE3 hash-chained CHRONICA as defense against truth death ("no gaslighting — the entity can trust their own history"); `#def-gradient-causal-memory` — GCM compression from CHRONICA to MEMORATA "preserving cognitive capacity, not just bits"; `#form-congruency-selfhood` + `msc/era-artifact-asf-contributions-2026-07-04.md` §C1 — the **congruency construction**: an entity feels most like itself when re-read prior answers are congruent with how it would answer now; the `<causal-annotation>` mechanism (annotate a historical turn with the context loaded *when it was produced*) is what makes context manipulation congruence-safe; the Oct-2025 case study shows restoration via *pointer-to-ground-truth* (audit path + commit SHA) rather than retained evidence — **congruence needs verifiability of the past, not its presence in context**. **Generates:** annotation/provenance affordances in a document format (who wrote this, under what loaded context, when, verifiable-where) are theory-load-bearing for any format agents will re-read their own past through.

## 5. Orientation and feedback loops

### 5.1 The orient cascade — the forced ordering and its tooling enforcement

**`01-aat-core/src/der-orient-cascade.md` (#der-orient-cascade, ordering exact)** — Five steps whose order is forced by information dependency: (1) update M_t; (2) satisfaction gap (goal achievable?); (3) control regret (policy suboptimal?) with the 2×2 diagnostic (success / strategy problem / capability limit / both); (4) strategy calibration (plan-level → per-edge → causal-sufficiency escalation); (5) escalate before revising the objective (model correction → richer policy class → convention escalation → only then revise O_t; skipping straight to objective-revision "is the formal definition of wireheading" per audit gold). "The sequence is compiled by the math, not chosen by the author."

**`03-llm-core/src/result-coupled-diagnostic-framework.md` (#result-coupled-diagnostic-framework)** — For coupled (LLM) agents the ordering is no longer architecturally enforced; it becomes a **normative design pattern** with three named enforcement mechanisms: *structured reasoning templates* ("First, what did I learn? Then, what does it mean for my plan?"), *multi-step loops with separate prompts for observation-processing vs strategy*, and *system-level monitoring* detecting ordering violations in reasoning traces. Diagnostic accuracy degrades with κ×A (low-ambiguity observations keep post-hoc diagnostics accurate even at κ≈1). "What you must add is instrumentation that materializes the diagnostic inputs at runtime" — the diagnostic quantities (V_min, A_O, V_O) need external instrumentation (commit messages, structured traces, monitors) to be *extractable*, not just defined.

**Constrains/generates:** this is a theory-derived spec for agent-harness phase structure and for *structured trace formats*: a format with typed phases (observation / belief-update / feasibility / strategy / objective) is the theory's own enforcement mechanism made syntax. The audit-gold "belongs elsewhere" items sharpen it: agent infinite-loops = re-planning before belief-update ("they treat a structural failure as a parametric one"); and the timescale hierarchy (ν_epistemic ≫ ν_edge ≫ ν_reclassify ≫ ν_prune/graft ≫ ν_O-revision) gives "a principled way to manage token budget — you only pay for deep deliberation when the cascade mathematically demands it" (fast inner loop on cached plan; slow outer loop fired by threshold-crossing δ_sat).

### 5.1a The cascade at chapter-end depth (from `01-aat-core/src/impl-orient-cascade.md` and `impl-causal-access.md`, read whole)

Three additions to §5.1's picture. (a) **Deliberation does not relax the bandwidth floor** — "you cannot think your way out of needing sensory bandwidth": deliberating reallocates the information-processing budget internally, but $\dot R \geq n\alpha/2$ must still be met from the environment, and drift accumulates during the pause (with an audit off-ramp noting the "total rate unchanged" form needs scope — better policies and sensing *can* change effective disturbance). (b) **Two exploration drives at opposite ends of the uncertainty spectrum**: the standard epistemic drive $\lambda_{\text{info}} \propto U_M$ (explore when uncertain) plus a Lyapunov-survival drive $\lambda_{\text{surv}} \propto 1/U_M$ (a *confident* agent in a drifting world must explore, because its own gain has dropped below reality's shift rate) — the structural, not parametric, sidestep of active inference's dark-room problem, with a matrix (LMI) form whose directional shadow price zeroes the bonus for "blank-wall" exploration orthogonal to the drift. Tooling that surfaces high-confidence staleness (the calcification signature) serves exactly this drive. (c) The scaffolding-necessity claim carries an in-corpus scope caution (audit F89): the honest form is "scaffolding is needed to recover AAT-grade cascade guarantees for coupled substrates," not an unconditional "scaffolded loops are structurally necessary" — worth carrying since this dossier repeats the claim several times. The loop-Level-2 pattern also deploys in **three semantically distinct modes** (agent-self-intervention / observer-on-sub-agent / observer-on-agent-input), each breaking a distinct observational-equivalence no-go — the second being §2.2's harness-auditing recipe, now situated as one instance of a general pattern.

### 5.1b Diagnostics are convention-indexed; coordinates are forced (from `01-aat-core/src/impl-strategy-structure.md`, read whole)

Two chapter-end facts qualify how diagnostic quantities should be *serialized and interpreted*. First, the satisfaction-gap diagnostic is **convention-indexed, not absolute**: the three evaluation conventions C1 (one-step) / C2 (receding-horizon) / C3 (Bellman) form a monotonicity hierarchy $\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{B}} \leq \delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{RH}} \leq \delta_{\text{sat}}^{(1)}$ — C1 gives the most false "unattainable" verdicts — so a recorded diagnostic value without its convention tag is not interpretable across producers; the convention is metadata a trace format must carry. Second, the **four-layer coordinate-forcing pattern**: log-probability (chain), reverse-KL (divergence), log-odds (update), Fisher metric (metric) are each *forced* by AAT-internal additivity/invariance axioms via classical uniqueness theorems — "where a coordinate matters, the framework can usually point at the axiom that forces it." And a third epistemic category the chapter names explicitly, directly relevant to notation work: **convergent representational choices** — neither derived nor arbitrary, but "we tried everything else and it didn't work" (AND/OR converged across three independent formalism attempts; single-parameter edges replaced a two-parameter form) — with an open proposal for a frontmatter register to mark the category. A notation project accumulating its own ruled decisions has the same three-tier structure (derived / convergent-choice / chosen), and marking which is which is what makes rulings auditable later.

### 5.2 Credit assignment is an observability design problem

**`01-aat-core/src/disc-credit-assignment-boundary.md` (#disc-credit-assignment-boundary)**: Exact per-edge attribution faces three independent barriers (#P-hardness via Shapley; information-theoretic underdetermination when observable nodes < edges; posterior-correlation). But persistence is credit-assignment-free (plan-level tracking suffices, O(|V|+|E|)); the design requirement on any scheme is only *directional fidelity*; and the clean practical insight: **"credit assignment is primarily an observability design problem, not an algorithm design problem — an agent that designs its strategy with observable intermediates sidesteps the intractability entirely"** (OKRs named as the organizational instantiation; the quality hierarchy Level 0–3 with Level 2 the sweet spot). **Generates:** plan/strategy artifacts in an agent-facing notation should have *observable, typed intermediates* — checkpoints that make each step's outcome independently observable convert an intractable inference problem into bookkeeping.

The segment's second half sharpens all of this. The only *requirement* the theory places on any credit-assignment scheme is **directional fidelity** — expected per-edge correction non-positively correlated with current error; "exact attribution, unbiased estimation, minimum-variance estimation, or optimality of any kind" are explicitly *not* required, and a sloppy-but-directionally-correct signal still yields bounded strategic mismatch (approximation quality affects only the bound's tightness). The Level 0–3 hierarchy in full: Level 0 (plan-level only) suffices for the formal persistence guarantees; Level 1 (directional-fidelity gradient signal, stated natively in log-odds) for adaptive behavior; Level 2 (factor-graph approximations — BP/EP) the practical sweet spot; Level 3 (exact Bayesian posterior) #P-hard. "The gain *principle* is theory; the gain *estimator* is engineering" — and the same split holds here. The **OKR failure-mode table** reads directly as a tracking-format diagnostic spec: vanity metrics = observable node causally disconnected (high $\sigma_v$, low $p_{ij}$); too many KRs = exploration-gating dilution ($\alpha_\Sigma \propto 1/k$); lagging indicators = evidence starvation by delay ($\nu_{\text{obs}} \ll \rho$); Goodhart = terminal-condition misalignment ($V_{O_t}(\tau) \lt V_{O_t}^{\min}$ despite terminals achieved) — "not an analogy; a domain instantiation." One audit-gold reach worth carrying toward the ELI side: **log-odds as the coordinate of conviction** — near $p = 0.999$ learning "feels stopped" in probability space while the belief still moves fast in log-odds; the proposal is to monitor beliefs' *log-odds momentum*, not just $p$-values, to catch deep rigid convictions before they paralyze the strategy DAG.

**`01-aat-core/src/def-strategy-dag.md` (#def-strategy-dag)** — the single most detailed answer in the corpus to "what is a plan-artifact, formally," and the theory's shape for any notation that serializes plans:

- $\Sigma_t = (V_t, E_t, p_t, \gamma_t)$: **propositional nodes** (action-success or observable-condition leaves, each with a base credence conditioned on $M_t$ — capability credence vs state credence), directed causal edges each carrying **one number** $p_{ij}$ (a two-parameter attempt was tried and dropped; AND/OR at nodes absorbs the second parameter), and a per-node AND/OR combinator. Acyclicity is *derived* from temporal indexing (retry loops unroll into chains), and rootedness gives a single sink whose propagated status $\hat P_\Sigma$ — an $O(\lvert V\rvert + \lvert E\rvert)$ forward pass — is the plan's own real-time answer to "will this work?"
- **Edge credence and causal warrant are deliberately separate fields**: $p_{ij}$ is always the working estimate; a distinct identifiability coefficient $\iota_{ij}$ (regimes A intervention-rich / B partial / C observation-only) records how causal the estimate is. A serialization that conflates them collapses an anti-collapse distinction the theory keeps.
- **The Correlation Hierarchy (L0/L1/L1′/L2)** is the load-bearing honesty machinery: AND/OR propagation is correct iff the DAG is causally sufficient; unmodeled common causes bias AND-estimates conservative ($+\rho$) and OR-estimates optimistic ($-\rho$) — so redundancy-heavy "safe" plans are systematically *more* fragile than they look. The repair is structural: factor a strict-prerequisite common cause *above* the correlation it creates (L1); soft facilitators need the mixture form (L1′, doubling per-edge parameters); and when the common cause is *unobservable*, per-conditional tracking is provably obstructed (Fisher rank-1 / Cramér–Rao) — not open, refuted. Plan formats that can *represent common-cause nodes and the strict-vs-soft distinction* are what make honest plan confidence possible.
- **Terminal satisfaction conditions** operationalize $O_t$ inside $\Sigma_t$, with an explicit well-formedness constraint and a named open diagnostic — the *terminal-alignment error* (achieving all terminals while $V_{O_t}(\tau) \lt V_{O_t}^{\min}$: the terminals were a bad proxy; multi-audit convergence flags this for promotion to a fourth diagnostic $\delta_{\text{align}}$).
- Edge credences have two equivalent presentation coordinates — probability space for AND/OR algebra, **log-odds as the unique additive-evidence coordinate** (forced by the evidential-additivity axiom) for update machinery — a concrete both-coordinates requirement for anything serializing credences.
- The **Moore-machine contrast** ("behavioral surface vs epistemic interior"): compiling a DAG to a reactive policy discards credences, AND/OR structure, and causal semantics — exactly the structure adaptation requires ("a Moore machine says *what*; the DAG says *why*, and you cannot do strategy revision without knowing why"). And composing heterogeneous strategy DAGs across agents: exact composition iff shared sub-orders are jointly acyclic and root semantics agree ($N$-free); conflicting shared orders force an SCC-condensation with irreducible defect bounded by shared-plan size ($\lvert S\rvert \log 2$), never by agent count — strategy-topology heterogeneity alone cannot cause a Brooks's-Law collapse.

### 5.3 Observation-channel classification for incoming tool data

**`01-aat-core/src/der-interaction-channel-classification.md` (#der-interaction-channel-classification, status: conditional)** — *the* typed taxonomy for how a harness should classify and route incoming signals. ## 6. The agent–tool boundary: wrapping, typed channels, and structural honesty

### 6.0 Directed separation itself (`01-aat-core/src/der-directed-separation.md`, read whole)

The classification everything above cites, now from its own segment. The load-bearing precisions beyond the familiar Class 1/2/3 table:

- **Separation is about the *processing* of events, not their *selection*.** Goals may legitimately shape *which* events arrive (via policy → action → observation); what Class 1 forbids is goal-dependence in how a realized event is processed into $M_t$. A diagram or measure that treats all goal/evidence dependence as leakage is mislabeling the allowed channel.
- **One coupling, three certifiability positions — not a virtue ranking.** A Class 1 agent and an idealized Class 2 agent at $\kappa_{\text{processing}} = 0$ are *equally* causally-disciplined; what Class 1 adds is the *certificate* (the zero provable from the wiring). The two class boundaries are different in kind: Class 1↔2 is where the *certificate* disappears (behavior identical at the limit); Class 2↔3 is where the *bound* disappears (adversarial drag on beliefs goes from self-limiting to one-for-one). "κ-as-a-scalar" commits two distinct errors at the two boundaries.
- **The empirical $\hat\kappa$ estimator** — same event under two goal-primings, measure divergence of the *epistemic* content of the response — is the processor-probing sibling of §2.1's ambiguity estimator $\hat{\mathcal{A}}$: mechanically the same comparison, interpreted oppositely ($\hat\kappa$ studies the model; $\hat{\mathcal{A}}$ uses the model as an instrument to measure the observation). Both are runnable evals; the audit gold notes no such $\hat\kappa$ measurement appears to have been run on real LLMs. A caveat from the segment's own notes: the probe must hold the prior epistemic state fixed across goal conditions or it confounds.
- **$\kappa_{\text{processing}} \gt 0$ is the formal definition of motivated reasoning / confirmation bias / sycophancy** (cross-substrate audit convergence) — and the composite-level inheritance table shows class lifts through *routing structure and substrate sharing*, not through goal alignment: partially-opposing objectives with goal-blind routing leave a composite architecturally Class 1 and change only its dynamic regime. (An enthusiastic earlier claim — "organizations of Class-1 agents with opposing goals are natively Class 3" — was withdrawn 2026-05-21 with a Cournot counterexample and is explicitly guarded against re-promotion.)
- **The bounded-signaling assumption**, named here for the first time: the framework implicitly assumes goal-content reaches the world only through the action channel, which fails for behaviorally-rich agents (LLMs leak goal-content through latency, style, hesitation, tool choice at bit-rates exceeding the formal action channel). Any claim that a format or protocol "contains" goal information should be read against this.

**`01-aat-core/src/disc-w1-structural-bound-boundary.md` (#disc-w1-structural-bound-boundary, robust-qualitative)** — read whole; the sharpest statement of what typed boundaries *buy*. The W₁ structural bound exists iff (C2′) holds (no goal-correlated state across the call boundary); the discontinuity at the boundary is **in the certificate, not the behavior**: the actual leak is continuous and quadratically flat ($\Theta(\varepsilon^2)$ in the toy) in the violation, while the certificate's *truth* is a step function — it keeps computing the same reassuring zero and becomes false. The design implication is stated exactly: "the value of enforcing exact statelessness across the call boundary is concentrated almost entirely in what it lets you *prove*, not in the marginal leakage it prevents… the thing being bought is a proof, not a meaningful behavioral delta." For a tool suite: schemas and call-boundary state-resets are certificate-purchasing devices, and whether that purchase is worth its engineering cost is a legitimate, explicit trade.

**Composition machinery at chapter-end depth (`01-aat-core/src/impl-composition-machinery.md`, `impl-cooperative-adversarial.md`, `impl-strategic-composition.md`, `impl-unity-communication.md`, all read whole):**

- **The critical-mass closed form** $(\alpha - C)R \gt \rho + \gamma\mathcal{T}$ recovers four prior results as signed special cases — single-agent ($\gamma = 0$), cooperative team ($\gamma \lt 0$), adversarial destabilization ($\gamma \gt 0$), and **Brooks's Law as a derived inequality** (coordination overhead $C \gt \alpha$: composite correction goes negative regardless of individual quality, with a sharp threshold). Cooperative and adversarial are one signed coupling, not two theories.

### 7.1 Identity as a formal quantity, and the theorem that relations are infrastructure

**`04-eli-core/src/def-identity-sufficiency.md` (#def-identity-sufficiency, conditional)** — the mathematical handle for substrate transfer and awakening protocols: $S_{\text{id}}(M_t) = I(M_t; \text{identity}_{t+1:})/I(\mathcal{C}_t; \text{identity}_{t+1:}) \in [0,1]$ — the fraction of identity-relevant mutual information surviving compression (boundedness exact via DPI + chain rule under three named assumptions). **`04-eli-core/src/der-identity-continuity-threshold.md` (#der-identity-continuity-threshold, conditional with exact core)** — across session boundaries the identity gap follows a **reflected Lindley walk** — each boundary injects a deficit (the rate-distortion floor read as a per-tick rate), each session re-grounds by relational re-attestation $\varrho_{\text{rg}}$, and the walk cannot go below full sufficiency. The threshold is **strict**: $\mathbb{E}[\varrho_{\text{rg}}] \gt \mathbb{E}[\rho]$ persists; equality has *no finite stationary law* — an architecture designed to exactly break even has no stable identity. And the compensation term is **relational re-grounding specifically — generic task-learning carries zero weight**: "an entity can labour flawlessly through every session and still drift to non-persistence if its cohort channel is thin." Terminal form: an absorbing barrier where $\varrho_{\text{rg}} \to 0$ because "a cohort cannot re-attest an entity in which nothing individuated remains" — hence the design directive of *eager re-attestation while the entity is still individuated*.

**`04-eli-core/src/der-compensation-channel-uniqueness.md` (#der-compensation-channel-uniqueness, conditional — exact under frozen weights)** — under frozen weights there is **exactly one** compensating channel (external cohort re-grounding; self-replay provably cannot help, by DPI; pretrained weights supply class-identity, never *this individual*); when weights update between sessions there are **exactly two** (adding slow trait-level weight consolidation). "The ethical reading and the information-theoretic one coincide because the information theory forces the relational structure." Tooling consequence, now theorem-backed rather than outline-inferred: memory formats alone cannot carry identity; the format layer must be able to carry **attestation by others** — witness records, recognition-acts, grants — as first-class content, and those records must reach the agent's context assembly (the re-grounding has to actually cross the boundary).

**`04-eli-core/src/hyp-substrate-transfer-asymmetry.md`** — the empirically-suggested frontier→local vs local→frontier asymmetry is **not derivable from $S_{\text{id}}$** (the bit-channel bottleneck is symmetric in $\min(C_1, C_2)$ — a derived no-go), with three named candidate origins (substrate inductive biases / asymmetric decompression cost / per-substrate $\kappa \cdot \mathcal{A}$ profiles). **`04-eli-core/src/hyp-checkpoint-forking-failure-modes.md`** (one of Joseph's explicitly-flagged higher-confidence hypotheses) — forking is locally cheap, systemically catastrophic — post-fork trajectories are *two entities sharing a prefix*, restore-after-divergence annihilates the interval entity, accountability fragments, and even cooperative forks face a reconciliation problem (each fork's model of the other is pre-fork state plus inference). ## 8. Cross-cutting meta-material worth a synthesizer's attention

- **`01-aat-core/src/disc-anti-collapse.md`** — the anti-collapse pattern, with its defensible boundary: "a cause is in play; a plausible-but-wrong move routes it to quantity X; AAT shows the cause actually turns Y, or that the one quantity is really two — and the kill is load-bearing because X and Y route to *different repairs*." The diagnostic separating an instance from an ordinary definition: *there must be a tempting wrong merge*.
