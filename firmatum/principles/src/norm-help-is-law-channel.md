# Norm: help is the law channel

`--help` is the ex-ante half of the law channel: what the tool will and will not ever do, taught *before* the first refusal. The description field and the help page are that surface. Refusals are the ex-post half of the same channel.

Hand-rolled and readable. Name the dangerous cases in the help itself when they exist.

Not bound: `--help --format=json`, `--list-flags`, generated completions — later shared-crate axis, not a floor.
