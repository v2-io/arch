# Norm: estate overlays are config

Estate law stays out of the baked binary. Adding a name to ignore, a furniture mapping, a pin — that is a config line, not a release.

The overlay lives with its **rightful owner** (the 2026-08-14 sharpening):

- **The tree owns facts about itself** — what may be touched, what is verbatim, what is furniture *in this place*. These may be repo files, discovered by walking up from the target ([[norm-config-walks-from-locus]]). Specimen: `.md-pressignore` → md-press.
- **The caller owns how it sees** — every tuning knob rides the caller stack, never a repo file ([[norm-caller-tunes-the-channel]], [[form-caller-key]]). Specimen: aspectus caller-stack config.

Either way the binary ships defaults and the estate overlays them without a rebuild.
