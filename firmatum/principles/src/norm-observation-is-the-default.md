# Norm: observation is the default; non-observation is a decision with a stated reason

Before acting in or reasoning about a place, look at it. The un-run glance is not a neutral omission — it is a choice to work priors-blind, and it owes its why (Joseph's form: *"unless you are purposefully trying to be blind to the surroundings / locus, run aspectus anywhere you go. And if you* are *purposefully blinding yourself, why?"* — and the mode is perceptual, not interrogative: landscape before question, [[form-agentic-eyes]] §mode-of-use).

**The duality** (field agent's articulation, 2026-08-14, banked in the udon estate's sop-influx the same evening): this is *"the read-side dual of 'constrain destructive actions by tool-set, not prose'"* — the write-side law bounds what a hand may do; this bounds what a mind may skip seeing. Both convert an invisible default into an owned decision.

**The enablement condition, stated honestly:** the blanket is only enforceable when an instrument makes the default nearly free — *"the friction was never missing intelligence, it was a missing cheap instrument"* (the crystallized-process thesis in miniature). A norm like this asserted *without* a cheap honest glance-instrument is exhortation; with one, it is an affordance. aspectus is the first such instrument; the norm outlives it.

**The one legitimate exemption, kept precise so the blanket needs no fuzzy escape hatch:** de-novo / anti-priming work — and even there the honest form is *unprimed observation, not blindness*: shape facts (tree, censuses, mass, kinds) are auditor-safe; provenance-of-work facts (heat, age, recency order, deltas) carry exactly the signal a de-novo pass must not inherit. The cold-preset demand ([[../../utils/aspectus/design/presets.md|presets]]) is this exemption made mechanical.
