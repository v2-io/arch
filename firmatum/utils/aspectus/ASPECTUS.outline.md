- The pipeline rows have a foundations list of segments and will usually have an associated working segment and/or a design segment. The work doesn't have to block on those existing **iff** the foundation documents cover the issue completely and are completely narrowed in scope and the description is self-explanatory.
- The pipeline is a representation of **user-facing stories** / features (where *user* is a human agent, logogenic agent, or system environment and automation scripts). A development task is not a feature. Those belong in the work/impl. notes. The one exception we can allow for simplicity here is preparatory *Prefactor* "feature" that puts in place any underlying scaffold, infrastructure, or meta-processes and tooling that directly enable the subsequent actual features. Ideally *prefactor* work should group together necessary chores and tasks that have a definitive test-provable outcome even if it isn't user-facing per se.
- User-facing and development-critical story, intent, behavior, sunny-day scenarios, edge & corner cases, fixtures, and so forth, justified by the justifying documents (that it cites) are in the **Feature** column link which links to the `design/` directory.
- Features are auditable-- if they are not justified and correctly cited, or especially if they oppose any of the underlying principles, they are considered malformed and unimplementable as drafted.
- Any new jargon associated with the utility needs to be fully defined in its own `def-*` segment in `src/`, and put in front of the design link in the table's feature column.
- **Development / Implementation** concerns, breakdowns, details, discussions, and tracking (hierarchal todos etc.) are all in the `impl/<slug>` file in the **Dev** column. Other higher-level efforts and tracking are in [[./PRACTICA.md|PRACTICA]]

#### Principles (The rest)

- There are unprocessed principles in `../../principles/influx/**` that can be used as segments here. For example, `cli-conventions/` lives there, with its undifferentiated cli-level general concerns, but without specific conventions nailed down necessarily.
- As they get specially modified, with decisions made etc., and especially if they are to be used as principles, properly broken into atoms, they should be moved to be segments in `../../principles/src/**`. Aspectus doesn't have to use everything there-- but what it does use for general principles or shared instrumentation principles should be there. 
- Principles and design that are unique (for now at least) to Aspectus will end up in `./src/`
- Note that if a principle is *too heavy / broad* to work as a justifying foundation for a feature, then it really does need to be broken up either for all projects that use the shared principles, or within src.

#### Appendix (Reports etc.)

- These are as of yet unseparated larger files mined from various places in the estate that can be used to understand some of the underlying principles and that *shouldn't* be used as foundation references but rather be used to copy from and refine for the utility's more specific needs etc. They are syntheses of some estate scattered principles and are the root documents of others.

## *Part* I — Aspectus pipeline

Facts × offices for the one look live in [[design/aspect-lattice|Aspect lattice]] — *very provisional*, orthogonal to the order of these rows, and not a substitute for a row’s own design.

The short-term aim above every row (Joseph, 2026-08-14): agents spot, focus, discount, and never see the holes in their own orientation — *"the biggest surprisal we are trying to fix in the short term is 'SURPRISE! This "directory" is far more than you thought it was!'"* An aspecta's first duty is calibrating the reader's sense of how much it has not seen ([[design/mass|Mass]], the censuses, [[design/denied|Denied]]) — and its second, inseparable (Joseph, on the recency+heat defaults): the glance *"immediately gets all the mass and 'unknown unknowns' turned into 'unknowns'* and *sees it as a living, evolving system"* — not an inventory of a dead tree but a place where work is visibly alive (recency sort, heat, the aliveness censuses). Row order follows one principle: do next what makes the one after it seem natural and readily obvious to everyone.
