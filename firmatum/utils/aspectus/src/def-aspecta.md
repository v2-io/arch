# Definition: aspecta

An **aspecta** is one look: the seen-things from one running of aspectus on one locus.

House morphology: memorata, percepta, operata — the *a* is what was gathered. aspectus is the faculty; an aspecta is one product of it. Two prints of the same path are two aspecta. They may differ.

It is the picture, not a summary essay about the place, and not the place itself. Text on stdout is one view of an aspecta. JSON or UDON, if they come, are other views of the same object — transport of the seen-things, not a second truth.

It is not an intermediate representation of something else. The snapshot *is* the thing.
