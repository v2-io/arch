## Claude-family blank agent — findings, routed

**Experiential confirmations** (the design working as intended, in its words):
- Attention order on every screen: `[git: …]` first ("the single highest-value fact and it's exactly where my eye lands") → `[kind: …]` → top children ("literally 'what's alive here'").
- Censuses: an unexpanded directory *"feels closed but not opaque"* — "with `tree -L 2` a cut branch is a void I feel obligated to go probe; here the census discharges that anxiety." Trusted without double-checking; the one spot-check held.
- `--inspect git` "resolved that trust question permanently"; the `≥`/`[denied]`/`[walk bound]` marks are "an explicit epistemics of its own incompleteness — the difference between 'picture' and 'sample.'"
- The killer demo, unprompted: `~/src` — "30-odd repos with remote/branch/sha/dirty each, in ~120 lines… a work-in-flight map of Joseph's whole world in one screen."
- "Over `tree` essentially always"; replaces the `ls` + `git status` + `git log -1` + probing ritual in one budgeted call.

**Findings, with routes:**

1. **Recency default scrambles *first* orientation** — "in asf, `audits/` outranked `01-aat-core/` and I momentarily mis-weighted what the repo *is*. `--sort name` fixed it instantly. Recency for re-orientation, name for first orientation… the default optimizes for the returning visitor, not the newcomer." → *Real tension with the steward-decided default; partially answered by important-files weight (load-bearing names float) and mass (structure legible regardless of order). A "first-contact" vs "what-moved" preset is its own idea — queued for Joseph.*
2. **`[kind: git]` misfires** — `.pytest_cache/` claims `git` (from a `.gitignore` inside); `ref/` dirs claim it from deep clones; and the `kind: git` word collides with the `[git: …]` facet ("made me distrust the facet for a minute"). → *Actionable: gitignore-family map rows should not stamp `git` kind (presence-of-ignore-file ≠ this-is-a-repo); kind `git` should require `.git` itself. Assign at Wave C review (furniture map).*
3. **Leftover vs census same grammar, different semantics** ("I briefly added them wrong") → *already fixed by the census rework in flight (unified `+` = unlisted; new grammar).*
4. **`--sort size` can't rank dirs and doesn't say so** → *mass's deep-agg gives dirs a size; until then the sort should name its limitation (menu-refusal honesty applied to partial keys). Wave C.*
5. **Load-bearing names buried** (README/CLAUDE/TODO in the `[+26: …]` remainder at asf) → *exactly the important-files row; priority confirmed by field testimony. Wave D.*
6. **JSON wanted** ("consume rather than read") → *Wave D row, design ready.*
7. **Help vocabulary costs a beat** ("aspecta/locus/carta… before the operative facts") → *noted; the defs are law-channel by design, but operative-facts-first ordering within help is worth weighing.*
