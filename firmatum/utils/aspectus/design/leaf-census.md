# Leaf census

Leftover *siblings* the line budget could not list:

```
└── [+ dir×1 ≈12f · txt×3]
```

The `+` marks “not listed” — the one place `+` appears since the census rework ([[dir-census|Dir census]], 2026-08-14): an unexpanded directory’s own census needs no `+` because position (on the dir’s line) already says “below this.” Omitted dirs render as containers with their mass, same as everywhere.
