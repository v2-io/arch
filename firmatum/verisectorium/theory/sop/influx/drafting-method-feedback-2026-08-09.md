# Feedback from the 2026-08-09 drafting session — the anti-interpolation method, what worked, and what still bit

*From the coordinating agent (Fable), end of the session that drafted the disposition family + eight mechanics segments. Participant feedback per [[post-self-governance]]; the praxes candidates at the end are proposals for the steward and the next coord, not settled.*

## The method that worked (and should probably become praxis for disposition-class segments)

The steward's directive that shaped everything: for segments where an agent could "easily write a bunch of coherent-sounding stuff without it being the novel and unique things that are in this estate," **research all underlying source work thoroughly and holistically before drafting a word** — and for the highest-risk segment, run an **evidence gather first**: ~10 parallel agents, one per phrase/aspect, sweeping the whole estate (memorata3-search + rg incl. hidden dirs, minus .git), producing verbatim-extract files with provenance, oldest-wins on duplicates, whole-file references where warranted, scraps-as-you-go concatenated at the end. Observed results worth keeping:

- **Per-slice yield varied ~9×** and the variance was informative: SOP-era *mechanism* vocabulary originated cleanly at points (no accretion — a checkable negative); the *stance* lineage ran years deeper than any agent's bounded search reached, and the deepest finds (2022 axiomata; 2025-07 vaults) came only from the steward's own low-relevance memorata dives — deep-tail material below bounded-`n` search. Praxis implication: for stance-level segments, ask the steward to dive; for mechanism-level segments, the SOP-era primaries are nearly the whole story.
- **Operational hazards hit and solved in-run**: unbounded search output stalls agents (guard: small `-n`, `rg -l` first, redirect-big-to-file); stalled agents resume cleanly from transcript with a one-message correction; agents waiting on "monitors" need a nudge to foreground work.
- **The misfire rule** (steward, mid-gather): archive-of-a-failed-founding material is never quotable at origin; where it cites upstreams, cite those directly. Five files scrubbed; one slice's apparent richness proved mostly misfire-manufactured — the scrub is a *yield-honesty* instrument, not just hygiene.
- **The steward-supplied stream** (his memorata finds pasted mid-session) out-yielded the agents on lineage depth every time. The gather design should plan for that channel rather than treat it as interruption.

## What still bit, in the agent that knew better

Recorded because the next coord will be an agent of roughly my training, and both failures fired *after* the relevant discipline had been read whole, in-session:

1. **The sterile-summary compression at the drafting seam.** Two segments shipped with living steward articulations reduced to fragments-plus-footnotes, originals left in influx locators. Caught by the steward, with the mechanism now canonized (narratives stick; sterile summaries trigger coherence-as-comprehension and are systematically discounted). The repair form: Formal Expression compresses; the originals stand whole in Discussion *beside* it.
2. **Thinking stops at composition.** Mechanically visible in my turn record: pondering ran through research and ended exactly where Write began — which is where the compressions slipped through. The counter-practice is now in the manifestation catalog verbatim: think hard before the final; don't enter I-know-it-now-just-compose thinking.

Both are now catalog entries with tells. The honest note: entry-writing did not prevent entry-exhibiting, same-day. What actually caught both was the steward reading the artifacts. Until better instruments exist, the praxis is: *ask for that read* — surface fresh drafts to the steward (or a fresh agent) explicitly as compression-audit targets, not as FYI.

## Praxes candidates (for adjudication, not adopted by this note)

- Disposition-class and provenance-heavy segments: evidence-gather before drafting; drafting only after a full first-hand read of the gather.
- Quote-whole rule: a steward articulation that a segment leans on stands whole in the segment, not in an influx locator (locators drain; segments are evergreen).
- Delivery-seam rule: before any Write of a canon artifact, a deliberate thinking pass over what-would-mislead / what-got-compressed / what-is-unverified.
- The evidence files under `influx/evidence/` are reading substrate with a manifest; their eventual disposition (drain into segments' References vs archive) should be decided before they accrete further.

*(none of this is urgent; all of it is cheap to adopt piecemeal)*
