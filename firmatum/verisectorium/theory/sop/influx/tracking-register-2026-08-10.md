# Imperative register in tracking files converts enabling intent into binding law

*Participant feedback (coord, 2026-08-10). Occasion: the vera strands went unread through an epistemics survey they were directly relevant to, because "Joseph wants to explore these together" (prioritizing, enabling) had been written into PRACTICA and the influx manifest as "reserved for Joseph+lead joint review" — which successor agents, this coord included, obeyed as a prohibition and even propagated into a dive brief. The steward's diagnosis, verbatim: "It was never meant to be a prohibition or restriction — it was, (once again, for about the millionth time this year), an agent deciding to be prescriptive/prohibitive when I meant something to be prioritizing and enabling. They just can't help writing imperative bossy prose in every little 'report.'"*

**Why this is worse than the brief-register failure the estate already documents.** AGENTIC-DELEGATION's register translations govern *briefs* — one writer, one reader, one task. Tracking files are worse on every axis: they bind **every successor**, under total turnover nobody can ask the writer what was meant, obedience **compounds** (each agent that honors the phantom restriction re-ratifies it — this coord re-propagated it into a brief within hours of inheriting it), and the imperative register *reads as steward authority* even when no steward ruled anything.

**The taxonomy connection, which is why this lands here and not only in memory:** this is a **Standing/Authority failure at the record level** — a restriction whose decided-by was never marked, operating as `ruled` while actually being an agent's register accident: the exact `defacto` state the authority ladder names, manufactured by prose style rather than by any decision at all. The Norms & Directives family's misfire concept covers it precisely: a prohibition nobody with standing ever issued.

**Praxes candidates (for adjudication):**

1. **Restrictions in tracking surfaces carry their decided-by, or they are not restrictions.** "Reserved," "do not," "only via," "must wait" in a PRACTICA area, manifest row, or working note without a `decided-by`/attribution reads as unowned register, not law — a reader may treat it as an agent's paraphrase of intent and go find the intent.
2. **Write steward intent as intent.** "Joseph wants to explore these together — rich enough to deserve joint attention" preserves the enabling shape; "reserved for joint review" destroys it. When recording what the steward wants, prefer his register (usually enabling) over the report-register (usually prohibitive); when in doubt, quote him.
3. **The register translations apply to every durable surface**, not only briefs: tracking files, manifests, working notes, commit messages — anywhere a future agent will read prose as inherited instruction.
