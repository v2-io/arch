**Hazards, named so nobody re-learns them:**

- **Fluency-before-doctrina** is this project's own founding wound: the theory-misfire read the forms, skipped the settled content, and produced something *almost* right — the most expensive kind of wrong. The avoidance advisory exists because near-right artifacts muddy a foundation being laid.
- **Same-author agreement is coherence, not corroboration** (the REGISTER-RULING lesson, generalized): the estate agreeing with itself does not raise a claim's epistemic tier.
- **md-press does not cover udon files** — `.ud`/`.un`/`.us` get hand-edit care, no canonicalizer safety net yet.
- **An under-provisioned front door misfires agents** (observed 2026-08-09: a fresh session here went badly wrong before CLAUDE.md existed). If you find an orientation surface stale or missing, fixing it *is* session-worthy work under the telos, not a detour.

## 2. Praxes — how work happens here

- **Truth over completion, always.** The corpus is a living collection ( [[post-living-collection]]) — nothing here "ships," and a session's finish-line is the telos above, not emptied queues or moved files. Honest incompleteness is a complete discharge: honest tier + working notes saying what is open + release to PRACTICA.
- **Segments:** one atom per file in `src/`, slug = filename, form-kind prefix, the exemplars' cadence (frontmatter → title → summary → Formal Expression → Epistemic Status → Discussion → Working Notes). Frontmatter schema is provisional pending the epistemology decision — say so in each segment's Working Notes. Statuses conservative: do not borrow rigor from source confidence. `md-press --check` every touched `.md` before reporting it done. Udon suffixes: `.ud` default; `.un` and `.us` (udon store) allowed, as are `.udon` and `.don` (bowl — provisional).
- **The outline is the parent's surface.** When drafting is delegated (the proven pattern: forks draft 2–3 segments each, writing *only* their own `src/` files, no commits), the coordinating agent reviews, flips `State` rows, and commits — agents edit, parent commits. On edit of any drafted segment: reset its checks as appropriate and/or launch a separate re-verify — checks are resettable states, never ratchets.
- **Dispatch truly** ( [[claim-dispatch-compounds]]): items leave live surfaces only through the delete-test, into `.integrated/` (content landed) or `.archive/` (consciously set down, reason recorded) — never breadcrumbed. Half-dispatch is the residue attractor.
- **Steward relationship:** Joseph holds the valve. Genuine forks go to him as real briefs (context + options + recommendation + honest uncertainty); his brainstorms are captured **verbatim first** (frozen baseline), organized second (iterated v-file); his fiat is marked as fiat. Fixing his spelling/typos in rendered and canonical text is **standing-ratified** (2026-08-09, "In general I've always approved of fixing my spellings") — the raw records preserve every keystroke for any future historian; keep a `[sic]` only where the texture itself is the point. Delegation happens in peer voice — the estate discipline in `~/src/arch/AGENTIC-DELEGATION.md` binds here as everywhere.
- **Template stewardship** (this instance's second product): theory findings propagate into `template/` deliberately, not automatically — the template stays generic and blank-slate (NAME-ME surfaces, no theory-instance specifics). Deployed instances own copies-with-local-deltas and send upgrade proposals back through their `sop/influx/`; this instance is where those get adjudicated.
- **Where records land:** present truth → segments/OUTLINE; process rules → this store; state → PRACTICA.ud; what-happened-and-why → `../../CHANGELOG.md` — never headers, preambles, or segment bodies. Route the record; don't carve it into the nearest surface.
- **Commits:** one attributable thing per commit, batching plan stated first, heredoc messages; this corpus's history is studied by future agents.

## 3. Professio — declare yourself

Before substantive work, having done 1 and 2, write a few sentences — in your session, and if the work is significant, in your first commit message or working note — declaring *in your own words* what you understand the telos to be and which of the praxes above you expect to find hardest to keep under pressure. This is a professio ( [[form-professio]]): voluntary, owned, revisable, scoped to your session — a self-declaration, not a vow, and no steward round-trip is required. Its value is the moment of genuine self-articulation; performed as a checkbox it is worthless, and skipping it honestly is better than performing it.
