# Formulation: The Evidence Gather

For interpolation-hazardous segments (disposition-class, provenance-heavy, steward-voice-carrying), drafting is preceded by a parallel estate-wide evidence gather — per-aspect sweep agents producing verbatim-extract files with provenance, oldest-wins deduplication, and whole-file references — read whole by the drafter before a word is composed.
