# Claim: Atomized Work Fits the Session Cycle

Atomized work grants agents concrete start/work/finish cycles without the completion ethos infecting the living whole — the proposed mechanism behind the pattern's success under ad-hoc application.

## Working Notes

*(pre-draft: a first-person specimen recorded while fresh, 2026-08-09 coord)*
