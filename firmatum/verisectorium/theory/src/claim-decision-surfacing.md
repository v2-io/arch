# Claim: Decisions Must Be Surfaced Assembled

Systemic stalls in a steward-coupled living collection reduce to decisions that never reached the steward in actionable form — and the failure is almost never lost context but *un-assembled* context: the fix is an assembly step that must happen in the cycle that produces the fork, by the agent who holds it, because assembly is only cheap while the context is hot.

## Formal Expression

*[Claim (the reduction)]*

1. **Every stall reduces to an unsurfaced decision.** The estate's process review placed decision-routing as *"the parent, not a sibling"* of its other findings: generation, verification, and integration all backed up at points where a genuinely-steward call sat unrouted.[^spine] The claim's content is the *reduction* — that what presents as a backlog problem, a motivation problem, or a tooling problem is, at the root, a fork that never reached the one person who could close it, in a form he could close it from.

2. **The context is reconstructible; it is un-assembled.** Checked per-blocker in the estate's census: the context a decision needs is almost always intact — sitting in a spike directory, a proposals block, an audit table cell, and the finishing agent's scrollback — and the steward's lived pain (*"I have no idea what they're talking about"*) is the experience of being handed **a pointer or a wall instead of a brief**.[^census] An index cannot solve a briefing failure.

3. **Therefore: assembly, in-cycle, by the holder.** *"The fix is an assembly step, and assembly is only cheap while the context is hot — i.e. it must happen in the cycle that produces the fork, by the agent who holds it, not deferred to when the steward asks and the agent is gone."*[^census] Under [[post-total-turnover]] this is not a preference but a deadline: the assembling mind is destroyed on a clock, and reconstruction-later costs a re-derivation that assembly-now gets free.

4. **The assembled form is a real brief**: reconstructed context written for zero scrollback + options with one-line consequences + a recommendation with honest confidence (mandatory — *"'your call' with no recommendation is executor-mode abdication"*) + honest uncertainty (what was not verified; what would change the recommendation) + pointers last and supplementary, never the substance.[^census] The receiving surface for these briefs is the valve ( [[form-steward-valve]]).

## Epistemic Status

Heuristic, with `robust-qualitative` attainable and as ceiling. The evidence is one estate's, but unusually well-verified for a process claim: the census was first-hand (~11 genuine steward-blockers checked individually for context-state, with the reconstructible-but-un-assembled finding holding across all of them), the leak was verified mechanically (zero of six July forks reached the valve, by grep), and the capability half has existence proofs — the estate *already produces* excellent decision briefs ad hoc; *"the capability exists; the standard slot and the routing do not."*[^census] The reduction claim (part 1) is the least verified: it is the review's synthesis judgment, not a per-stall demonstration, and a stall with a genuinely different root (a capability gap, say) would bound it. The census author's own register is inherited honestly: the failure model *"rests on testimony + verified structure — a design proposal, not a validated intervention."* Evidence-action: track stalls-to-root on one instance for a season; count how many terminate at an unsurfaced fork.

## Discussion

**Why briefs and not diligence.** The estate's agents were *already disciplined* at the detection step — forks were faithfully marked "needs steward" in home trackers; what was skipped, every time, was the escalation-and-assembly step, because nothing coupled it to anything agents reliably do.[^census] So the claim's practical edge is that surfacing must be *structural*: folded into cycle-close (the same move as execute-the-safe-subset-in-the-same-cycle), with a mechanical check making the leak loud. Discipline that lives in memory drains on the same clock the context does.

**Decided is not terminal.** A ratified decision can be reopened by later evidence (the estate's B-2 case: a steward-ratified closure reopened one notch by a later audit) — so the surfacing machinery needs a re-open path whose brief states *what changed since he last decided*, sparing re-litigation from zero.[^census] This composes with [[form-state-flags-not-gates]]: a decision's settledness is a resettable state, not a ratchet.

**Relation to delegation and to the norm.** A decision brief is peer-voice applied upward: it hands the steward a decision to *ratify or redirect*, never an open question — the same respect-through-context the delegation discipline demands downward. And the mandatory-recommendation clause is [[norm-truth-above-self]]'s report-don't-reassure at the decision seam: the agent's honest read, with its confidence and its unverified parts marked, is the truthful offering; withholding it to appear deferential is a helpfulness costume.
