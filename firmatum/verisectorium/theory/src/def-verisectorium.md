# Definition: Verisectorium

A verisectorium is the nine-organ structure that lets a living collection accumulate adjudicated truth across total reader turnover.

## Formal Expression

*[Definition (verisectorium)]*

A **verisectorium** is a living collection ( [[post-living-collection]]) equipped with nine organs — structural components the instance *has* — together with a process layer of clocked flows that run over them ( [[form-process-map]], [[claim-organ-process-duality]]):

| # | Organ | One-line charge |
|---|-------|-----------------|
| I | **Corpus** | Atoms under stable identity: one record per atom, slug identity, cluster anatomy, coverage honesty, layout-independent addressing |
| II | **Vocabulary & Notation** | The naming layer as component: terminology stores, notation, bounded-context scoping — the interface every reader meets before content |
| III | **Veritas** | Epistemic state as stored structure: kind ⊥ strength ⊥ process-state, evidence over assertion, resettable check-flags |
| IV | **Adjudication** | The processes that change veritas: tribunal roles, strengthen-first, verification at three tempos, decision records |
| V | **Flux** | Material crossing membranes, both directions: influx queues, integration semantics, pending surfaces, efflux seams, clocked drains |
| VI | **Views & Assembly** | Exposition as cheap projections over the expensive population — including the canon view and the outline altitude at which structural thought happens |
| VII | **Experiential Interface** | How an agent mind meets the corpus: experiential reading, orientation gates, corpus verbs, the front door |
| VIII | **Stewardship Interface** | How the human steward meets the corpus: decision surfacing, the valve, the coherence role, the accumulated steward-model |
| IX | **Norms & Instantiation** | The meta layer as its own governed corpus: rule classification, enforcement profiles, the instantiation kit, self-governance |

The definition is *structural, not prescriptive of substrate or dialect*: atom kind (claim, term, precept, section, entry, norm, …), file technology, and enforcement profile are deployment choices ( [[form-enforcement-profile]]); an instance is a verisectorium by virtue of answering the nine charges, however minimally ( [[obs-instance-spectrum]], [[obs-uneven-maturation]]).

## Epistemic Status

Definitional; the truth-apt content lives in the claims the definition organizes, not in the carve itself. The nine-way carve is one decomposition among possible ones — held because each organ names a failure class observed independently in the field data, and because the organ/process dual matched an existing instance's self-map (asf's PROCESS-MAP) without forcing. Three carve-tensions are open and tracked at the outline level: vocabulary as organ vs sub-instance of corpus; the observation store as tenth organ vs process-layer property; and the stewardship organ's boundary with the estate-wide delegation disposition.

## Discussion

**What the definition is for.** Naming the organs makes instance maturity *assessable* (which charges are answered, at what depth), makes the instantiation kit *specifiable* (minimum viable form per organ plus upgrade path — [[form-instantiation-kit]]), and gives failures an address: a rotting provenance path is an Organ I defect, a silently-red checker an Organ IV defect, a backed-up integration queue an Organ V defect, a steward who cannot see what needs deciding an Organ VIII defect.

**The two interface organs are deliberately symmetric.** VII and VIII are the same charge — how a mind meets the corpus — for the two kinds of mind that actually meet it. The asymmetry in their content (agents need orientation and causal-order reading; the steward needs decision surfacing and an unread-corpus view) is a fact about the minds, not about the structure.

**Why "adjudicated truth" and not "verified truth."** Adjudication ( Organ IV) includes verification but is wider: decisions, conventions, and normative choices are legitimately *decided* rather than verified, and a verisectorium records them in their own register rather than dressing them as derivations. The name says the collection's contents have been through a process with a record — not that everything in it is proven.
