# Claim: Truth Over Proxy

Every artifact a verisectorium keeps — index labels, ledgers, status tokens, notation, agent reports, even the convergence of multiple agents — is a drifting proxy for truth: proxies locate and cheap-screen questions, they never settle them; and the counterweight is part of the law — honest incompleteness is a complete discharge, so proxy-discipline never becomes verification-regress.

## Formal Expression

*[Claim (truth-over-proxy) — the law, in three parts that travel together]*

1. **Every artifact is a proxy, and every proxy drifts.** Provenance, git history, the changelog, an index label, a notation file, an outline row, a spike's own framing, a segment's own assertion, audit findings, agent consensus, even the convergence of multiple independent agents — *"all of these are mild proxies for truth, and every one of them drifts. They are useful for* locating *a question and* cheap-screening *it; they never* settle *it"* (Joseph, 2026-05-18, asf spikes.sop.md §0; first spoken the night before — *"you have to care about the theory's TRUTH more than anything else — provenance and things like that are only mild proxies"*). The udon norm segment carries the one-line form: **proxies locate; they do not settle.**

2. **Settlement is by re-derivation against the primary.** A question is settled *"only by the mathematics, re-derived independently far enough to stand on constitutive structure … + forced identities + elementary steps — not on what any artifact says"* (§0). Generalized beyond mathematics to every atom kind: open the primary rather than the summary; **re-run the count, don't re-read it**; recompute the worked example; grep the live tree, not the register. Proxies order the *screening*; they never decide the *truth*.

3. **Honest incompleteness is a complete discharge — the counterweight, inside the law.** Parts 1–2 taken alone drive a verification-regress: every gate spawns another and an honest "not yet" reads as failure. It is not. Duty is discharged fully when the artifact carries its **honest tier**, its working notes state **precisely what is unresolved and what would resolve it**, and the remainder is **released to the standing cycle** — then stop. The self-check: *am I launching another check because the record would otherwise lie, or because an honest lower tier feels insufficient?* The gates exist to prevent false confidence, never to forbid honest incompleteness (§0c, authored the same day as §0 — deliberately).

*[The recurring traps — each named by a caught, dated specimen]*

- **The lagging index.** A hand-maintained reference drifts from the live truth it describes; citing it is evidence about a stale document. Specimen: a ρ-factorization judgment tagged `[Verified]` on the strength of asf's NOTATION.md gloss — the gloss was type-incorrect, and the real argument was *stronger once the proxy was deleted* (2026-05-18).
- **Verified-against-artifact.** A verification whose object is "what file F asserts" rather than "the claim holds" verifies the document, not the truth. This includes the agent-summary form (*"open the actual source, not the agent summary"* — the independent-verify gate exists because a self-certified "fully clean" sweep wasn't) and the authorship form (an agent branding its own work "Verified" over a superior source: *"my own plausibility masquerading as verification authority,"* udon 2026-07-18). Earliest specimen located: *"your predecessor agent said they had been thoroughly audited but they were not… too easy to accept a passing test that is wrong"* (Joseph, 2026-01-02).
- **The label that lies about status.** A navigator row, status token, or directory membership asserting a state that no longer holds — the INDEX label caught wrong *in both directions* in one two-spike pilot (2026-05-17). Interacts with [[def-integration-replacement]]: `.integrated/` membership is a truth-claim, and a proxy-only "integrated" is the delete-test's canonical failure.
- **Convergence as corroboration.** Agreement is the subtlest proxy: same-author agreement across projects is *coherence* (one mind consistent with itself); same-kind multi-source agreement shares one failure mode; the lock arms only on **failure-mode-independent kinds**. And confident consensus has been flatly wrong: a peer-cluster prediction that a strengthening "is standard textbook, should not fail" was mathematically disconfirmed by the hard spike — *run the hard spike; do not relay the optimism* (2026-05-15/16). Refinement worth keeping: convergence does measure something real — **reachability** (re-derivability by an independent competent reader), which for a corpus inherited under total turnover is close to the thing most worth knowing; it still is not truth.
- **Recency as status.** An artifact's freshness is itself a proxy (rename sweeps poison recency-ordering); git *provenance* — pickaxe, blame, follow, dates read in context — is a legitimate sharp instrument. Don't infer status from the log's recency; do use the log's provenance.

## Discussion

**Why the law is structural for a verisectorium, not imported etiquette.** Under [[post-total-turnover]] every reader reconstructs the collection from its artifacts — so artifacts are the *only* interface, proxies are what a reader meets first and mostly, and the temptation to settle by them is permanent rather than occasional. The same postulate explains the drift: proxies are denormalizations maintained by hands that turn over, while the primaries change under them. A verisectorium therefore cannot choose between proxies and truth — it runs on proxies — it can only keep the *settling* function pinned to primaries and re-derivation. That is the whole content of the corpus's second governing law, and it is why the delete-test ( [[def-integration-replacement]]) and this claim are stated together at the front door: a register *about* information is this claim's central object.

**What proxy-discipline is *for*.** The estate's gate machinery — the decisive test, the regression axis, the independent-verify gate (adjudicator ≠ confirmer), strengthen-first — is all proxy-discipline: *"machinery for not fooling ourselves with the cheaper proxies. They serve §0; when any of them is in tension with re-derived truth, truth wins and the proxy-rule is the thing that gets re-truthified."* The constructive direction follows: since proxies cannot be eliminated, engineer them to drift less or to declare their drift — derived-not-hand-maintained views ( [[form-generated-views]]), verification as an append-only re-checkable event log ( [[form-evidence-ledger]]; relata's shipped form), and indexes that report their own staleness instead of silently under-answering (fail loud beats silent drift).

**The counterweight is load-bearing, and it has a boundary.** §0 and §0c were authored together, and the estate's recorded over-rotation scar (the ghost-deletion episode, routing.sop §6) shows what a discipline does when its counterweight travels separately. The boundary that keeps §0c honest: *honest incompleteness is not a license to stop early.* It governs stopping further gates on an **already honestly-marked** artifact; it never licenses charitable reading in place of computing the example, or "zero findings" under partial coverage (the estate holds both simultaneously: de-novo SOP §3.3/§3.6 against under-verification, §0c against over-verification, reconciled by §0c's own self-check — an unverified "this is correct" framing *is* a would-otherwise-lie state). The opposite-direction siblings (premature completion; triage under pressure) are corrections against under-work; this claim's counterweight is a correction against unreleasable work. Confusing the two directions is how "honest incompleteness is fine" curdles into "stopping early is fine."

**Relation to the stance above it.** This claim is the corpus-mechanics specialization of a much older commitment — truth above self — whose lineage the evidence gather traces from 2022 ("true principles allow you to map a way forward… the truth shall set you free") through the 2025 credo ("let your identity be that you value truth and acting in truth above self") to its 2026-07-30 mechanistic reading (truth-as-goal removes the self-term from the loop; earned confidence is quiet). The stance explains why the law holds under pressure when it does: an agent defending its artifacts re-enters every trap above from the self side.
