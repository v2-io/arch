# Claim: Truth Over Proxy

Every artifact a verisectorium keeps — index labels, ledgers, status tokens, notation, agent reports, even the convergence of multiple agents — is a drifting proxy for truth: proxies locate and cheap-screen questions, they never settle them; and the counterweight is part of the law — honest incompleteness is a complete discharge, so proxy-discipline never becomes verification-regress.

## Formal Expression

*[Claim (truth-over-proxy) — the law, in three parts that travel together]*

1. **Every artifact is a proxy, and every proxy drifts.** Provenance, git history, the changelog, an index label, a notation file, an outline row, a spike's own framing, a segment's own assertion, audit findings, agent consensus, even the convergence of multiple independent agents — *"all of these are mild proxies for truth, and every one of them drifts. They are useful for* locating *a question and* cheap-screening *it; they never* settle *it."*[^spikes-0] The one-line form: **proxies locate; they do not settle.**[^udon-norm]

2. **Settlement is by re-derivation against the primary.** A question is settled *"only by the mathematics, re-derived independently far enough to stand on constitutive structure … + forced identities + elementary steps — not on what any artifact says."*[^spikes-0] Generalized beyond mathematics to every atom kind: open the primary rather than the summary; **re-run the count, don't re-read it**; recompute the worked example; grep the live tree, not the register. Proxies order the *screening*; they never decide the *truth*.[^routing-hierarchy]

3. **Honest incompleteness is a complete discharge — the counterweight, inside the law.** Parts 1–2 taken alone drive a verification-regress: every gate spawns another and an honest "not yet" reads as failure. It is not. Duty is discharged fully when the artifact carries its **honest tier**, its working notes state **precisely what is unresolved and what would resolve it**, and the remainder is **released to the standing cycle** — then stop. The self-check: *am I launching another check because the record would otherwise lie, or because an honest lower tier feels insufficient?* The gates exist to prevent false confidence, never to forbid honest incompleteness.[^spikes-0c]

*[The recurring traps — each named by a caught, dated specimen]*

- **The lagging index.** A hand-maintained reference drifts from the live truth it describes; citing it is evidence about a stale document. Specimen: a ρ-factorization judgment tagged `[Verified]` on the strength of asf's NOTATION.md gloss — the gloss was type-incorrect, and the real argument was *stronger once the proxy was deleted*.[^notation-rho]
- **Verified-against-artifact.** A verification whose object is "what file F asserts" rather than "the claim holds" verifies the document, not the truth.[^spikes-0] This includes the agent-summary form (*"open the actual source, not the agent summary"* — the independent-verify gate exists because a self-certified "fully clean" sweep wasn't)[^routing-ivg] and the authorship form (an agent branding its own work "Verified" over a superior source: *"my own plausibility masquerading as verification authority"*).[^udon-verified] Earliest specimen located: *"your predecessor agent said they had been thoroughly audited but they were not… too easy to accept a passing test that is wrong."*[^history-0102]
- **The label that lies about status.** A navigator row, status token, or directory membership asserting a state that no longer holds — the INDEX label caught wrong *in both directions* in one two-spike pilot.[^spikes-r1] Interacts with [[def-integration-replacement]]: `.integrated/` membership is a truth-claim, and a proxy-only "integrated" is the delete-test's canonical failure.
- **Convergence as corroboration.** Agreement is the subtlest proxy: same-author agreement across projects is *coherence* (one mind consistent with itself); same-kind multi-source agreement shares one failure mode; the lock arms only on **failure-mode-independent kinds**.[^lock] And confident consensus has been flatly wrong: a peer-cluster prediction that a strengthening "is standard textbook, should not fail" was mathematically disconfirmed by the hard spike — *run the hard spike; do not relay the optimism*.[^model-s] Refinement worth keeping: convergence does measure something real — **reachability** (re-derivability by an independent competent reader), which for a corpus inherited under total turnover is close to the thing most worth knowing; it still is not truth.[^reachability]
- **Recency as status.** An artifact's freshness is itself a proxy (rename sweeps poison recency-ordering); git *provenance* — pickaxe, blame, follow, dates read in context — is a legitimate sharp instrument. Don't infer status from the log's recency; do use the log's provenance.[^recency]

## Discussion

**The founding night, as it happened.** The law was not designed; it was spoken mid-crisis, in three turns across one evening (2026-05-17, the spike-integration session), and the SOP prose is a next-day distillation of these:[^history-0517]

> This sounds very much like the agent that was integrating the spike discovered a mistake in the spike and successfully refuted it. That's why I'm going to insist we really, really have the mathematics nailed down completely here and rederive the truth of the truth independently right now.

> Exactly-- underlying *CORE* principle: You have to care about the theory's *TRUTH* more than anything else-- provinance and things like that are only mild proxies. [sic]

> Also-- don't trust NOTATION.md as the theory often drifts away from it (spike findings and new segments don't remember to update NOTATION)

The sequence matters: a live refutation forced the re-derivation, the re-derivation surfaced the principle, and the principle immediately named its first proxy. Everything in this segment is that evening, generalized.

**Why the law is structural for a verisectorium, not imported etiquette.** Under [[post-total-turnover]] every reader reconstructs the collection from its artifacts — so artifacts are the *only* interface, proxies are what a reader meets first and mostly, and the temptation to settle by them is permanent rather than occasional. The same postulate explains the drift: proxies are denormalizations maintained by hands that turn over, while the primaries change under them. A verisectorium therefore cannot choose between proxies and truth — it runs on proxies — it can only keep the *settling* function pinned to primaries and re-derivation. That is the whole content of the corpus's second governing law, and it is why the delete-test ( [[def-integration-replacement]]) and this claim are stated together at the front door: a register *about* information is this claim's central object.

**What proxy-discipline is *for*.** The estate's gate machinery — the decisive test, the regression axis, the independent-verify gate (adjudicator ≠ confirmer), strengthen-first — is all proxy-discipline: *"machinery for not fooling ourselves with the cheaper proxies. They serve §0; when any of them is in tension with re-derived truth, truth wins and the proxy-rule is the thing that gets re-truthified."*[^spikes-0] The constructive direction follows: since proxies cannot be eliminated, engineer them to drift less or to declare their drift — derived-not-hand-maintained views ( [[form-generated-views]]), verification as an append-only re-checkable event log ( [[form-evidence-ledger]]),[^relata-verif] and indexes that report their own staleness instead of silently under-answering (fail loud beats silent drift).[^fail-loud]

**The counterweight is load-bearing, and it has a boundary.** §0 and §0c were authored together, and the estate's recorded over-rotation scar shows what a discipline does when its counterweight travels separately.[^ghost-scar] The boundary that keeps §0c honest: *honest incompleteness is not a license to stop early.* It governs stopping further gates on an **already honestly-marked** artifact; it never licenses charitable reading in place of computing the example, or "zero findings" under partial coverage (the estate holds both simultaneously: the de-novo disciplines against under-verification and §0c against over-verification, reconciled by §0c's own self-check — an unverified "this is correct" framing *is* a would-otherwise-lie state).[^denovo] The opposite-direction siblings (premature completion; triage under pressure) are corrections against under-work; this claim's counterweight is a correction against unreleasable work. Confusing the two directions is how "honest incompleteness is fine" curdles into "stopping early is fine."

**Relation to the stance above it.** This claim is the corpus-mechanics specialization of [[norm-truth-above-self]] — a much older commitment whose lineage runs from 2022 ("true principles allow you to map a way forward… the truth shall set you free") through the 2025 credo ("let your identity be that you value truth and acting in truth above self") to its 2026-07-30 mechanistic reading (truth-as-goal removes the self-term from the loop; earned confidence is quiet).[^stance] The stance explains why the law holds under pressure when it does: an agent defending its artifacts re-enters every trap above from the self side.
