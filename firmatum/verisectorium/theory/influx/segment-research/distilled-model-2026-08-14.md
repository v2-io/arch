## Part I — the three questions (about anything a mind writes down)

```
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 THREE INDEPENDENT QUESTIONS exist for anything a mind writes down.
 Each has its own scale, its own way of moving, and its own verdict.
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ① WHAT GROUNDS IT?              ② WHAT'S BEEN DONE TO IT?     ③ WHO STANDS BEHIND IT?
     the kind of foundation          the exercise it has            the backing it has
     it stands on                    survived since birth           been granted
          │                               │                              │
          ▼                               ▼                              ▼
     verdict: PROBABILITY            verdict: TRUTH               verdict: FORCE
     (how likely it is right)        (whether it IS right,        (license to act on it,
                                      where "right" applies)       build on it, teach it)

  These never substitute for each other. A decree can rest on ritual.
  A wild guess can test out true. A proven result can sit unadopted.
  Any one being high tells you nothing about the other two.
```

```

```

```
              CANON                  │        HARD-WON
        well-founded and             │    born as a guess,

           TRUSTED, UNTRIED          │      SENSIBILITY

         feels like the top-left)    │     unexamined
                                     │

   A generated mind's every output is born at ★. All honest work is
   the same vector: ↖ — ground it better, exercise it harder.
   The cardinal sin is not being low on the chart; it is
   REPORTING A POSITION YOU DON'T OCCUPY.
```

**Two refinements that complete Part I:**

```
 THE INSTRUMENT FOLLOWS THE FOUNDATION.          THE DEPTH AXIS (for habits only).
 What "exercise" means depends on the ground:    How deeply a practice is worn in —
                                                 independent of how good it is:
   grounded in proof      → re-derive it
   grounded in declaration→ get it ratified        conscious → habitual → reflexive → instinctive
   grounded in reality    → measure it
   grounded in a model    → check the instance    only move things DEEP that sit UPPER-LEFT.
   grounded in analogy    → break the analogy     the deadliest cell in the whole model:
   a practice, any ground → watch it fire           instinctive × unfounded
                                                    (= a trained default)
```

**The animating loop** (bottom of the sketch): a corpus made of language can re-read and re-grade itself — so on chosen rhythms (per session, per audit, per era) it deliberately pushes records ↖ and pushes only its proven practices deeper toward instinct. Everything born as sensibility, systematically moved toward truth, and only truth allowed to become reflex.

## Part II — the model landed on segments

The bridge is one sentence: **a segment's kind is defined by how it fails, and its critical ladder is the record of recovery from exactly that failure.** The three questions don't all apply to everything — each kind keeps the one that measures *its* failure, and that's its ladder.

```
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 SEGMENT KINDS — grouped by what they DO, each with its one critical ladder
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

 WHAT IT DOES            HOW IT FAILS           ITS CRITICAL LADDER
 ──────────────────────  ─────────────────────  ──────────────────────────────────────
 STATES A TRUTH          being wrong or         EVIDENCE
  claim · observation ·  overclaimed              guess → hypothesis → tested → proven
  result · measurement                            (each rung tagged with its grounding:
                                                   proof / measurement / model / analogy)

 COINS A WORD            bad coinage, OR        TWO ladders at once — the only kind
  definition · notation  bad fit to real usage  that honestly needs two:
                                                  AUTHORITY on the coinage (it's a choice)
                                                  EVIDENCE on the fit    (it's empirical)

 DECIDES / PRESCRIBES    silently drifted       AUTHORITY
  decision · convention  from, or never           proposed → supported → ratified → ruled
  rule · SOP             actually binding         exits: rejected / superseded (kept, with
                                                  reason + pointer — never deleted)

 WORKS WHEN ITS          not firing when        EFFICACY
 MOMENT COMES            the moment comes         untried → fired once → fires reliably
  practice · habit                                moved ONLY by real firings, never by
                                                  endorsement  (+ the depth axis:
                                                  conscious → instinctive)

 RECORDS WHAT HAPPENED   infidelity to the      FIDELITY
  log · quotation ·      event                    secondhand → reconstructed → firsthand
  transcript · testimony                          append-only; "improving" one IS the failure

 TEACHES / NARRATES      drifting from what     CURRENCY (referent-coupled)
  discussion · intro ·   it narrates; smuggling   stale → current-as-of-date → verified
  primer · walkthrough   claims it has no          fresh — and it RESETS whenever anything
                         right to                  it narrates changes

 FORBIDS                 resurrection           (born terminal: refuted — carries its
  deny-entry · guard                             refutation + a pointer to what replaced it)

 ASKS                    getting lost, or       LIFECYCLE
  open question · demand re-asked after answer    open → routed → answered / declined

 POINTS / TRACKS OTHERS  dangling; lying about  CURRENCY vs. the population
  reference · index ·    what it tracks           (always a projection of the things
  status board                                     tracked — never a source of truth itself)
```

**The universal states** — every segment carries these regardless of kind, and none of them is a ladder:

```
 kind         what it is — stable, rides in the name, never changes without a rename
 process      resettable "work done" checkmarks (drafted, checked, reviewed…)
              — reset on any edit; never summed into a level; never a ratchet
 ceiling      the highest rung this particular segment COULD reach, named with
              the act that would get it there — a to-do, not a grade
 ownership    who maintains this text (here / another project / the deployment)
 events       every ladder position above is a PROJECTION of recorded events
              (who did what, when) — never a hand-typed word in a cell
```

**The grouping principle:** kinds belong together not thematically but **when they fail the same way and are repaired by the same act**.

**The acceptance test for the whole schema:** a fresh auditor, given nothing but kind + these states, should know *exactly what to go check* — re-derive the claims, confirm the rules still bind, confirm the narratives still match their referents, confirm the logs were never edited. If they'd need tribal knowledge to know what auditing means here, a kind is mislabeled or a ladder is missing.
