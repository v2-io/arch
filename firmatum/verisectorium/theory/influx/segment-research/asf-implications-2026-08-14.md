## The headline, revised

**ASF already implements most of the distilled model — as prose conventions of remarkable discipline. What's misaligned is the small set of machine-readable fields, which compress that richness lossily and with inconsistent projection rules.** The earlier framing ("the labeling conflates; add machinery") was wrong in emphasis: the machinery exists; it lives in prose; the frontmatter is the weakest layer, not the only layer.

What the prose layer already does, observed live:

- **Per-clause grain, with provenance**: nearly every formal block carries an inline register label — `*[Derived (name, from Cauchy-Schwarz)]*`, `*[Hypothesis (aspiration-dominance)]*`, `*[Formulation]*`, `*[Postulate]*`, `*[Worked Quantity]*` — kind + derivation-source at the clause level.
- **Regime-partitioned status**: `emp-update-gain` is frontmatter `robust-qualitative` while its Epistemic Status says *"Derived* under the Fisher-local regime… outside it, robust qualitative" — the true state is a conditional map, and the prose carries it faithfully. `result-persistence-condition`'s Epistemic Status gives distinct statuses per part.
- **Ceiling as a prose convention**: "**Max attainable:** derived (the mechanism is analytically tractable…)" (obs-gated-tempo-advantage); "**Max attainable:** exact — nothing to tighten" (detail-linear-ode-approximation); the sketch names its own promotion path ("a closed bridge would be `derived (conditional)`… until attempted, this stays `sketch`").
- **Event-trail in prose**: dated adjudications in Working Notes ("2026-07-14 adjudication: … lifted from *empirically exact* to *[Derived]*"), with statuses updated to match.
- **A full sub-record system nobody typed**: 122/170 vol-1 segments carry "Incidental audit gold" sections — accounts-family records with witness-position discipline ("substrate attribution inferred from voice where not explicit; uncertain cases hedged"), coverage statements, usage-licenses on candidates ("verify before promoting past discussion-grade"), "Belongs elsewhere" routing, "Off-ramp (NOT gold) — routed for adjudication," and regression guards ("do not 'restore' an iff"). Plus 42 `## Findings` sections whose **Novelty Claims carry their own verification-depth axis** (Search Logs tiered *intuition-only / targeted / nominally-comprehensive*).

## The misalignments (each with its specimen)

**1. `status:` is two axes plus leakage — and "empirical" is the smoking gun.** The enum members sort cleanly under the model: *axiomatic, empirical* are foundation-kinds (what grounds it); *exact, conditional, robust-qualitative, heuristic* are strengths (how far carried); *discussion-grade* is a claim-license; *sketch* is a kind. Proof of tangling: `obs-gated-tempo-advantage` is `type: observation, status: empirical` while `emp-update-gain` is `type: empirical, status: robust-qualitative` — **"empirical" appears on both axes**, meaning kind in one file and grounding in another. And `norm-explicit-strategy-condition` is `status: conditional` where conditional stretches to mean "correct when a precondition holds" for a design criterion that its own Epistemic Status says is "normative, not derived."

**2. The frontmatter scalar has no declared projection rule — it means different things per segment.** `def-satisfaction-gap` = `exact` (the *headline* claim; a marked Hypothesis rides inside its Discussion). `emp-update-gain` = `robust-qualitative` (the *floor*; its core regime is derived-exact). Same field, opposite projection choices. A mechanical consumer (or a fresh auditor sorting by status) cannot trust the field. Either declare one rule ("the status of the Formal Expression's headline claim") or carry per-part statuses.

**3. `type:` re-types on promotion, and the prefix↔type mapping has drifted.** hypothesis → derived → result are ladder positions wearing type labels (a proven hypothesis changes *type* — the one thing the model says must stay stable). Meanwhile: `impl-causal-access` has prefix `impl-` but `type: discussion`; `internal-external-decomposition` is `type: derivation` with no `deriv-` prefix; chapter intros are unprefixed discussions. The genuinely good pair to keep: **der- (result stated) vs deriv- (proofs shown)** — a real kind distinction (claim segment vs proof appendix) the wild evolved on its own.

**4. "discussion-grade" covers two failure modes; "discussion" covers two kinds.** `disc-anti-collapse` is a *claim about the framework* with convergence evidence explicitly weighed in its Epistemic Status ("convergence is not derivation; the honest tier is discussion-grade"); `cooperative-adversarial-intro` is a pure narrating bridge carrying "no formal claim of its own" — with an explicit recorded hazard that its vivid previews "should read as signposts, not as independent support." One fails by being wrong; the other fails by referent-drift. Same type, same status. (The intro even has the referent-currency problem named by a fresh reader; nothing mechanical resets it.)

**5. The evolved sub-records deserve names before they need repairs.** The audit-gold/Findings/off-ramp machinery works *because* its authors were disciplined — but it is unaddressable (you cannot cite or query "the off-ramp items across vol-1"), its promotion pipeline is manual, and its conventions are demonstrably not self-evident (two independent fresh readers flagged in-src Findings blocks as misplaced audit output — a recorded de-novo-auditor-criterion failure at the convention level). This is verisectorium's grain claim confirmed in the wild: **state and kind attach below the file**, and the taxonomy must reach that grain or it will mis-describe ASF's actual best practice.

## What follows (proposed, in leverage order)

1. **Split `status:` → `support:` × `strength:`**, with `conditional` becoming strength `exact` + named premises (the segments already name them). The prose Epistemic Status stays as the *reasons* channel — the model's projection: fields for the auditor's query, prose for the auditor's understanding.
2. **Declare the projection rule** for whatever scalar survives (headline vs floor), one sentence in FORMAT, then a sweep pass — cheap, and it makes the existing field honest without changing any segment's actual knowledge.
3. **Freeze `type:` as speech-act**; hypothesis/derived/result become ladder positions moved by recorded adjudication events (which already happen — they're in Working Notes). Reconcile the prefix drift while at it.
4. **Promote "Max attainable:" to a field** — the convention exists; this is transcription, not design.
5. **Give the sub-records kinds** (audit-gold → account; off-ramp → routed question; Findings novelty-claim → its own record with search-depth as its verification axis) — even if they stay physically inside Working Notes, a recognizable header contract makes them addressable and makes their promotion pipeline auditable.
6. **Split "discussion" honestly**: framework-claims (evidence-bearing, qualitative tier) vs narrative/pedagogy (claim-licensed, referent-current) — and give the second the referent-reset machinery, because nothing today tells an intro its chapter changed underneath it.

## Honest edges

- The strongest counter-consideration, upgraded by this read: **the prose-first system may be load-bearing for truthfulness.** The regime-partitioned Epistemic Status prose is *more* truthful than any enum; a mechanization that pressured authors to flatten "derived under Fisher-local, robust-qualitative outside" into one cell would be a regression. Any field schema must be additive over the prose, never a replacement for it.
