## The primitive

> **A record is a promise made to future strangers. Its epistemic state is the ledger of what has been done to secure that promise — kept so a stranger knows exactly how much weight to put on it.**

Everything else — kinds, ladders, states, ceilings, resets, certifications — is derived from this by one procedure. The founding agent never faces an inventory of families and axes to pick from; they face their own project's promises, and run the questions below per promise. The states and ladders are *outputs*.

## The contract — six questions per kind of record

```
 0. DID IT VALIDLY COME INTO BEING as its declared kind?
    The standing gate — before any ladder. A "decision" recorded by someone
    without the power to decide is not a weak decision; it never existed as
    a decision (Austin's misfire; Hohfeld's power/liability; chain-of-custody).
    Misfire ≠ wrong-content: a gate, never a rung.

 1. WHAT IS PROMISED?             true? · wisely chosen? · binding? · faithful
    (the promise-type IS the kind;   to what happened? · current with what it
     stable, rides in the name)      narrates? · fires when its moment comes?

 2. WHAT ACTS SECURE IT, and through which named channels?
    → the set of securing acts IS the ladder-generator. Derive it as "the
      checks this promise can actually undergo, in this project": derive it,
      test it, measure it, adversarially verify it, watch it fire, verify
      against referents, ratify it…
    → each recorded securing names its CHANNEL and DIRECTION (started High
      by support-kind; −1 imprecision; −1 indirectness → Moderate). Status
      is always a projection of these named, itemized reasons — confidence-
      with-reasons is what survives audit; confidence-as-number is not.
    → securing-act KINDS are unranked (measured is not "better" than
      expert-attested, only differently produced — GUM refused the folk
      hierarchy); only the accumulated ledger orders records.

 3. WHO MAY PERFORM EACH ACT — and how trusted is each performer?
    → authority, ownership, and CEILING fall out: ceiling = the highest act
      available to this record here; steward-reserved acts = ratification.
    → higher rungs escalate the required grader (self-assess → peer
      consensus → independent review), and top rungs gate on EVENTS, not
      judgments — the flight happened or it didn't.
    → and the finding easiest to miss: WHAT THE ACT SHOWED and HOW TRUSTED
      ITS PERFORMER/CHANNEL IS are two ledger columns, never one grade.
      Every mature tradition that survived centuries split them: Admiralty's
      source-reliability × information-credibility; hadith's chain-grading
      (isnād) vs content-grading (matn); intel's likelihood vs analyst-
      confidence with a don't-combine-in-one-sentence rule. A claim can be
      well-supported by a shaky witness or barely-supported by a stellar
      one, and fusing the columns is the halo-effect failure by construction.
      Corollary: a perfect provenance chain is NOT trust — the two most
      careful provenance lineages (PROV, nanopublications) deliberately
      excluded confidence from their schemas, and metrology's VIM states
      outright that traceability does not guarantee adequacy.

 4. WHAT UNDOES EACH SECURING — and what remains honestly unknown?
    → resets, freshness, and process flags fall out: an edit, a referent
      changing, time passing, a refutation — each undoes SPECIFIC securings,
      not all of them. A securing whose undoing-trigger nobody can name is
      a label-lie waiting.
    → the ledger also records declared ignorance, in vocabulary: quantified
      spread → scenario (range known, shape not) → recognized ignorance
      (gap known, shape not) — with the reducibility split carrying the
      ceiling's WHY: epistemic gaps have no principled ceiling; aleatory
      gaps ARE the ceiling. Total ignorance is assignable only by an
      external auditor — declaring it yourself converts it.
    → null and terminal are off-ladder, and NOT-PROVEN ≠ REFUTED: "cannot
      be judged" is structurally separate from "judged worst," and both
      differ from "shown false." Termination is typed and pointed:
      revised-by vs invalidated-by vs alternate-of, whole vs partial —
      a superseded record keeps standing as history with a forwarding edge.

 5. WHAT DOES EACH RUNG LICENSE a consumer to do — or plan on?
    → CERTIFICATION: the reason tracking exists at all, and the rung-count
      discipline — a rung that changes nothing a consumer may do or rely on
      is not a rung.
    → but the license is a JUDGMENT, not a formula over the ledger: strong
      action on weak evidence and weak action on strong evidence are both
      legitimate, expected cells (GRADE's two deliberately-uncoupled axes;
      law's standard-of-proof vs what-the-evidence-shows). The certification
      act is itself a securable act — performed by whoever question 3 says
      may perform it.
```

**And one carrier the questions must not forget: the edges.** Past a bare credence, no rich system grades a claim in isolation — the load-bearing state is relational (supports vs rebuts vs *undercuts*; acceptance as a property of the attack-graph, not the node; SKOS making the relation-kind itself the confidence statement, with transitivity declared per kind to stop compound errors). The contract questions apply to `depends:`/reference edges too: an edge is a small promise ("A supports B") with its own securing and its own composability rule.

## The lifecycle — one loop, every kind

```
  ENTER (unsecured; influx = where the promise isn't even stated yet)
    → DECLARE (standing gate passed; kind chosen = promise stated; born at floor)
      → SECURE (acts performed or witnessed; recorded as EVENTS naming their
                channels; state = projection of events, never a hand-set word)
        → CERTIFY (consumers take what the rung licenses — a judgment act)
      ↖ UNDO (named triggers reset specific securings)
    → TERMINATE (typed + pointed: revised / invalidated / alternate; whole
                 or partial; promise withdrawn, standing-as-history kept)
```

One structural law from the lifecycle survey rides here: **uncertainty never lives in the lifecycle slot.** Every primary-verified standards lifecycle (IETF, W3C, Wikidata, Wikipedia) routes confidence and gaps to a different structural location than the maturity token. The loop's position and the ledger's content are different fields, always.

## The familiar ladders, falling out as theorems

- **Evidence** = the securing sequence for "this is true" (derive → test → measure → adversarially verify), each move citing its channel.
- **Authority** = securing acts reserved to the steward for "this binds" — and rungs like supported vs ratified that license the same execution still differ in what a consumer may *plan on* (declared intent = the consumer's risk model), which is rung-worthy under Q5.
- **Fidelity** = securing available only at capture time — you cannot re-witness — which *derives* accounts' append-only write semantics and low ceiling, rather than positing them.
- **Currency** = a securing whose undoing-trigger is "any referent changed" — which derives referent-reset for teaching prose.
- **Efficacy** = securings witnessed rather than performed (firings) — which derives "endorsement cannot move it."
- **Ceiling** = the highest securing act *available here* (Q3), with Q4's reducibility split supplying the why.
- **Kind-merging** = two promises are one kind exactly when their contracts come out identical; a survey of other projects is evidence for Q2 (which acts are practical), never a source of kinds.
