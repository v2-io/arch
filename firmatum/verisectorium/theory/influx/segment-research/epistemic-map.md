## The taxonomy question this map opens

The Axis-0 grouping (truth-apt · choices · directives · references · decisions) is a working carve that emerged organically, and the steward has flagged it as the start of an **official segment-kind taxonomy** — something the theory does not yet have. Known to be missing from the carve as drawn: the segment kinds actually alive in the wild beyond claim corpora — newer `.ud`/`.un` segments, `def/` entries, sop segments, discussions, intros, narratives, the comproprium kinds (precepts, practices, exempla, vera), terminology entries, demands, findings, paper sections. `form-slug-form-kinds`' speech-act vocabulary and `obs-instance-spectrum`'s atom-kind list are partial views of the same underlying question.

**Next step (steward-proposed, 2026-08-10):** an estate-wide survey of segment kinds in the wild, landing here in `segment-research/` — the intelligence needed to nail the scaffolding down rather than legislate it from one corpus's experience.
