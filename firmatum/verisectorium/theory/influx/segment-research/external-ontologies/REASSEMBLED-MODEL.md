## I. The eight cross-field laws

Ordered by strength of independent convergence — each law names its convergent traditions; two same-lineage systems never counted as two.

**L1 — Kind gates vocabulary.** Mature systems fork by claim-type *before* applying any ladder: Oxford CEBM is a matrix whose rung-referent changes per question-type[^cebm]; IPCC assigns likelihood only to claims probability applies to, confidence otherwise[^ipcc-gate]; the trio system fixes strength by register. Three unrelated lineages (clinical epidemiology, climate assessment, this estate) independently: **not every claim gets every axis; the claim's type decides which epistemic vocabulary is even meaningful.** This is the external validation of the taxonomy's deepest premise ("honesty is only well-defined relative to kind") — the strongest single finding of the survey.

**L2 — Source-trust and content-credibility are two axes, everywhere.** Admiralty A–F × 1–6 (explicitly orthogonal by construction)[^admiralty]; hadith science's isnād (chain/narrators) vs matn (content) dual grading[^hadith]; historiography's external vs internal criticism[^historiography]; ICD-203's likelihood vs analyst-confidence with a mandated don't-combine-in-one-sentence rule[^icd203]. Two strongly-attested independent traditions (Admiralty, hadith — the two oldest in the survey) plus two weaker-verified echoes (ICD-203's non-conflation rule is reported-not-read; historiography is recalled-grade with no real source behind it) — the law stands on the first two alone, and the count is stated at its honest weight *(adjudicated down from "four-plus" after the annotation pass, 2026-08-10)*.

**L3 — Evidence-certainty and action-strength are separate and non-mechanically coupled.** GRADE: strong recommendations on low-certainty evidence and weak on high-certainty are both legitimate, expected cells[^grade-2axis]; law: standard-of-proof (required confidence to act) is distinct from admissibility (may this evidence enter at all) and from what the evidence shows[^legal-proof]. Validates Assertions ⊥ Norms/Decisions and the fill's "authority composes with evidence, never substitutes."

**L4 — Uncertainty never lives in the lifecycle slot.** All four primary-verified document-lifecycle systems (IETF[^ietf], W3C[^w3c], Wikidata[^wikidata], Wikipedia[^wikipedia-assess]) — and, consistently but at recalled grade, the ISO and Akoma Ntoso entries — route confidence/gaps to a *different structural location* than the maturity/state token: implementation reports, references/qualifiers, reassessment processes[^lifecycle]. The lifecycle sweep's own strongest finding, by side-by-side convergence. Direct external confirmation of flags-not-gates and of keeping Evidence out of `state:`.

**L5 — Status is a projection of named, itemized reasons.** GRADE's eight signed domains (each rung-move attributable, auditable, directional — with *different evidentiary bars for up vs down*)[^grade-domains]; NUSAP's pedigree matrix[^nusap]; Wikidata's deprecated-rank-requires-P2241-reason[^wikidata]; IRT's misfit diagnostics[^irt]. "Reasons" here means: justifications for the *status's position*, not arguments for the claim — the answer to "why Moderate rather than High?", itemized as which named channels fired, in which direction (started High by evidence-kind; −1 imprecision; −1 indirectness → Moderate), each checkable by a reader against the underlying material. Confidence-with-attached-reasons beats confidence-as-number in every mature system that had to survive audit. Confirms the event-projection design — and GRADE adds the middle layer ours lacks: a **standard, named, per-kind catalog of the channels** a status-movement must cite (the manifestation-catalog pattern, applied as the evidence axis's movement rules), which is what makes movements comparable across atoms and aggregatable across a corpus.

**L6 — Null and terminal states are first-class and off-ladder.** "Cannot be judged" ≠ worst rung (Admiralty F/6, structurally separate from E/5)[^admiralty]; *not-proven ≠ proven-false* (legal, with different downstream consequences)[^legal-proof]; Wikidata's `normal` = no-judgment, deliberately not "pending"[^wikidata]; mawḍūʿ (fabricated) as a hard terminal off the hadith ladder, not its bottom rung[^hadith]. Validates our off-ladder honesty states and the rejected/superseded terminals — and adds the not-proven/refuted distinction our Questions and Prohibitions boundary needs.

**L7 — Supersession is append-only, typed, and pointed.** ADR's superseded-with-link ("still relevant to know it *was* the decision")[^adr]; PROV's wasRevisionOf / wasInvalidatedBy / alternateOf as three *different* supersession kinds[^prov]; Wikidata retains deprecated statements ("verifiable that someone claimed it, whether or not true")[^wikidata]; IETF's **Obsoletes vs Updates** — wholesale vs *partial* supersession as typed graph edges[^ietf]; residue's replace-with. Our design is confirmed and extended: supersession wants *grain* (partial vs whole) and *kind* (revised vs invalidated vs alternate), not one flag.

**L8 — Top rungs are event-gated and grader-escalated.** TRL 9 is unforgeable (the flight happened or it didn't)[^trl]; proof-assistant kernel-checking is a verification *event*, with gaps (`sorry`) enumerated and auto-propagated rather than graded[^proof-assistants]; Wikipedia's three governance tiers (self-assess → 2-editor consensus → formal independent review) escalate *who may assign* a rung with the rung[^wikipedia-assess]. Grounds our Max/ceiling discipline and adds a design element ours lacks: **per-rung grader-authority requirements** (kin to the independent-verify gate, made structural).

## II. The five state-tracks (the tractable per-record architecture)

What the laws assemble into — five separable tracks, each with its established backer. A record carries the tracks its kind declares (L1); no field ever does two tracks' jobs.

| Track | Question | Grounded shape | Stolen from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Standing** *(new — see III)* | Did this validly come into being as its declared kind? | A gate, not a ladder: misfire = the record never existed *as that kind* (unauthorized decision, broken chain of custody); distinct from content being wrong | Austin's misfire/abuse[^austin]; Hohfeld's power/liability[^hohfeld]; Daubert admissibility[^legal-proof]; chain-of-custody[^custody] |
| **2. Evidence** | How strongly held, on what support? | Support-kind (declared, **unranked** — GUM's Type A/B refuses hierarchy) × strength (rungs moved only by named signed domains); interval-width where evidential weight matters (same point estimate, different backing — Walley's fair-coin/unknown-coin) | GUM[^gum]; GRADE[^grade-domains]; imprecise probability[^walley]; trio system |
| **3. Force** | Who stands behind it, how bindingly? | Decided-by ladder projected from events; ceiling derived from ownership/standing; composes with track 2, never substitutes (L3) | GRADE's recommendation axis[^grade-2axis]; ADR statuses[^adr]; legal precedent (binding/persuasive × hierarchy-position)[^ratio-obiter] |
| **4. Lifecycle** | Where in the process, superseded by what? | Resettable flags + typed, pointed supersession edges (partial vs whole); default-expiry beats default-limbo (IETF drafts die in 6 months absent renewal); non-monotonic (W3C loops backward) | IETF[^ietf]; W3C[^w3c]; ADR[^adr]; PROV revision vocabulary[^prov] |
| **5. Gap declaration** | What don't we know, and is it closable? | The Walker/Harremoës ladder as vocabulary: quantified spread → scenario (range known, distribution not) → **recognized ignorance** (gap known, shape not) — with *total ignorance assignable only by an external auditor, never self-reported* (structurally impossible); plus reducibility: epistemic gaps have no principled ceiling, aleatory gaps ARE the ceiling | Walker/Harremoës[^walker]; NUSAP[^nusap]; GUM's declared-escape-hatch pattern[^gum]; Hubbard's EVI for *which gap next*[^hubbard] |

**Edges are the sixth carrier.** The formal-epistemology sweep's deepest finding: past Bayesian credence, no rich system grades one claim in isolation — the load-bearing state is *relational* (Pollock's rebut vs undercut[^pollock]; Dung's acceptance as a graph-fixpoint[^dung]; SKOS's relation-kind-IS-the-confidence with the compound-error transitivity rationale[^skos]; AIF's inference/conflict/preference edge types[^aif]). The grounded move for us: **type the `depends:`/reference edges** (supports / rebuts / undercuts / derives-from / revises / alternate-of) and declare each type's composability — the DAG carries as much epistemic state as the nodes.

## III. What our organic taxonomy got right, wrong, and missed

**Right — independently convergent with established practice** (adherence-weight aside, these now have evidence-weight): kind-gates-axes (L1); Evidence ⊥ Authority (L2/L3); flags-not-gates (L4); event-projection (L5); off-ladder honesty states + pointed terminals (L6/L7); Max/ceiling (L8 + reducibility grounds *why* ceilings exist)[^walker]; support-kind × strength (GUM + GRADE's start-by-kind-then-adjust is its exact shape)[^gum][^grade-domains]; witness-position for Accounts (hadith + historiography + testimony literature — with the reductionism/anti-reductionism fork marked as a real design *choice*, unresolved in the field itself)[^hadith][^historiography][^testimony]; Claim License (journalism's attribution ladder — more than the commonly-cited four rungs, with "not for attribution" and "unattributable" as distinct licenses — kept strictly separate from corroboration-count, "learned the hard way")[^journalism].

**At risk — where the survey pushes against our instincts:** any temptation to *rank* support-kinds (GUM explicitly refuses: Type A is not better than Type B, only differently produced)[^gum]; a single strength scalar (loses evidential weight — the credal-width argument, with SEP's ready-made coin example)[^walley]; "one status per atom" (per-clause state is live in the wild, and FRBR's four-grain WEMI stack shows identity itself has grain — same-claim-restated vs different-claim vs this-carrier *(FRBR leg: secondary-verified)*)[^frbr]; treating a well-formed provenance chain as trust (VIM NOTE 5: perfect traceability does not guarantee adequacy[^vim] — and PROV + nanopublications *both deliberately exclude* confidence from their schemas: the two most careful provenance lineages refused the conflation)[^prov][^nanopub].

**Missed — grounded additions the taxonomy should weigh:**
1. **The Standing track** (II.1) — three unrelated traditions converge on validity-of-origin as orthogonal to content-quality[^austin][^hohfeld][^ratio-obiter]; our Authority axis currently does both jobs. Also names a *commissive gap*: records committing the project to future action fit no current family cleanly (Searle's commissives)[^austin].
2. **Typed epistemic edges** with declared composability (II, edges)[^skos][^aif].
3. **Not-proven ≠ refuted** as distinct terminal states (L6)[^legal-proof].
4. **Recognized ignorance** as citable gap vocabulary + the reducibility split grounding ceilings (track 5)[^walker].
5. **Partial supersession** (Updates vs Obsoletes) — grain on the supersession edge[^ietf].
6. **EVI/EOL gap-triage** — the only principled *ordering* over open gaps found anywhere; the economics our pending-surfaces and Salience axis lack[^hubbard].
7. **Per-rung grader authority** (L8) — the independent-verify gate generalized into the ladder itself[^wikipedia-assess].
8. **Efficacy detection for Exposition** — the forcing-function debt has established instruments: misconception-keyed diagnostic distractors (FCI), normalized gain (contested but citable)[^fci], Miller's knows/shows/does split (knowledge tests are *measured poor proxies* for behavior — the founding datum)[^miller]. Exposition's instrument question has excellent external answers, exactly as the steward predicted.

## IV. Named gaps — where the survey found nothing (novel synthesis, labeled as such)

1. **No *surveyed* system grades "how far along" and "why not further" together** — TRL (pure readiness, no gap vocabulary)[^trl] and GRADE (pure gap vocabulary, no readiness)[^grade-domains] sit at opposite poles with nothing between found *in this survey*[^trl-grade-polarity]; the sweep itself names safety-case frameworks (GSN, ISO 26262 ASIL) as the unexamined territory where a counterexample most plausibly lives. Our State + ceiling + evidence-action combination is *invention until that follow-up runs*; build and label it that way.
2. **No first-class meta-uncertainty rung anywhere** — confidence-in-the-confidence-rating exists formally (KMM smooth ambiguity: a distribution over distributions) but operationally nowhere[^kmm]; nearest kin are NUSAP's pedigree[^nusap] and ICD-203's separate confidence axis[^icd203]. If we want it, we are inventing with precedent-adjacent grounding only.
3. **Downward revision is under-proceduralized *across this survey*** — every system describes arriving at a rating; almost none describes revising one downward later (the deliberate-practice literature's public self-downgrade is the one worked specimen found)[^deliberate]. Caveat before treating this as absence-in-the-world: the pedagogy sweep says outright that ten of its eleven systems were surfaced only in settled/canonical form — the sampling favored arrival-stories over revision-stories, and retraction vocabularies (COPE's "expression of concern") sit unexamined in §V. Our reset-on-edit + no-go cascade machinery may be ahead of the surveyed world here; whether it is ahead of *the* world needs the follow-up.
4. **"Suspected but unquantified bias" has no slot even in GUM** *(inference from secondaries' silence, flagged in its sweep)*[^gum-bias-gap] — a very common real state for knowledge-atoms, apparently unhomed in metrology's flagship.

## Working Notes

- The sweeps' own meta-finding deserves separate standing: even canonical standards are mostly *transmitted through unverified secondary paraphrase* (paywalls, 403s, binary PDFs defeated a diligent verification pass on GRADE, ICD-203, CEBM, NASA, CEFR, IFLA primaries) — the ambient world does not practice verified-vs-recalled discipline, which is an argument for ours being first-class, and a caution: several of this model's own legs stand on convergent-secondary rather than primary text, per-element as marked in the sweeps.[^meta-finding]
- Pedagogy sweep's structural observation, held as hypothesis: every two-axis system beat every single-axis ladder on usefulness, without exception in that sample — "established systems keep re-discovering that a second axis was hiding inside what looked like one dimension."[^two-axis-hypothesis]
- This model deliberately does not choose values for the Evidence axis's MECE table — that remains the steward-paced epistemology decision; what it contributes there: GRADE's start-by-kind-then-adjust shape, GUM's unranked support-kinds, the interval-width option, and L5's named-domain discipline as the candidate ingredients.

[^walley]: Walley's imprecise probability: a credal set / lower-upper envelope where **interval width is itself the evidential-weight signal**, with SEP's ready-made worked pair — a fair coin observed many times gives a precise P(H)={0.5}; a coin of unknown bias gives P(H)=[0,1] — identical point estimates, honestly different epistemic states. [^walker]: The Walker/Harremoës Level ladder — determinism → statistical uncertainty → **scenario uncertainty** (outcome space known, distribution not) → **recognized ignorance** (gap known, shape not) → total ignorance — crossed with the orthogonal **Nature** axis (epistemic/reducible vs aleatory/irreducible), which is what grounds *why* ceilings exist: epistemic gaps have no principled ceiling, aleatory gaps are the ceiling. [^hubbard]: Hubbard's EOL / EVI / EVPI as the only formal, computable *ordering* over which gap to close next — EOL = P(choice is wrong) × cost of being wrong; EVI = the expected reduction in EOL a proposed measurement would buy; EVPI = the unattainable upper bound that lets you rule out over-investing before spending anything. [^pollock]: Pollock's two structurally distinct defeat kinds — **rebutting** (a prima facie reason for the negation of the conclusion) vs **undercutting** (a reason to doubt that the premise supports the conclusion *in these circumstances*, with the premise possibly still true) — plus warrant defined relative to an evidence set rather than as an intrinsic per-claim property.
