# The reassembled model — what an empirical ladder/DAG looks like, as established across fields

*Coordinator synthesis (2026-08-10) from the ten sweep files beside this document, each read whole; per the delegation calibration, the sweeps are gathering instruments and this synthesis is coord judgment. Verification honesty inherited, not laundered: where a sweep marked a system's structure `recalled` rather than `verified`, elements leaning on it say so. Register: a proposal for the taxonomy discussion rounds — grounded, not settled; the working taxonomy should lose to this only where the grounding is genuinely stronger, and vice versa.*

*The steward's charge, verbatim: "the reassembled mental model of what an empirical ladder/DAG/DG looks like, as established in multiple fields, and in differences that our atom-kinds that we know of will care about." Tractability binds here (not in the sweeps): the model is eight laws and five tracks, not an encyclopedia.*

## I. The eight cross-field laws

Ordered by strength of independent convergence — each law names its convergent traditions; two same-lineage systems never counted as two.

**L1 — Kind gates vocabulary.** Mature systems fork by claim-type *before* applying any ladder: Oxford CEBM is a matrix whose rung-referent changes per question-type; IPCC assigns likelihood only to claims probability applies to, confidence otherwise; the trio system fixes strength by register. Three unrelated lineages (clinical epidemiology, climate assessment, this estate) independently: **not every claim gets every axis; the claim's type decides which epistemic vocabulary is even meaningful.** This is the external validation of the taxonomy's deepest premise ("honesty is only well-defined relative to kind") — the strongest single finding of the survey.

**L2 — Source-trust and content-credibility are two axes, everywhere.** Admiralty A–F × 1–6 (explicitly orthogonal by construction); hadith science's isnād (chain/narrators) vs matn (content) dual grading; historiography's external vs internal criticism; ICD-203's likelihood vs analyst-confidence with a mandated don't-combine-in-one-sentence rule. Four-plus independent traditions, including the two oldest in the survey. *(Historiography leg: recalled-grade in its sweep.)*

**L3 — Evidence-certainty and action-strength are separate and non-mechanically coupled.** GRADE: strong recommendations on low-certainty evidence and weak on high-certainty are both legitimate, expected cells; law: standard-of-proof (required confidence to act) is distinct from admissibility (may this evidence enter at all) and from what the evidence shows. Validates Assertions ⊥ Norms/Decisions and the fill's "authority composes with evidence, never substitutes."

**L4 — Uncertainty never lives in the lifecycle slot.** All six verified document-lifecycle systems (IETF, W3C, Wikidata, Wikipedia, + the two recalled) route confidence/gaps to a *different structural location* than the maturity/state token — implementation reports, references/qualifiers, reassessment processes. The lifecycle sweep's own strongest finding, by side-by-side convergence. Direct external confirmation of flags-not-gates and of keeping Evidence out of `state:`.

**L5 — Status is a projection of named, itemized reasons.** GRADE's eight signed domains (each rung-move attributable, auditable, directional — with *different evidentiary bars for up vs down*); NUSAP's pedigree matrix; Wikidata's deprecated-rank-requires-P2241-reason; IRT's misfit diagnostics. Confidence-with-attached-reasons beats confidence-as-number in every mature system that had to survive audit. Confirms the event-projection design — and GRADE adds what ours lacks: a *standard domain vocabulary* for the reasons.

**L6 — Null and terminal states are first-class and off-ladder.** "Cannot be judged" ≠ worst rung (Admiralty F/6, structurally separate from E/5); *not-proven ≠ proven-false* (legal, with different downstream consequences); Wikidata's `normal` = no-judgment, deliberately not "pending"; mawḍūʿ (fabricated) as a hard terminal off the hadith ladder, not its bottom rung. Validates our off-ladder honesty states and the rejected/superseded terminals — and adds the not-proven/refuted distinction our Questions and Prohibitions boundary needs.

**L7 — Supersession is append-only, typed, and pointed.** ADR's superseded-with-link ("still relevant to know it *was* the decision"); PROV's wasRevisionOf / wasInvalidatedBy / alternateOf as three *different* supersession kinds; Wikidata retains deprecated statements ("verifiable that someone claimed it, whether or not true"); IETF's **Obsoletes vs Updates** — wholesale vs *partial* supersession as typed graph edges; residue's replace-with. Our design is confirmed and extended: supersession wants *grain* (partial vs whole) and *kind* (revised vs invalidated vs alternate), not one flag.

**L8 — Top rungs are event-gated and grader-escalated.** TRL 9 is unforgeable (the flight happened or it didn't); proof-assistant kernel-checking is a verification *event*, with gaps (`sorry`) enumerated and auto-propagated rather than graded; Wikipedia's three governance tiers (self-assess → 2-editor consensus → formal independent review) escalate *who may assign* a rung with the rung. Grounds our Max/ceiling discipline and adds a design element ours lacks: **per-rung grader-authority requirements** (kin to the independent-verify gate, made structural).

## II. The five state-tracks (the tractable per-record architecture)

What the laws assemble into — five separable tracks, each with its established backer. A record carries the tracks its kind declares (L1); no field ever does two tracks' jobs.

| Track | Question | Grounded shape | Stolen from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Standing** *(new — see III)* | Did this validly come into being as its declared kind? | A gate, not a ladder: misfire = the record never existed *as that kind* (unauthorized decision, broken chain of custody); distinct from content being wrong | Austin's misfire/abuse; Hohfeld's power/liability; Daubert admissibility; chain-of-custody |
| **2. Evidence** | How strongly held, on what support? | Support-kind (declared, **unranked** — GUM's Type A/B refuses hierarchy) × strength (rungs moved only by named signed domains); interval-width where evidential weight matters (same point estimate, different backing — Walley's fair-coin/unknown-coin) | GUM; GRADE; imprecise probability; trio system |
| **3. Force** | Who stands behind it, how bindingly? | Decided-by ladder projected from events; ceiling derived from ownership/standing; composes with track 2, never substitutes (L3) | GRADE's recommendation axis; ADR statuses; legal precedent (binding/persuasive × hierarchy-position) |
| **4. Lifecycle** | Where in the process, superseded by what? | Resettable flags + typed, pointed supersession edges (partial vs whole); default-expiry beats default-limbo (IETF drafts die in 6 months absent renewal); non-monotonic (W3C loops backward) | IETF; W3C; ADR; PROV revision vocabulary |
| **5. Gap declaration** | What don't we know, and is it closable? | The Walker/Harremoës ladder as vocabulary: quantified spread → scenario (range known, distribution not) → **recognized ignorance** (gap known, shape not) — with *total ignorance assignable only by an external auditor, never self-reported* (structurally impossible); plus reducibility: epistemic gaps have no principled ceiling, aleatory gaps ARE the ceiling | Walker/Harremoës; NUSAP; GUM's declared-escape-hatch pattern; Hubbard's EVI for *which gap next* |

**Edges are the sixth carrier.** The formal-epistemology sweep's deepest finding: past Bayesian credence, no rich system grades one claim in isolation — the load-bearing state is *relational* (Pollock's rebut vs undercut; Dung's acceptance as a graph-fixpoint; SKOS's relation-kind-IS-the-confidence with the compound-error transitivity rationale; AIF's inference/conflict/preference edge types). The grounded move for us: **type the `depends:`/reference edges** (supports / rebuts / undercuts / derives-from / revises / alternate-of) and declare each type's composability — the DAG carries as much epistemic state as the nodes.

## III. What our organic taxonomy got right, wrong, and missed

**Right — independently convergent with established practice** (adherence-weight aside, these now have evidence-weight): kind-gates-axes (L1); Evidence ⊥ Authority (L2/L3); flags-not-gates (L4); event-projection (L5); off-ladder honesty states + pointed terminals (L6/L7); Max/ceiling (L8 + reducibility grounds *why* ceilings exist); support-kind × strength (GUM + GRADE's start-by-kind-then-adjust is its exact shape); witness-position for Accounts (hadith + historiography + testimony literature — with the reductionism/anti-reductionism fork marked as a real design *choice*, unresolved in the field itself); Claim License (journalism's attribution ladder — six rungs, not four — kept strictly separate from corroboration-count, "learned the hard way").

**At risk — where the survey pushes against our instincts:** any temptation to *rank* support-kinds (GUM explicitly refuses: Type A is not better than Type B, only differently produced); a single strength scalar (loses evidential weight — the credal-width argument, with SEP's ready-made coin example); "one status per atom" (per-clause state is live in the wild, and FRBR's four-grain WEMI stack shows identity itself has grain — same-claim-restated vs different-claim vs this-carrier *(FRBR leg: secondary-verified)*); treating a well-formed provenance chain as trust (VIM NOTE 5: perfect traceability does not guarantee adequacy — and PROV + nanopublications *both deliberately exclude* confidence from their schemas: the two most careful provenance lineages refused the conflation).

**Missed — grounded additions the taxonomy should weigh:**
1. **The Standing track** (II.1) — three unrelated traditions converge on validity-of-origin as orthogonal to content-quality; our Authority axis currently does both jobs. Also names a *commissive gap*: records committing the project to future action fit no current family cleanly (Searle's commissives).
2. **Typed epistemic edges** with declared composability (II, edges).
3. **Not-proven ≠ refuted** as distinct terminal states (L6).
4. **Recognized ignorance** as citable gap vocabulary + the reducibility split grounding ceilings (track 5).
5. **Partial supersession** (Updates vs Obsoletes) — grain on the supersession edge.
6. **EVI/EOL gap-triage** — the only principled *ordering* over open gaps found anywhere; the economics our pending-surfaces and Salience axis lack.
7. **Per-rung grader authority** (L8) — the independent-verify gate generalized into the ladder itself.
8. **Efficacy detection for Exposition** — the forcing-function debt has established instruments: misconception-keyed diagnostic distractors (FCI), normalized gain (contested but citable), Miller's knows/shows/does split (knowledge tests are *measured poor proxies* for behavior — the founding datum). Exposition's instrument question has excellent external answers, exactly as the steward predicted.

## IV. Named gaps — where the survey found nothing (novel synthesis, labeled as such)

1. **No system grades "how far along" and "why not further" together** — TRL (pure readiness, no gap vocabulary) and GRADE (pure gap vocabulary, no readiness) sit at opposite poles with nothing between. Our State + ceiling + evidence-action combination is *invention*, not adaptation; build and label it that way.
2. **No first-class meta-uncertainty rung anywhere** — confidence-in-the-confidence-rating exists formally (KMM smooth ambiguity: a distribution over distributions) but operationally nowhere; nearest kin are NUSAP's pedigree and ICD-203's separate confidence axis. If we want it, we are inventing with precedent-adjacent grounding only.
3. **Downward revision is under-proceduralized everywhere** — every system describes arriving at a rating; almost none describes revising one downward later (the deliberate-practice literature's public self-downgrade is the one worked specimen found). Our reset-on-edit + no-go cascade machinery may be genuinely ahead of the surveyed world here.
4. **"Suspected but unquantified bias" has no slot even in GUM** *(inference from secondaries' silence, flagged in its sweep)* — a very common real state for knowledge-atoms, apparently unhomed in metrology's flagship.
