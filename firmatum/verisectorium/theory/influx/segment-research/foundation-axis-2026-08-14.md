## What the sketch is

## The salient model (proposed)

**1. The sketch is the missing Evidence-analog for the choice register — a support-kind scale for *choices*, not a strength ladder for claims.** The current map's ② says "choices aren't truth-apt; decided is the ceiling by construction" — true about *truth*, but it leaves choices carrying only Authority. Yet the estate constantly makes groundedness judgments about choices: "principled vs unprincipled," "instances are inertia, not verdicts," vestiges, and FILL-v1's FM?/Instr? working columns (✓/~/✗) — which are exactly a crude ternary projection of this scale. The sketch names the full gradient and its own captions carry the composition discipline: foundation predicts *probability of being right* ("may still be true, but least certainty") — it never decides truth or adoption. So the three-way composition extends the fill's existing rule:

- **Foundation** governs expected survival / prior probability (per the sketch),
- **Authority** governs adoption (decided-by ladder),
- **Evidence** governs truth, where truth applies.

A `ruled` choice can still be cargo-cult-founded; a first-principles choice can sit unratified. Both cells are informative, and today only one of them is representable.

**2. Foundation is support-kind, generalized — and the sketch and the trio agree more than they appear to.** The trio's cut holds that support-kinds have no total order up high (conditional ⊥⊥ robust-qualitative). The sketch draws an ordering — but *by projection onto certainty/probability*, and it draws the non-linearity itself: the visible branch/funnel after Informed Hypothetical, with the degenerate tail (speculative / random / cargo-cult) hanging off-row. Candidate reconciliation for [[disc-ladder-reconciliation]]: **support-kind is a partial order whose projection onto prior-probability is what the sketch depicts** — and the sketch extends the vocabulary *below* the trio's floor (analogy, informed-hypothetical, speculative, random, cargo-cult), which is where design work actually lives. The trio never needed those rungs because it was built for claims; the choice register needs them daily.

**3. "Constitutive (e.g. speech act)" is a genuinely new rung that speaks to E1 (the def straddle).** True-by-declaration is a support-kind neither the trio cut nor the fill names. A definition's *coinage* is constitutive-grade foundation (its "truth" is made by the declaring act, so Authority is its natural adjudicator); its *fit-to-usage* is empirical-grade. The straddle stops being an anomaly: it's one kind whose two halves sit at two rungs of the same Foundation scale, each pulling in the adjudicator native to that rung. That is a point in favor of "two axes on one kind" over "two kinds" — and suggests the deeper rule: **which adjudication instrument applies is a function of foundation rung** (constitutive → authority; empirical → measurement; mathematical → re-derivation; analogical → the convergence/independence discipline).

**4. Sensibility-to-truth is the process-side trajectory, not another record-side ladder.** Its rungs (Pure Guess → Pattern Match → Hypothesis → Tested → Proven → Fundamental Truth) mix support-kind and strength because it is a *path through* the 2-D space walked by one generating mind, in-flight — "always know where you are on this ladder" is its keepable core, and that is register-marking discipline (Process State's territory), not a status enum. Its anti-patterns (Sensibility Worship / Premature Certainty / Truth Paralysis) are already doctrine elsewhere. Recommend: mine it for the Process-State discussion and the drafting-register SOPs; do not add its ladder to the reconciliation set as a competitor — it's a different object.

**5. The bottom notes give Practices a second candidate axis: inculcation depth.** Conscious → Habitual → Reflexive → Instinctive is orthogonal to both Foundation and Efficacy: it measures how deeply a practice is internalized, not whether it's well-founded or whether it fires. The dangerous cell the crossing exposes: *instinctive × unprincipled* (trained defaults — the estate's most-documented failure class); the aspiration cell: *instinctive × axiomatic*. The "logocentric self-governed meta-loop … progressive inculcation at various cycle frequencies" note is the mechanism claim: a language-constituted corpus can deliberately move practices along this axis at chosen cycle frequencies — which is what the sop-influx channel, clocked drains, and comproprium's "performance becomes constitutive" doctrine already do in practice. The March sketch states the design intent for it.
