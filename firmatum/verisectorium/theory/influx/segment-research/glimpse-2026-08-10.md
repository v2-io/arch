**The most worked-out system in the influx** is the trio-ratified three-axis cut (`instrumenta/bridges/method-evidence-tiers.md`, read whole today): support-kind (six, each defined by its *repair*) × strength (seven rungs, explicitly not a total ranking) × register (derived/evidenced/decided/proposed — only the truth-apt two take strength; **register fixes strength at two of four values**), plus the convergent lock (failure-mode-independent kinds only) and the transmission lock (source-tier by reference + carriage audited as events). **Prior coord notes exist** (`../segment-research-notes-2026-08-09.md`): the Organ IV/VII live-source reading ledger (spikes/routing/de-novo SOPs, PROCESS-MAP, decision-routing findings read whole) with per-segment drafting synthesis. The dive should extend its reading ledger, not duplicate it.

## What the influx does NOT yet hold (the dive targets)

1. **Newest material postdating the gather:** our own theory segments + template (this repo), and the udon paths deployment's `def/` entries (`../udon/v2/paths/**`, .ud/.un dialects) — the evolving working deployment that seeded the template.
2. **Comproprium's kinds at first hand** — the gather carries scatter-maps and format-divergence notes, but the precept/practice/exemplum/verum anatomy (frontmatter, sections, registers) needs specimen-grade verification at the live tree.
3. **Anatomy-grade specimens generally:** the spectrum names kinds; almost nowhere does the influx carry a *verbatim exemplar with its frontmatter* per kind. That is the specimen grain the taxonomy needs.
4. **Root-type files as a class** — decision logs, navigators, history layers, notation files, valves: attested by name, never surveyed as a population (which projects have which, in what dialect, with what registers).
