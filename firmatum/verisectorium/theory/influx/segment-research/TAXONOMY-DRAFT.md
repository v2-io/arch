## The problem this solves

Under total turnover ( [[post-total-turnover]]), the theory's core promise is *epistemic state carried honestly per atom* — and honesty is only well-defined relative to **kind**: which state-axes apply, on which ladders, adjudicated by which instrument, changeable by whom. This taxonomy is **the type system for veritas**.

Its consumers are concrete: [[def-epistemic-axes]] (Organ III's ground), [[form-instantiation-kit]] + [[form-kit-questionnaire]] (per-deployment kind declaration), [[disc-ladder-reconciliation]] (variation is intrinsic iff it traces to kind), [[form-slug-form-kinds]] (what may ride in identity), and every agent choosing a writing register.

**Scope: record kinds, not only segment kinds** — the root-file population (ledgers, navigators, charters, adversarial reviews) carries the ladder wherever segments don't.

## Principles (steward-set)

1. **Instances are inertia, not verdicts** (2026-08-10): *"Every single instance, no matter how rigorous and authoritative it sounds or how closely it is adhered to, is nothing more than an evolved/evolving guess with inertia and momentum."* The survey supplies a distribution of kinds and the beginnings of orthogonal state/status/max concerns — never a taxonomy by counting. Adherence-weight is not evidence-weight; the *discussions* that produced an instance's apparatus outrank its settled artifacts.

2. **The de-novo-auditor criterion** (2026-08-10): another way to see the hopeful taxonomy — *"is it immediately obvious to a de-novo auditor, given nothing but the outlines and segments, exactly what things should be audited?"* Where that fails (or a practical equivalent of it), treat the failure as evidence the taxonomy is missing something — kind + carried state should make audit-targets self-evident, without tribal knowledge, for a fresh mind.
