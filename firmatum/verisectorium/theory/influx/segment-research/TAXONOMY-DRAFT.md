# Record-kind taxonomy — working draft

## Guiding Context

1. **Scope**: *Record kinds, not only segment kinds* — the root-file population (ledgers, navigators, charters, adversarial reviews) carries the ladder wherever segments don't.
2. **Discipline**: This draft (opened 2026-08-10) carries *only what has been discussed and that is solid enough*. Proposals wait in [[pre-taxonomy-synthesis]] (which was fed by the [[synthesis-v0-2026-08-10|survey synthesis]] which includes the four dive maps + coord-browsed primaries and was, in turn, fed by other influx documents and originals).
3. **Principle**: Under total turnover ( [[post-total-turnover]]), the theory's core promise is *epistemic state carried honestly per atom* — and honesty is only well-defined relative to **kind**: which state-axes apply, on which ladders, adjudicated by which instrument, changeable by whom. This taxonomy is **the type system for veritas**.
4. **Down-stream**: This work's consumers are concrete: [[def-epistemic-axes]] (Organ III's ground), [[form-instantiation-kit]] + [[form-kit-questionnaire]] (per-deployment kind declaration), [[disc-ladder-reconciliation]] (variation is intrinsic iff it traces to kind), [[form-slug-form-kinds]] (what may ride in identity), and every agent choosing a writing register.
5. **Survey Discipline**: *Instances are inertia, not verdicts*: *"Every single instance, no matter how rigorous and authoritative it sounds or how closely it is adhered to, is nothing more than an evolved/evolving guess with inertia and momentum."* -JAW. The survey supplies a distribution of kinds and the beginnings of orthogonal state/status/max concerns — never a taxonomy by counting. Adherence-weight is not evidence-weight; the *discussions* that produced an instance's apparatus outrank its settled artifacts.
6. **De-Novo-Auditor Criterion**: Another way to see the hopeful taxonomy — *"is it immediately obvious to a de-novo auditor, given nothing but the outlines and segments, exactly what things should be audited?"*-JAW. Where that fails (or a practical equivalent of it), treat the failure as evidence the taxonomy is missing something — kind + carried state should make audit-targets self-evident, without tribal knowledge, for a fresh mind.
7. **Revisability**:  This is a 'working draft.' As we lay down this guiding context and outline of the taxonomy, we don't know what shape it might have eventually. We'll do the best to incorporate our most principled thoughts, but it is a living document as of 2026-08-10 and likely for a few days after.
