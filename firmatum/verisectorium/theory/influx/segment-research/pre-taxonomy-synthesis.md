## 0. The problem this solves

Under total turnover ( [[post-total-turnover]]), the theory's core promise is *epistemic state carried honestly per atom* — and honesty is only well-defined relative to **kind**: which state-axes apply, on which ladders, adjudicated by which instrument, changeable by whom. This taxonomy is **the type system for veritas**. Its consumers: [[def-epistemic-axes]] (Organ III's ground), [[form-instantiation-kit]] + [[form-kit-questionnaire]] (per-deployment kind declaration), [[disc-ladder-reconciliation]] (variation is intrinsic iff it traces to kind), [[form-slug-form-kinds]] (what may ride in identity), and every agent choosing a writing register.

**Scope: record kinds, not only segment kinds** — the root-file population (ledgers, navigators, charters, adversarial reviews) carries the ladder wherever segments don't.

**Register discipline (steward, 2026-08-10):** every wild instance is an evolved/evolving guess with inertia; the survey supplies a distribution, never a verdict. Axes are the stable convergent layer (support-kinds reinvented independently; the event layer four generations old); kind lists are the volatile layer.

## A. The kind-individuation heuristic *(hypothesis — re-registered 2026-08-10 after steward challenge)*

**The question worth asking** when a kind split or merge is proposed: *do the two fail differently, and are they repaired/adjudicated differently?* Difference-in-repair is good grounds for distinguishing two kinds — this much has two same-estate design-register precedents (comproprium FORMAT's kind separation; the trio evidence-tiers "anti-collapse test": two support-kinds are worth distinguishing only when they route to different repairs). Same-author design conventions: coherence, not corroboration.

**Retraction on record:** the first fill stated this as a "generative law" — *distinct iff failure mode + adjudication instrument differ* — with "must survive stress tests" framing. The "iff" was this session's addition, claimed by neither source: the sources support *sufficiency* (different repairs → worth distinguishing); the invented *necessity* half licensed "no instrument → not a kind" and deleted the Exposition family — the estate's largest by count — until the steward caught it. The failure anatomy is banked at `../../sop/influx/survey-census-and-law-filter-2026-08-10.md`. **Redeemed form (steward, 2026-08-10): a forcing function for generative deliberation.** Same blade, opposite grip: for every candidate kind, *force* the discussion — "what **should** its failure mode be? what **should** adjudicate and repair it?" — and hold the kind open until the discussion produces answers, never concluding anything from the question being unanswered in the wild. Under this grip the heuristic is "the answer to the survey being just a survey of organic attempts that we use to define the principled taxonomy" (steward's words) — the step that converts inventory into principle. The disposition rule that travels with it: a kind whose concerns/instruments aren't principled yet is declared as *exactly that* — a loud named gap ("can't fill yet; probably more than half the result") — which is a finding, not a failure; a gap declaration needs no argument, and an exclusion that argues is the tell of a gap in costume.
