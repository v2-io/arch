## 3. Historical spine (why the conflations exist — steward, 2026-08-10)

asf's slug-prefix decision months ago was made in deliberate discussion (naming.sop/principles: prefixes mechanical from `type:`, validated by pilot) — and the recent realization (here, [[form-slug-form-kinds]]) is that the `type:` enum it keys on conflates **three** families, not two ✦: stable form-kinds (postulate/definition/scope), mutable trajectory-kinds (hypothesis/derived/result), and exposition-weight kinds (aside/sketch/detail/worked-example). Every instance's apparatus is an evolved guess with inertia; the *discussions* that produced them (asf's naming cycles, the trio epistemology sessions, comproprium's FORMAT divergence arguments D2/D4/D5) are higher-signal than the settled artifacts — they record the considered alternatives.

## 4. Leading generative principle (candidate, not settled)

Comproprium's organizing law, generalized: **kinds separate where failure mode and adjudication instrument separate** (two kinds are distinct only if they fail differently and are repaired/adjudicated differently — the two-repairs test, already used by the trio system's support-kinds and comproprium's FORMAT). Every family in §1's third column is this principle read backwards. If it holds, the taxonomy becomes *derivable* (a new kind earns its place by exhibiting a new failure-mode/instrument pair) instead of enumerated. The test it must survive: the def-kind straddle (§1 vocabulary row) and the exposition family (which has no adjudication instrument at all — is "none, by declaration" an instrument?).

- Which of §2's eighteen axes are per-kind vs universal (grain, freshness, and process-state look universal; efficacy and witness-position look kind-specific — is axis-applicability itself what defines a family?)
