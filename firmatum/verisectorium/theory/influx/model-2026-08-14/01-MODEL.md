# The generative model: adjudication currencies, and the nine questions that derive a kind

*Register: proposed (see 00-README). The claim this file makes about itself: a founding agent who runs §2's procedure, drawing values from §1's spine and §3's universal machinery, derives their instance's kinds, tracked state, movement rules, and certifiable surface — rather than inheriting any prior instance's evolved guess. Per the steward's method ruling: the wild population is a completeness backstop for the output of this procedure, never a filter on what it may generate.*

---

## §0. The three commitments underneath everything

Three findings recur so consistently — internally across pressure-diverse instances, and externally across unrelated mature traditions — that the whole model is built on them:

1. **Kind gates vocabulary.** Not every record gets every state-axis; the record's *kind* decides which epistemic vocabulary is even meaningful for it. (Externally: CEBM's question-type matrix, IPCC's confidence/likelihood gating, GRADE's per-outcome binding — three unrelated lineages. Internally: the trio system's register-fixes-strength; ennaos VERA's `claim_type ⊥ epistemic_status`, designed nine months earlier under different pressure.) *Honesty is only well-defined relative to kind* — this is the taxonomy's deepest premise, and it survived every collision in the ingest.

2. **Status is a projection of named, itemized events.** No status cell is hand-set; every value is the projection of an append-only trail — who did what, against which criterion, when, with what outcome — and rung-movements cite named channels a reader can check. (Externally: GRADE's eight signed domains, Wikidata's deprecation-reason qualifier, ADR supersession. Internally: shipped four generations deep — neurips/refs verifications → terminology decision-events → udon-needs `verified:` lists → relata; VERA's "the graph records the results of reasoning, it does not perform the reasoning.")

3. **Kinds individuate by failure-and-repair.** Two record-kinds earn separation exactly when they *fail differently and route to different repairs* — held as the forcing function, not as an iff: for every candidate kind, force the questions "what SHOULD fail here? what SHOULD adjudicate and repair it?" and hold the kind open until answered from first principles. Silence in the wild is a to-do, never a verdict. (The steward-redeemed form of the retracted "generative law"; the exposition-family deletion is the recorded failure of the iff-form.)

## §1. The spine: adjudication currencies

**The synthesis this model adds** *(mine — proposed)*: the answer to "what SHOULD adjudicate this kind?" always lands in one of a small set of **adjudication currencies** — what a record's strength is *denominated in*, what its ladder measures, and what moves it. A kind's currency is the most consequential fact about it; nearly everything else (write semantics, ladder shape, movers, terminals) follows from the currency plus the universal machinery of §3.

| Currency | Speech-act it prices | The record fails by | Strength is denominated in | Ladder shape & movers | Write semantics that follow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Evidence** | *asserts* — a truth-apt claim about the world | being false or overclaimed | support-kind (declared, **unranked**) × strength rung | rungs moved only by **named signed channels** (GRADE-shaped); convergent lock arms only across failure-mode-independent kinds; settled ultimately by re-derivation at the primary | replacement (present truth; delete-test; no-go protocol) |
| **Backing** | *decides* — a choice among live alternatives (conventions, formulations, rulings, coinages) | silent overturn; drift-from; masquerading as a derivation | **decided-by** (who stands behind it, how) × **groundedness** (what the choice was derived *from* — see below) | authority rungs projected from decision events (proposed → supported → ratified → ruled; terminals rejected / superseded; honesty states defacto / transition); groundedness assessed, not climbed | append-or-expressly-overturn ledger; the projected cell is a denorm |
| **Fidelity** | *records* — an account of what happened | infidelity to the primary record | witness position (attested / reconstructed / secondhand) × coverage (+ date-confidence) | proximity cannot be improved by work — only by finding a closer witness; corroboration can *upgrade across* records (ṣaḥīḥ-li-ghayrihi-shaped), never within one | **append-only**; corrections chain beneath; in-place repair only for errors about the record itself |
| **Efficacy** | *prescribes-at-a-moment* — a practice, probe, or trigger | not firing when its moment comes | demonstrated firing — with **fired-for-author ≠ fired-for-inheritor** as different events, and only the second being what a corpus is for | event-accumulating (proposed → fired-once → fired; honesty state failed-to-fire; terminal retired); endorsement moves *authority on the text*, never this axis | text replaceable; firing trail append-only |
| **Disposition** | *asks / awaits* — a question, demand, pending item | re-billing or silent loss | progress toward a **terminal state** (answered / rejected-with-reopen-condition / superseded), with who-may-close declared | lifecycle, not strength; every open item names its closer-kind (steward-fact / demand / agent-open) | queue semantics + clocked drain; asked-and-answered is recorded |
| **Currency** (freshness) | *points* — a reference, index, notation entry, tracker row | dangling; staleness; the label-lie | a **decaying claim of currency**, in two forms: *own*-currency (the thing I describe still works this way) and *referent*-currency (the atoms I speak about still say what I say they say) | reset by change in the referent; never ratchets; conflict-precedence declared ("on disagreement, I lose") | regenerate-or-reverify; generated views declared as derived, never hand-edited |

Two structural notes on the spine:

- **Composite kinds are currency-compositions, not new currencies.** A *definition* is a coinage (Backing) plus fit-to-usage (Evidence) — the def-straddle dissolves rather than being forced to one side. A *prohibition* is an assertion-of-refutedness (Evidence) plus a **usage license** on consumers (allowed-as-hypothesis / do-not-resurrect / forbidden). *Exposition* is the outward dual: near-zero Evidence of its own by declared **claim license** (none / summarize / generalize / original), priced almost entirely in referent-Currency plus a mis-teaching failure mode whose instruments exist (experiential reading as the causal-order verifier; misconception-keyed diagnostics; Miller's knows≠does as the external warrant that reading-comprehension proxies behavior poorly). An *instrument/tracker* (navigator, manifest, valve, process map) is priced in Currency against the population it tracks, with its opening **charter sentence** (append-only / refresh-in-place / lagging-not-arbiter) as the root-file analog of frontmatter.
- **Foundation is one general coordinate across registers** *(A1, adopted from the quarantined foundation-axis file's E6)*: "what grounds it" instantiates as *support-kind* on assertions (the trio's axis) and as the sketch's *groundedness scale* on choices — one coordinate, two register-instantiations, unranked-by-kind in both, with an ordering only under projection onto prior-probability. The FILL's FM?/Instr? working columns are its crude ternary. The sketch's contribution is extending the vocabulary *below* the trio's floor (analogy, informed-hypothetical, speculative, cargo-cult) — the rungs where design work actually lives.
- **Groundedness is the coordinate the estate's Authority register was missing** *(carried from the steward's March-2026 sketch; placement mine)*. The Design/Architectural Epistemic Foundation Scale — axiomatic/first-principles → mathematically grounded → constitutive → empirical/emergent → well-modeled (instance) → model-grounded-in-analogy → informed-hypothetical ⤳ speculative → random → cargo-cult — grades *what a decided thing was derived from*, orthogonally to *who stands behind it*: a `ruled` decision can be cargo-cult-grounded, a `proposed` one first-principles-grounded, and the two must not be conflated (that conflation is exactly how authority launders groundedness). "May still be true, but least certainty" (the sketch's own annotation) marks the low end as honest rather than forbidden. This is also the discipline's answer to "a convention honestly stated is a complete answer": the statement *is* the groundedness declaration.

## §2. The derivation: nine questions that generate a kind

*(The procedure a founding agent — or the kit questionnaire — runs per record-kind the instance will hold. Extends the kind-declaration schema of `../segment-research/pre-taxonomy-synthesis.md` §B with the standing, movers/warrant, groundedness, and certification questions, and puts the questions in derivation order. Q0 restored per 03-ADVERSARIAL-READ A3.)*

0. **Standing.** What must be true for a record of this kind to *validly come into being at all* — who has the power to produce one, through what act, with what chain-integrity? A gate, never a rung: a "decision" recorded by someone without the power to decide is not a weak decision; it never existed as one (Austin's misfire; Hohfeld's power/liability; chain-of-custody). Misfire ≠ wrong-content.
1. **Speech-act.** What does a record of this kind *do* — assert, decide, record, prescribe, ask, point, teach, track? (Composites answer with more than one.)
2. **Failure.** What SHOULD go wrong with one? Forced from first principles; the wild corpus checks completeness of your answer, never bounds it.
3. **Instrument.** What SHOULD adjudicate and repair that failure? Half of this answer is derivable from the record's foundation rung (A2, instrument-follows-rung): grounded in proof → re-derive it; in declaration → get it ratified; in reality → measure it; in a model → check the instance; in analogy → break the analogy; a practice, any ground → watch it fire. A declared-∅ instrument is legitimate for records whose value is that nobody repairs them (sketches, raw reflections) — but declared intent ("this is a sketch; treat accordingly") is the enforceable floor, and principled-∅ must never be conflated with nobody-has-asked-yet.
4. **Currency (⇒ tracks).** Which §1 currency (or composition) denominates strength? This yields the kind's state-tracks by construction, and §1's write-semantics column follows from the kind's truth-conditions — never from style.
5. **Movers & warrant.** Who may move each value, on what warrant, recorded how? Every movement is an event (subject · act · criterion · actor+authority · date · outcome · revisit-when?); a status assigned from a summary is a proxy and is marked as one; top rungs are **event-gated and grader-escalated** (self-assess → independent review → formal, per stakes). And the ledger keeps **two columns, never one grade** (A4): *what the act showed* and *how trusted its performer/channel is* — the split every long-surviving tradition made (Admiralty's reliability × credibility; hadith's isnād vs matn); fusing them is the halo-effect failure by construction.
6. **Ceiling & evidence-action.** Per laddered axis: the strongest value this kind can reach — assignable from kind before work — and *the named action that would raise a given record toward it* (the strengthen-first to-do generator). Transmitted ceilings cap at the source's own tier; authority ceilings derive from ownership; reducibility grounds why any ceiling exists at all (epistemic gaps have no principled ceiling; aleatory gaps are the ceiling).
7. **Grain & edges.** At what depth does state attach (record / section / clause / edge), and what is the projection rule upward? Per-clause status, per-clause deciders, and scoped ceilings (`exact (sub-scope); robust-qualitative generally`) are live practice, not exotica; edges carry state of their own (typed supersession — revised / invalidated / alternate, partial vs whole; transmitted-tier by reference; relation-kind-as-confidence where chaining would compound error).
8. **Certification.** What may a consumer conclude from this kind's state, checkable by a de-novo auditor from the record + its events alone? The acceptance test for the whole declaration is the steward's: *"is it immediately obvious to a de-novo auditor, given nothing but the outlines and segments, exactly what things should be audited?"* Where that fails, the kind-declaration is missing something.

## §3. Universal machinery (every kind, regardless of currency)

These are not per-kind choices; they are the constants the derivation assumes:

- **Identity is stable and mutability stays out of it.** Slug = identity; ordering, numbering, and location are view/layout facts; slug prefixes carry form-kind only, never trajectory position (honest updating must never force a rename). Identity itself has grain (same-claim-restated vs different-claim vs this-carrier — restatement families are linked, not deduped on sentiment).
- **The event trail is the substrate; cells are projections.** (§0.2.) Includes the `deliberately-corrected-away` event — the regression guard against re-landing the cleaner-looking wrong form.
- **Process-state is flags, not a ladder.** Independent resettable checks, reset-on-edit; gates only at genuine destinations (emission seams); updating a well-verified record must feel like advancement. Lifecycle is non-monotone; default-expiry beats default-limbo for drafts and queues.
- **Absence and conflict are asymmetric instruments.** Absence (bare gap, unrun check, empty search) is never refutation and never success — it is a named to-do, and absence claims carry their search; *conflict* (present-truth collision, support collision, outline-vs-frontmatter disagreement) must surface and block "done." Not-proven ≠ refuted, as distinct terminals. Instruments distinguish empty / failed / unrun.
- **Views select and project; the population is presentation-neutral.** One canon view declared among many; view rows are denorms except the view's own perspective column; generated views declared derived.
- **Material crosses membranes as recorded events**, with typed outcomes (rejected ≠ needs-review ≠ skipped ≠ landed); leaving a live surface passes the delete-test; dispatch compounds and half-dispatch breeds false nuggets.
- **Every process ships with its amendment channel** (participant feedback as the meta-process's only source/sink), and every process is born clocked (trigger + drain).

## §4. Certification, precisely

*(Synthesis, from L8 + the estate's gates + the de-novo-auditor criterion.)* A **certification** is a claim *about a record* that is checkable without trusting the record's author: a named criterion, a named grader whose authority meets the rung's requirement, a dated event, and a reproducible check (re-derivation, re-run, quiz, lint, span-locate). What an instance "can then certify" is exactly the set of such claims its declared kinds + event trails support — no more. Three consequences:

1. **Certification-strength is bounded by grader-independence**, not effort: self-assessed rungs certify authorship diligence; independent-reviewed rungs certify the claim; the independent-verify gate is load-bearing precisely because the conviction that it is unneeded is the failure it catches.
2. **A certificate decays with its warrant**: reset-on-edit for content checks, referent-change for currency checks, era-boundaries for measured numbers. A certificate that cannot decay is a label-lie in waiting.
3. **Chain-integrity is not fitness** (VIM NOTE 5): a perfect provenance chain certifies lineage, never adequacy — the two are separate certifications and must not share a cell.

## §5. The meta-loop: progressive inculcation

*(Carried from the sketch's lower half + the crystallized-process thesis + comproprium's praxis machinery; assembly mine.)* The logocentric estate gives a **self-governed meta-loop**: disciplines migrate down an inculcation ladder — **conscious** (prose precept, read each time) → **habitual** (probe/trigger that fires at the moment) → **reflexive** (mechanism: gate, lint, hook, generated view) → **instinctive** (substrate/tooling where the wrong act is no longer expressible) — at *various cycle frequencies*, with each migration event recorded (comproprium's "caught disciplines become mechanisms"; the repair-shape ordering: make-the-true-thing-cheaper > acts-that-leave-traces > probes > resolve). The kit's obligation is to make each migration cheap and its current rung *declared*, because a discipline's rung on this ladder is the honest statement of how much it can be relied on under turnover — prose reliably fails its author within the hour; mechanisms don't.

## §6. Composites: the closure rule

*(Added 2026-08-14 from steward dialog; the shapes were latent across §3 but the rule was not stated.)* The ontology does not grow richer to absorb composite or ill-behaved material; it **closes over composites with four declared shapes**, and its worst recorded failures are the undeclared fifth:

1. **Reify → a new atom.** When substance lives *between* records — a cross-segment synthesis, a pattern over instances, a compression-interface claim over a cluster or cycle — mint an atom that takes the underlyings as `depends:` and carries the composite claim itself; the nine questions then apply to it unchanged. (VERA's compression boundaries are the worked deep case: a stable interface claim over an internally-reorganizable subgraph, with a stability threshold governing when internal change escapes.)
2. **View-ify → no warrant of its own.** Outlines, digests, lexicons, narrative chains: select, project, order, carry perspective and edge-facts — "it may reference and order; it may not introduce." A view's entire epistemic state is Currency against its population, plus its declared conflict-precedence ("on disagreement, I lose").
3. **Cluster → typed parts under one identity.** Body / working notes / events / companions, each with its own write semantics — composite inside, one atom outside; the part-typing is what keeps different truth-conditions from bleeding.
4. **Exempt → visibly.** Non-conformant material (legacy `old-*`, archives, ingest staging) is deliberately outside the ontology, made visible by prefix or location so tooling skips it and no reader mistakes it for population. The only legitimate exemption mechanism is a visible one.

**The pathological fifth shape is the undeclared mixed composite**: the multi-claim file that goes one-third stale without colliding with anything (adjudication grain ≠ record grain), the wall-document that is part claim, part decision, part history, part instruction. One-atom-per-record is not aesthetics; it makes the collision surface coincide with the adjudication surface.

**Emissions are the hard legitimate composite**, and still under-served: a paper or monograph has its own lifecycle, gates, and fixity (frozen-copy vs regenerable), and its epistemic state is *not* the sum of its atoms — it can overclaim or lag them independently. What exists: composite status bounded by the **weakest necessary premise** (never the weakest thing mentioned, never laundered by a strong source supporting only part); assembly-time verification events; and post-review re-atomization (the adjudicated-overlay pattern) so a composite's claims become auditable again. Certification of any legitimate composite is **inherited through edges, never averaged**.

**Processes are the dual, not a composite record**: what runs over the organs, leaving events as its record-trace, with the process map as its instrument.
