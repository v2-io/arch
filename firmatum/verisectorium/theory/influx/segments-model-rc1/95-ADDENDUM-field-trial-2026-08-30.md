## The finding — RC1 has no episode (routed to the fork queue)

The running system exposed one genuine object-model gap, and it is the fresh yield of the trial rather than anything already in the comparison doc's mapping.

**The gap.** EVENT attaches to `RECORD or LINE {1,1}`; TRAIL is per-record; certification is per-record. But the field evidence says the common case of a securing act is **multi-record**: a cascade episode touches a dozen records as one act; a review certifies an episode, not a record; the session is the unit that an author-note, a close-gate, and a signature actually bind to (live_docs: 67 review episodes, each spanning multiple docs, each carrying one signature and one summary). Under the current model, an episode-shaped act must either be duplicated into N per-record events — losing its unity and its single certification — or recorded once and lose per-record trail completeness. The uniform-anatomy claim (02's most-exposed element, by its own Working Notes) is strained here from a direction none of the per-dimension tests probe: not *which dimension* fails to fit record+trail+projections, but *which acts* do.

**The evidence that the fix is real rather than speculative.** Samuel's architecture holds the inverse factorization, arrived at for enforcement reasons: the WAL (trail) is **session-scoped**, and per-record history is a **materialized projection of it** at session close. It runs; its projections are the per-record trails RC1 wants; its episode object is what the human signs.

**Proposed shape (fork-queue register — ratify, redirect, or let the specimen kill it):** an **EPISODE** object — actor(s), span, summary-note, certification — that events *reference*; per-record TRAILs become **declared projections over episodes** rather than primary storage. Order-invariance (04) is unthreatened: projections stay computations; only the substrate's grain moves. Certification (08) gains its natural target — the thing the grader actually graded.

**The honest path in, per the stranger-reads' own queued recommendation ("more specimens before more ontology"):** do not mint the object first. Write **SPECIMEN #3 — one multi-record episode worked whole** (a cascade touching four records; one signature; per-record projections shown at two moments; the duplication-vs-unity strain exhibited rather than asserted). If the anatomy holds without an EPISODE object, this addendum's finding dies honestly and says so; if it strains, the object arrives demanded by a file, which is 02's own grown-as-needed discipline.
