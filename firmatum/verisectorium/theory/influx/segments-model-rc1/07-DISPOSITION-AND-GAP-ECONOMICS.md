# Disposition and the economics of gaps

*Register: **proposed**. The question lifecycle, the influence semantics behind the steward's placement-for-influence hypothesis, and the value-ordering over open gaps that the model has three independent arrivals at and zero landings of.*

## The dimension, restated for work

`salience/disposition` prices: **is this where it can be found and exert its due influence — exciting the work it should, inhibiting what it should?** A question buried in a wall-document exerts nothing; a question duplicated across five surfaces exerts its influence five times and bills five times; a question parked where the deciding mind never passes exerts it on nobody. The dimension's value is not the question's importance — it is the *match between where the question sits and where its influence is owed*.

**Influence has two signs, and both are work the record does while still open:**

- **Excitatory** — the open item prompts the right work at the right moment: a fork surfaces exactly when its decision blocks; a hazard comes to mind at its trigger; a should-come-to-mind-when fires. (This is where the salience fold lives: a practice or hazard whose moment arrives un-remembered failed on *this* dimension, not on Efficacy — it never got its chance to fire.)
- **Inhibitory** — the open item blocks what it should: an unresolved collision blocks "done"; an open scope question inhibits serving the claim outside its verified range; a pending steward fork inhibits the irreversible act. An open question that fails to inhibit is how premature completion ships.

## The lifecycle

**Posed** (any origination signal, converged to a question) → **routed** with a **declared closer** — who may close it: *steward-fact* (only he knows or only he may decide), *awaiting-ground* (closes itself when a named event lands), *agent-open* (any mind with the context may close it) → **ripens** (its ground arrives; its decision approaches; its readers accumulate) → **closed** at a terminal: *answered* · *rejected-with-reopen-condition* · *superseded-by*. Two laws keep the lifecycle honest:

1. **Asked-and-answered is recorded** — a closed question keeps answering "was this ever considered?" at zero search cost, with its reason and reopen-condition attached; deletion re-bills the deliberation to every future session that re-derives the question.
2. **Silent loss is the deep failure.** Not-closed-and-not-influencing is worse than wrongly-closed, because wrongly-closed collides with reality eventually and silent loss never does — nothing marks the absence of the influence that should have been exerted. (The absence-discipline generalized: an un-routed question is an unrun check wearing a question's clothes.)

## Which gap next — the value-ordering

Three independent arrivals, one principle, now landed together:

- **Decision-inflection** *(steward)*: the pending decision defines which gap is worth closing — a fork demanding a call value-orders the questions that would inform it, often before any hypothesis exists.
- **Expected value of information** *(the formal form)*: a gap's closing is worth what it changes — roughly, the probability the pending choice is wrong times the cost of being wrong, discounted by how much this particular measurement would reduce that. Its ceiling corollary prevents over-investing: no gap is worth more than the value of *perfect* information about it.
- **The estate's own designed form** *(never implemented)*: significance × uncertainty × cascade-size × staleness-age — the same quantity with corpus-native terms (a gap under many dependents, long unexamined, orders higher).

The unified statement: **a gap's priority is the influence its closing would exert, priced by the decisions waiting on it** — which makes gap-ordering a *projection over the Disposition dimension plus the edge graph* (what depends on the unknown; what decision is inflected), never a hand-ranked list. A ranked to-do that no pending decision justifies is somebody's mood wearing a number.

**The counterweight, inseparable** *(steward-attested, from the estate's record)*: cheap truth is run, not triaged. When a bounded check is available, filter on **cost**, never on downstream tactical utility — the ordering above governs where *expensive* attention goes; it must never become a reason to skip a check that costs nothing, because the felt "this one's just belt-and-suspenders" has repeatedly marked exactly the checks that yielded new information.

## Declared ignorance — the vocabulary

A gap is citable only as precisely as its shape is stated: **quantified spread** (a range with known shape) → **scenario** (the outcome space known, the distribution not) → **recognized ignorance** (the gap known, its shape not). Two structural facts ride along: **total ignorance is assignable only by an external auditor** — declaring it yourself converts it to recognized ignorance, so it can only ever be a finding *about* a record, never a self-report; and the **reducibility split carries the ceiling's why** — an epistemic gap (more work could close it) has no principled ceiling, while an aleatory gap (the world itself varies) *is* the ceiling, and confusing them either wastes work on the irreducible or excuses work on the closable.
