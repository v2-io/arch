## What a dimension is

**An epistemic dimension is more than a state — it is a state that carries its warrant by degrees, together with the failure modes that make it dishonest** *(steward articulation, 2026-08-14)*. Three parts, inseparable:

1. **A question** the kind's soundness consists in (is it true? faithful? current?…),
2. **degrees of warrant** — the recorded securing that entitles an answer, moved only by the dimension's own movers,
3. **its dishonesty modes** — the specific ways a record can *report a position it doesn't occupy* on this question.

The word "dimension" is load-bearing: it structurally resists the collapse into one scalar or enum — the estate's recorded historical failure, where a single `status:` field carried kind, grounding, and strength at once and meant different things per record. Dimensions do not sum, average, or reduce to a composite level; a record's epistemic state is a *vector*, and any scalar shown to a consumer is a declared projection of it.

## The substitution law, and the legitimate couplings

**No dimension's value ever substitutes for another's.** Each forbidden substitution is a failure the estate has paid for:

- Backing ↛ Evidence: *ruled* does not make it true — authority never launders groundedness.
- Evidence ↛ Backing (mechanically): strong evidence does not auto-decide — values, resources, and stakes enter the second judgment; strong-on-weak and weak-on-strong are both legitimate cells.
- Backing ↛ Efficacy: endorsement cannot move the firing ladder.
- Comprehension ↛ Evidence: a beautiful explanation is not support.
- Conviction ↛ Evidence: committing hard does not make the design sound.
- Fidelity-corroboration ↛ witness position: more accounts raise credibility, never proximity.
- Freshness/traceability ↛ adequacy: a perfect chain certifies lineage, never fitness.

Dimensions legitimately *inform* one another — through recorded events, never by value-copy: evidence informs decisions; rulings re-scope what claims mean (a rescope changes truth-conditions); teaching narrates claims and resets when they change; firings are empirical events; ground-validating a spec generates evidence; misses and confusions mint questions; decisions originate work.

## Orthogonality is the principle; grouping is organization

*(Steward-corrected, 2026-08-14, dissolving the earlier "Backing cardinality" question — which was a question about grouping, and grouping carries no principled weight.)*

**A dimension is the thing that is orthogonal as it is created**: one independently-movable value with its own field, its own ladder, its own movers, accumulating its own subrecords. Empirical evidence does not share a state field with the theorem; freshness does not share a field with disposition; grounds does not share a field with authority. Two values that can move independently never share a field — that is what dimensionhood *is*.

**The mechanical test** *(steward)*: if capturing two things honestly forces a ladder whose values are unrolled permutations of independently-movable parts — `ruled-groundless | consulted-independent-agent-grounded | …` — they were orthogonal dimensions fused, and the longhand cross-product is the hack that proves it. This failure is already live in corpus records that try to capture decidedness in one field.

Under this rule, grouping is a **namespace in the dimension's name** — not an object at all *(steward, same day, reducing an object this model had briefly minted)*:

```text
evidence/derivation    evidence/in-vivo      evidence/wild-empirical
evidence/instance      evidence/testimony    evidence/carriage
decision/grounding     decision/accountability
decision/consultation  decision/falsifiers
salience/freshness     salience/disposition
```

Each named dimension has its own ladder and accumulates its own evidences (weight-of-evidence style, theoretically limitless subrecords, each item at its own rung). A claim's overall "truth," a decision's overall "decidedness" — any namespace-level value — is never a stored field; it is a **declared projection** over member dimensions (strongest-line / every-member-sustains / independence-locked). The namespace is part of the name: it structurally *cannot* bear invariants, re-cutting it is a rename, and the steward's own illustrative cut differs from this file's table (freshness and disposition under `salience/`; the reopen-trigger coordinate named `decision/falsifiers`) — kept as the demonstration that the cuts are free, not as a pending decision.

## Kinds as dimension-compositions

A kind's dimension-profile *is* most of its declaration: a **definition** is a coinage (Backing) + fit-to-usage (Evidence); a **prohibition** is an assertion-of-refutedness (Evidence) + a usage license (bound); **teaching prose** is Comprehension + a claim license + referent-Freshness; an **instrument** is Freshness against its population + a conflict-precedence bound; a **spec** is Conviction + Backing on its choices. Composites close over four declared shapes — **reify** (mint an atom for substance living between records), **view-ify** (select and order without introducing), **cluster** (typed parts under one identity), **exempt visibly** (outside the model, marked so) — and the named pathology is the undeclared fifth: the mixed file that is part claim, part decision, part history, adjudicable as none.

**The cardinal sin's mirror: under-recording** *(named by the de-novo read; steward-corrected to full severity; economics in 08)*. The securing work done but never recorded leaves the record occupying a position it doesn't report — **just as dishonest as an unwarranted lift** *(steward)*, with a distinctive poison: it corrupts the semantics of absence, since a reader seeing work beside an un-lifted status cannot tell *unrun* from *unrecorded* from *"did they see a mistake that made them decline the upgrade?"* — a phantom negative finding nobody made. The remedy is economic (recording made cheaper than not recording — the affordance law; 09) **plus semantic**: deliberate non-lifts are recorded as decline-events with reasons, so absence stays meaning *unrun* and nothing else.
