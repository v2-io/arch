## Why this directory exists

Three generations of partial models now describe the same object, each written under the frame that produced it:

1. **The taxonomy arc** (2026-08-10) — families and axes, static, survey-checked. Frame: *classification*.
2. **`01-MODEL.md`** (2026-08-14, first successor) — currencies + nine questions + universal machinery. Frame: *generative derivation for a founding agent*.
3. **The nets + `06-COUPLING`** (2026-08-14, second successor) — the cycle, the lock, the commitment law, the recording proposal. Frame: *executable dynamics*.

Nobody has stated the whole in one reading order, and the steward's honest report is that despite real advances "it's still not particularly clear to me." The specific hazard RC1 exists to repair: **formalism capture** — the Petri net is a *verification instrument* (it proved order-invariance, made status-projection unforgeable, caught the wrapper law), but an instrument cannot be the model of record, because the model's consumer is a founding agent and a reviewing steward reading prose, not a token interpreter. The net stays; it moves from spine to appendix.

**The charge, verbatim (steward, 2026-08-14):** *"The ultimate objective is for any agent creating a verisectorium to look at the kinds of things they need to create and see clearly what they need to track and where and how it enters and upgrades and gets refined for their verisectorium's needs, and what it can then certify etc."* RC1 is measured against that sentence and nothing else.

## The model's actual shape (the one-paragraph statement RC1 must never lose)

**A record is — as an opening gloss, not a derivational primitive — a promise made to future strangers.** *(The demotion travels with the frame, deliberately: this is the quarantined securing-contract's primitive, and `03-ADVERSARIAL-READ` adjudicated it down — "an honest equivalent phrasing worth one line, not a primitive" — because its derivational claims were a graft; failure-and-repair stays the primary individuator, steward-redeemed and survey-grounded. The rehabilitation argued here is narrower and different in kind: as *pedagogy* the sentence is the best first contact the arc produced — it tells a fresh reader why any of the machinery exists before one mechanism appears — and a gloss claims no derivations. If even that use proves to anchor readers into the contract-frame, cut it; the model loses nothing.)* **Its kind is what it promises, individuated by how it fails and what repairs it; its currency is what its strength is denominated in; its state is a ledger of the securing acts performed on it — never a hand-set word. Truthification is the cycle that runs those acts:** something originates (a mismatch, or a generator), a question forms, a record is minted with its foundation declared, independent lines of securing work run over it (each line the diachronic form of one foundation rung, each with its own rungs, channels, and ceiling), results pay into the ledger order-invariantly, the record lands in the population under its currency's write semantics, consumers take what its certified state licenses — and consumption, decay, and staleness feed back into origination. Aggregation across lines is guarded by one lock (independent-failure-mode legs only), inter-line contact is governed by one commitment law (question/design channels free; verdict channels pre-committed), and every ladder's warrant rests on the fidelity of the trail beneath it.

Everything else in the corpus — the nine organs, the taxonomy, the nets, the recording layout — is that paragraph at some altitude or grain.

## Proposed contents, in reading order, with the why

Numbered files; each `proposed`; each states per-element provenance (carried / synthesized / open) in the 02-GROUNDING style, which the exercise audit validated as the format that survives scrutiny.

**`02-RECORD-ANATOMY.md` — the statics: what one record physically and logically is.**  
Body (present truth, replacement semantics, the Kahn-determinate collision surface) · line sidecars (append-only securing events, one per active line, event tuple: channel · actor+authority · criterion · criterion-commitment · date · outcome · era-key) · projections (every status cell computed from the trail; max / floor / lock as three declared projections, never one scalar by default) · **working notes** (forward-work only, per `def-atom-cluster`'s earned four-part carve — restored here after the predecessor's review caught the first cut dropping them; crumb-routing empties *into* the trail, working notes carry what has no event yet) · crumb-routing (O16: inline mark → harvested to trail → stripped from body) · companions. Why its own file: this is the **uniform-anatomy claim** — that all currencies share one mechanism and differ only in admissible line-sets and guard vocabularies — which is the strongest simplification available and must be *tested per currency, in writing*, because it is exactly the kind of beautiful claim that assimilates counterevidence. The test protocol: as each remaining failure-mode burst lands (Efficacy, Comprehension, Conviction, Currency, Disposition), check whether that currency fits body+sidecars+projections without strain; a currency that doesn't fit falsifies the claim and the file says so.

**`03-CURRENCIES.md` — the denominations, consolidated, with the steward's failure modes folded in.**
