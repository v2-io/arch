# Segments model RC1 — outline and charter

*Register: **proposed**. This file is the charter; content files land beside it incrementally, each carrying per-element provenance (carried / synthesized / open).*

## Purpose

Three partial statements of one model exist — the record-kind taxonomy (static classification), the concerns model with its derivation questions (generative), and the cycle/coupling work (dynamics) — and no surface states the whole in one reading order. RC1 is that surface. Its consumers: the founding agent of any new verisectorium, and the steward reviewing. Its measure is the standing objective: *"for any agent creating a verisectorium to look at the kinds of things they need to create and see clearly what they need to track and where and how it enters and upgrades and gets refined for their verisectorium's needs, and what it can then certify."*

Prose leads. The executable nets were scaffolding — useful checks, now normalized out of the model's vocabulary; the intended formal carrier is the declarative language of 09.

## The model in one paragraph

**A record's kind is individuated by how it fails and what repairs it, and adjudicated on its kind's concerns — the questions its soundness consists in; its state is the trail of securing acts performed on it, with every status a computation over that trail — never a hand-set word. Truthification is the cycle that runs those acts:** something originates (a mismatch, or a generator), a question forms, a record is minted with its foundation declared, independent lines of securing work run over it (each line the diachronic form of one foundation rung, each with its own rungs, channels, and ceiling), results are recorded order-invariantly, the record lands in the population under its kind's write semantics, consumers take what its certified state licenses — and consumption, decay, and staleness feed back into origination. Aggregation across lines is guarded by one lock (independent-failure-mode legs only), inter-line contact is governed by one commitment law (question/design channels free; verdict channels pre-committed), and every ladder's warrant rests on the fidelity of the trail beneath it.

The rest of RC1 is this paragraph at higher resolution.

## Contents, in reading order

**`01-SPINE.md`** — the model at three altitudes: *why* records must carry their own epistemic honesty (living collection; total turnover; comprehension economics), *what* a record is (anatomy + concerns), *how* records truthify (the cycle). The foundation × exercised plane — everything born at sensibility; all honest work moves toward better-grounded and harder-exercised; the cardinal sin is reporting a position you don't occupy — as first-reader scaffolding.

**`02-RECORD-ANATOMY.md`** — what one record is: **body** (present truth, replacement semantics, the collision surface) · **working notes** (forward-work only) · **per-line trails** (append-only securing events; tuple: channel · actor+authority · criterion · criterion-commitment · date · outcome · era-key) · **projections** (every status cell computed from the trail; max / floor / lock as declared projections, never one scalar by default) · **crumb routing** (inline mark → harvested to trail → stripped from body) · **companions**. Carries the **uniform-anatomy claim** — all concerns share this one mechanism, differing only in admissible line-sets and guard vocabularies — untested and attractive, so it is tested per concern in writing: a concern that does not fit falsifies it, and the file says so.

**`03-CONCERNS.md`** — the concern table in full; the coupling map (legitimate causal channels; the forbidden substitutions — authority never launders groundedness, endorsement never moves firing, a beautiful explanation is not support, commitment is not soundness, corroboration raises credibility never witness position, traceability is not adequacy); Fidelity as the substrate every ladder's warrant rests on; Disposition as placement-for-influence, routing work between concerns; kind as concern-composition profile, with the composite closure shapes (reify / view-ify / cluster / exempt-visibly; the undeclared mixed composite as the named pathology). **This file's first job is the completeness check the spine declares open**: every catalogued axis of the research (standing, salience, coverage, grain, audience-safety, usage- and claim-licenses, and the rest of the eighteen) adjudicated *concern / bound / universal machinery / gate*, and the Backing-cardinality question (one concern with four coordinates, or several orthogonal concerns) resolved on the merits. Failure modes fold in from the verbatim series per concern as each rests, marked steward-attested vs interpolated.

**`04-CYCLE.md`** — the dynamics in prose: two-pole origination (corrective: mismatch, collision, gap, staleness; generative: proposals, brainstorms, wandering, counterfactuals, curiosity; decision-inflection crossing both) · minting with declared foundation · fan-out derived from the declared rungs · signed ladders (refutation channels first-class; a break that narrows scope is a result) · order-invariant recording · landing · serving · maintenance. Instantiated for Evidence and Backing; stubs for the rest. The inter-line laws live here: **the lock** (a consumption-time question over the trail — which is what frees lines to collaborate during work), **the commitment law** (contact through question and design channels is legitimate and usually obligatory; a check counts toward corroboration only if its criterion was committed before contact with the outcome) with the coherence-vs-corroboration tells (pre-committed criterion; divergence nailed to root vs tuned away; mutual-adjustment agreement measures the reconciliation, not the world; descent traced before legs count as independent), and **transmission as the adjudicated/calibrated/qualified ambient prior** (every line partially descends from it; the lock looks through the carriage wrapper to the source's own method family; the de-novo audit is the deliberately manufactured transmission-independent leg).

**`05-OBJECT-MODEL.md`** — the model's logical objects and relations, stated the way a schema is stated: record (body · working notes · trail · projections · companions), line, event, concern-declaration, the ambient prior as a store-scope object with per-record descent edges, coordination surfaces (in-flight work; reconciliation findings) as tracked objects, and the **era registry** — a key → current-era object, so decay has a recorded referent and an era bump is an event. Deliberately *not* physical: file layout, storage, concurrency mechanics, and addressing are deployment matters under the estate's already-settled principles (write-isolation, the store anatomy, the udon paths work) and are named as interfaces here, not designed.

**`06-EDGES.md`** — typed relational state. `depends:`, cascade-marking on refutation, descent tracing, `seeded-by:`, and supersession are all edge operations, currently untyped. This file adjudicates the edge vocabulary (a rich typed family vs a minimal three-type set, with each type's composability declared — chaining compounds error unless the relation kind licenses transitivity) and applies **rebut vs undercut** to dependency re-pricing: a *refuted* premise rebuts its dependents (hard cascade — mark or re-derive); a *weakened* premise undercuts them (support degraded, conclusion possibly still true — softer, different semantics). Re-pricing is the model's hardest open problem; this distinction is the candidate that makes it precise.

**`07-DISPOSITION-AND-GAP-ECONOMICS.md`** — the question lifecycle (ripeness, routing, declared closer, terminal states; asked-and-answered recorded so nothing is re-billed) plus what it lacks: a **value-ordering over open gaps**. Decision-inflection supplies the principle (the pending decision defines which gap is worth closing); expected value of information supplies the formal form (which measurement would most reduce the cost of deciding wrong). Declared ignorance gets vocabulary: quantified spread → scenario (range known, shape not) → recognized ignorance (gap known, shape not); total ignorance is assignable only by an external auditor; epistemic gaps have no principled ceiling, aleatory gaps *are* the ceiling.

**`08-CERTIFICATION-AND-AUDIT.md`** — what a consumer may conclude from a record's state, checkable without trusting its author: a named criterion, a grader whose independence meets the rung, a dated event, a reproducible check. Certification strength is bounded by grader independence, not effort; a certificate decays with its warrant (edit, referent change, era bump) — one that cannot decay is a label-lie in waiting; chain-integrity is *verification*, fitness-for-purpose is *validation*, and they never share a cell. Auditing is reading over the trail — which is why Fidelity underlies everything. Ends with RC1's own acceptance protocol: the de-novo-auditor walk (hand a fresh mind one corpus plus this model; ask only "what should be audited?" — the model's own stated criterion); a founding dry run of the derivation questions on a live deployment; the two queued external checks (the evidence-to-decision structure; the safety-case frameworks, where a counterexample to the ceiling-plus-evidence-action design most plausibly lives).

**`09-LANGUAGE.md`** — the model as a declarative language *(steward-offered direction, adopted as RC1's formal horizon)*: a small rule language in which a deployment *states* its kinds, concerns, lines, states, movers, and enablements — "a rule that adds a new state available to this line under these conditions, enabling this" — so an instance is programmed and reinforced rather than prose-governed, and extending it is writing a rule, not editing a document. The prose model (01–08) is this language's semantics; the derivation questions are its interactive front-end; the retired nets were early gropings toward it. Sketch register until the object model stabilizes.

**`90-PROVENANCE-AND-FORKS.md`** — per-element carried/synthesized/open across all files; the supersession map into the prior artifacts (nothing there moves until its content demonstrably lands here — the delete-test governs); the open steward forks, each as a brief.
