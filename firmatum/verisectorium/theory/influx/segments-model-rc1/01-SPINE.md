# The spine — the segments model at three altitudes

*Register: **proposed**. This file is the whole model at low resolution; 02–08 are its parts at full resolution; per-element provenance is in 90. The model here is a **logical object-model**: nothing in it decides physical realization — file layout, storage, tooling — which are deployment matters under the estate's already-settled principles. A reader may stop here and hold the whole.*

## Altitude 1 — why a record must carry its own honesty

A verisectorium governs a living collection: never finished, improved across unbounded sessions, its deliverables emissions rather than endings. Every mind that works it arrives with total turnover of collection-specific knowledge — whatever a session understood and did not exteriorize is gone at its boundary. The collection is therefore the sole carrier of what it knows, and comprehension cost is paid per reader forever while authoring cost is paid once. Three consequences set the entire design:

1. **Every record must say, in itself, how strongly it is held and why** — or every future reader must either re-derive it (unaffordable) or trust it (unwarranted).
2. **Honesty is only well-defined relative to kind.** Whether a record is sound cannot be judged until you know what the record *does* — asserts, decides, recounts, teaches, prescribes, asks, points, or commits. These fail differently, and a status vocabulary that ignores the difference is dishonest somewhere.
3. **The only status a stranger can trust is one they can recompute.** A hand-set word — "verified," "done," "ratified" — is a label that can lie. A status projected from a trail of recorded acts — who did what, against which criterion, when, with what outcome — can be checked by anyone, forever.

Everything below is machinery for satisfying these three at a cost a living collection can afford.

## Altitude 2 — what a record is

**One atom, of one kind.** A record's kind is what it does, and kinds individuate by **failure-and-repair**: two kinds deserve separation exactly when they go wrong differently and route to different repairs. This makes the kind-set *derivable* per instance rather than inherited — the founding agent asks, of each thing the project must write down, "what should go wrong with this, and what should adjudicate and repair it?", and holds the kind open until the question is answered from first principles. A survey of other projects checks the answer's completeness; it never supplies the answer.

**Each kind is adjudicated on its own epistemic dimensions** — the questions its soundness consists in, each with its own movers. A dimension is the unit of *orthogonality*: one independently-movable value with its own field, ladder, and movers — two values that can move independently never share a field. Dimensions are named under organizational **namespaces** — `evidence/derivation`, `evidence/testimony`, `decision/grounding`, `decision/accountability` — and the namespace is part of the name, never an object: a claim's derivation, measurements, testimony, and carried sources each ladder separately, a decision's grounding, consultation, and accountability likewise, and any namespace-level value a consumer sees is a declared projection, never a stored field. The table shows the questions at namespace altitude:

| Namespace / Dimension | The question it asks | Moved only by |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence** | is it true? | securing acts on named, signed channels; settled at the primary |
| **Backing** | who is accountable for this choice; who was consulted; on what grounds was it made; what reopens it? — four coordinates, never fused into one grade | decision events |
| **Fidelity** | is it faithful to what happened? | nothing after capture — witness position is fixed; corroboration raises credibility only |
| **Efficacy** | does it fire when its moment comes? | real firings |
| **Comprehension** | does it land in a reader's mind? | reader events — misses, confusions, walks |
| **Conviction** | is the commitment borne out? | ground-validation — builds, tests, adoption |
| **Freshness** | is it still current — itself, and about its referents? | resets on referent change; never ratchets |
| **Disposition** | is it where it can be found and exert its due influence — exciting the work it should, inhibiting what it should? *(steward-offered hypothesis, adopted here)* | routing and lifecycle events |

Three honesty notes on the table itself. **The rows are not equally mature** — Evidence and Backing are worked (steward-attested failure modes, worked specimens, per-dimension ladders); Fidelity's role is settled; Efficacy, Comprehension, Conviction, Freshness, and Disposition are open hypotheses wearing full rows, awaiting the steward's failure-mode illumination — roughly three matured namespaces and five candidates, not a finished octagon. **The set is not established complete.** These eight are what the arc has surfaced; whether they cover even this estate's own record population — let alone what a foreign deployment needs — is an open check, owed in 03 against every axis the research catalogued (standing, salience, coverage, grain, audience-safety, usage- and claim-licenses), each of which must be adjudicated *dimension / bound / universal machinery / gate* rather than assumed absorbed. Standing, at least, is settled as a **gate, not a dimension**: a record produced without the power to produce it never existed as its kind — a validity question before any soundness question. And the tell for a fused dimension is mechanical: a ladder whose values are unrolled permutations of independently-movable parts (`ruled-groundless | consulted-independent-agent-grounded | …`) is two dimensions sharing a field, and the cross-product longhand is the proof — a failure already live in corpus records.

Dimensions inform one another through recorded events — evidence informs decisions, rulings re-scope claims, firings are empirical facts — but the load-bearing half of the table is what it forbids: **no dimension's value ever substitutes for another's.** *Ruled* does not make it true. Strong evidence does not auto-decide. A beautiful explanation is not support. Endorsement cannot move the firing ladder. Commitment is not soundness. Corroborating accounts raise credibility, never witness position. A perfect provenance chain certifies lineage, never adequacy. Every one of these substitutions is a failure already paid for at least once; the model makes each one checkable, and — wherever the machinery reaches — inexpressible.

Composite kinds are dimension-compositions, not new dimensions: a definition is a coinage (Backing) plus fit-to-usage (Evidence); teaching prose is Comprehension bounded by a declared claim-license and referent-Freshness; an instrument is Freshness against the population it tracks.

**Anatomy** (logical). Every record, whatever its dimensions, is a small cluster: a **body** carrying present truth under its kind's write semantics (replacement for present-truth kinds, append-only for accounts and events); **working notes** carrying forward-work only; an append-only **trail** of securing events; **projections** computed from the trail — every status cell, every outline row; **companions** as needed. *(That this one anatomy serves all dimensions is itself a proposed claim, tested per dimension in 02.)*

**Foundation and exercise are the two coordinates of any record's honesty.** What grounds it — first principles, proof, declaration, measurement, instance of a proven model, analogy, informed hypothesis, downward to ritual — is set at conception and predicts only the probability of being right. What has been *done* to it is accumulated by acts and determines what is actually known. Everything a generative mind writes is born at sensibility — plausible, pattern-shaped, unexamined — and all honest work is one vector: ground it better, exercise it harder. The cardinal sin is not being low on either coordinate; it is reporting a position you don't occupy.

## Altitude 3 — how records truthify

Truthification is a cycle, and a living collection is a metabolism running it, not a warehouse storing its outputs.

**Origination** has two poles: *corrective* — a model-observation mismatch, a present-truth collision, a named gap, staleness — and *generative* — proposals, brainstorms, first-encounter wanderings, counterfactuals, curiosity; **decision-inflection** crosses both: a fork demanding a call, often before any hypothesis exists, and the pending decision is what makes one gap worth closing before another. All signals converge to questions.

**Minting** turns a question into a record: kind chosen by failure-and-repair, foundation declared. The declaration is load-bearing — it derives the record's **securing lines**, one per declared foundation rung, because *the instrument follows the ground*: grounded in proof → derive it; in declaration → ratify it; in reality → measure it; in a proven model → check the instance; in analogy → break the analogy; a practice, any ground → watch it fire. A line is one rung worked over time, with its own rungs, channels, refutation channels, and ceiling — and refutation is a first-class result: a break that narrows scope is a finding, not a failure.

**Recording** appends each line's results to the record's trail; the summary a consumer reads is *computed* from the trail, and every such computation must be **order-invariant** — the same whatever order the lines reported in. An aggregation that depends on arrival order has a race condition built into its epistemology; anchoring is that race condition in cognitive form.

**Landing** changes the population under the kind's write semantics; collisions surface and block "done"; dependents re-price.

**Serving** is what the collection is for: certifying, deciding, teaching, applying — each consumer taking exactly what the record's certified state licenses, no more.

**Maintenance** is decay made honest: measured results carry their era and expire when it bumps; teaching prose resets when its referents change; carriage re-verifies on a schedule because walls move. Staleness, consumption feedback, and reader confusion flow back into origination, and the cycle closes.

Three laws govern how the lines touch:

1. **The coherence default** *(steward formulation, superseding an earlier cap-and-unlock construction called "the lock" in older material)*. A record's epistemic value never exceeds its correctly-aggregated evidence — aggregation is conditioning, and there is no combination bonus to grant or withhold. The only live question is correlation structure: **agreement between lines adds weight exactly to the degree they can be shown not to share descent — and the default assumption is that they do share it.** Same-author agreement, same-method repetition, and lines seeded by one source are coherence — restatement, valuable for catching errors, ~zero marginal weight. What cuts descent: contact with reality, genuinely unseeded readers, independent method families — each only as far as its own inputs were unseeded. The question is asked at consumption time, over a trail whose events record their descent — which is exactly what frees the lines to collaborate during the work.
2. **The commitment law.** Lines may — usually must — inform each other through question and design channels: a simulation built from the math is what makes it a test *of* the math. What destroys evidential value is contact through the **verdict channel**: adjusting what-counts-as-passing after seeing the outcome. A check corroborates only if its criterion was committed before contact — and a reconciliation loop that learned by mutual adjustment restores eligibility cheaply, with one fresh committed run of each line's final form.
3. **Fidelity underlies everything.** Every warrant question is a read over the trail; corrupt the trail and every status above it becomes unwarranted at once. This is why the trail is append-only, why corrections chain beneath rather than overwrite, and why auditing *is* reading the trail.

**Certification** closes the loop with the stranger the collection exists for: a certification is a claim about a record checkable without trusting its author — named criterion, grader whose independence meets the rung, dated event, reproducible check. It is bounded by grader independence, not effort; it decays with its warrant; and it is *verification* (the record meets its stated criterion), never by itself *validation* (the criterion was adequate for the purpose) — the two never share a cell.

**The acceptance test for the whole model:** a fresh auditor, given nothing but the records and their carried states, knows exactly what to check — re-derive these claims, confirm those decisions still stand on their named grounds, confirm the narratives still match their referents, confirm the trails were never edited. Wherever tribal knowledge is needed to know what auditing means, a kind is misdeclared or a dimension is missing.
