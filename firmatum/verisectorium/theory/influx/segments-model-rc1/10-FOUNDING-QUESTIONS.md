# The founding questions — the derivation procedure, in this set

*Register: **proposed**. The procedure a founding agent (or the kit questionnaire) runs per record-kind their instance will hold — restated here in RC1's vocabulary because both stranger reads found the set "incomplete at the exact point of use" without it. Carried from the prior model's nine questions with the dimension-grain corrections applied; the wild population of existing instances checks your answers' completeness, never bounds them.*

Run these **per kind of record the project must write down**, in order:

0. **Standing.** What must be true for a record of this kind to *validly come into being at all* — who has the power to produce one, through what act, with what chain-integrity? A gate, never a rung: a "decision" recorded by someone without the power to decide is not a weak decision; it never existed as one. Misfire ≠ wrong-content.
1. **Speech-act.** What does a record of this kind *do* — assert, decide, record, prescribe, ask, refer, teach, commit? (Composites answer with more than one; they close over the four shapes — reify, view-ify, cluster, exempt-visibly.)
2. **Failure.** What *should* go wrong with one? Forced from first principles; hold the kind open until answered. The wild corpus checks your answer's completeness; it never supplies the answer.
3. **Instrument.** What *should* adjudicate and repair that failure? Half-derivable from the record's ground — proof → re-derive; declaration → ratify; reality → measure; a proven model → check the instance; analogy → break it; a practice → watch it fire. A declared-∅ instrument is legitimate for records whose value is that nobody repairs them (sketches, raw reflections) — but declared intent is the floor, and principled-∅ must never be confused with nobody-has-asked-yet.
4. **Dimensions.** Which epistemic dimensions does this kind open (or: which foundation declaration derives them)? This yields the kind's tracked state by construction, and its write semantics follow from its truth-conditions — never from style. The fusion check runs here: any proposed ladder whose values are cross-products of independently-movable parts is two dimensions sharing a field — split them.
5. **Movers and warrant.** Who may move each value, on what warrant, recorded how? Every movement is an event (channel · actor+authority · criterion · commitment · date · outcome · era-key); top rungs are event-gated and grader-escalated per stakes; and the ledger keeps *what the act showed* and *how trusted its performer* as two columns, never one grade.
6. **Ceiling and evidence-action.** Per laddered dimension: the strongest value this kind can reach — assignable from kind before work — and the named action that would raise a given record toward it. Transmitted ceilings cap at the source's own tier; authority ceilings derive from ownership; epistemic gaps have no principled ceiling, aleatory gaps *are* the ceiling.
7. **Grain and edges.** At what depth does state attach (record / section / clause), what is the projection rule upward, and which edge kinds does the kind participate in (with each kind's composability declared)?
8. **Certification.** What may a consumer conclude from this kind's state, checkable from the record and its trail alone? The acceptance test for the whole declaration: *is it immediately obvious to a de-novo auditor, given nothing but the records and their carried states, exactly what should be audited?* Where that fails, the declaration is missing something.

### Strain surfaced 2026-08-30 (steward, working a hypothetical founding for a udon spec) — two missing sentences, proposed repair

*Register: proposed — steward-surfaced strain, repair drafted by that session's coord; the strain is attested, the repair wording is synthesis. Both stranger reads flagged the same class ("incomplete at the exact point of use"); this names the two specific gaps.*

**The strain, verbatim in substance:** *"most decisions in most projects have a combination of assertions, decisions, prescriptions, how to teach them, commitments… I'm still stuck on Q1 — and how to properly tease them out when there doesn't seem to be any domain-level spine that is topical — the main spine in any actual verisectorium."* Q1 as written invites reading it as pick-one-verb, and nothing in this file says where the corpus's topical structure lives — so a founder reasonably fears the kind-set is being asked to organize the collection.

**Missing sentence 1 — the record-boundary question precedes the speech-act question.** Q1's honest output is a *list* of acts, not a choice — composition is the normal case (a definition decides + asserts; a spec row commits + decides), not the pathology. What actually fixes record boundaries is 02's grain invariant, run as a question *before* Q1: **can this piece fail — be refuted, rot, reopen — while that piece stands?** Fail-together → one record, multiple dimensions (a rule's rationale is its own `decision/grounding`, not a record). Fail-apart → separate records (the rule's examples rot per parser-era while the rule stands; its teaching prose can mis-teach while both stand). Speech-act is downstream shorthand for failure-profile: when Q1 feels muddy, run Q2 directly and let the acts fall out of the failure analysis. *(Candidate re-ordering for ratification: insert this as Q1a, or re-letter Q1 as "Speech-acts (enumerate)" with the boundary test as its gate.)*

**Missing sentence 2 — the topical spine is the outline; kinds never organize a verisectorium, they adjudicate it.** The domain's own anatomy (a spec's chapters; a theory's organs) lives in the authored OUTLINE view, with records of every kind interleaved row by row; dependency lives in the edges, cutting across chapters freely; kind is pure per-record metadata that matters only at adjudication, audit, and cascade moments. No directory, chapter, or grouping is ever kind-shaped. This is already theory canon ([[claim-outline-as-view]], [[form-pedagogical-ordering]], [[norm-outline-first]]) — but a founder working from this set alone cannot know it, and the fear that the taxonomy must double as the table of contents is exactly the misreading the omission licenses. Compressed rule of thumb, offered for the set: **topic → outline · dependency → edges · failure → record boundaries · adjudication → kind & dimensions** — four structures, four questions; the muddle comes only when one tries to do another's job.

**Disposition:** both sentences want landing in the body (Q1 and a short "where structure lives" preamble note) on ratification; held here as strain-record until the steward rules. The founding dry run should test whether the repair suffices — a founder who still asks "where's my spine?" means it didn't.
