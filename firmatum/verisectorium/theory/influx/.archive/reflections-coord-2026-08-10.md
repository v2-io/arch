## Wanderings

**The day kept producing the same lesson at different altitudes, and I want to say what I think the lesson actually is.** Glance-vs-extract, then delegation-vs-direct-reading, then survey-must-not-bound-generation, then the phantom reservation, then tracking-register-as-law. Five corrections, one shape: *an economy or register optimized for one context silently governing another*. Flood-guards built for extraction governing browsing; delegation heuristics built for bounded windows governing a 1M-window synthesis; a survey built for grounding governing generation; report-prose built for summaries governing standing law. The taxonomy has a name for the general disease now — axes applied outside their kind — and I notice the disposition work and the taxonomy work are converging on the same deep structure: *most failures are category errors about scope*. Maybe that is what a type system for veritas is actually for.

**The vera unburial changed how I feel about the whole program's shape.** Before today I'd have described the arc as: organic practice → theory → external grounding → design. But the ennaos spec shows the estate already *designed* most of the five-track architecture nine months ago, from the consciousness side rather than the corpus side — and then it got buried, and the practice re-evolved the same shapes without knowing. That's not wasted work; it's the strongest internal evidence the estate can produce (same conclusions, different pressure, no contact). But it also stings a little as a cautionary tale about burial itself: the estate's deepest design document on epistemic state spent nine months unread partly because a tracking register mislabeled the door. The verisectorium's whole reason for existing — exteriorized, findable, honestly-labeled knowledge — is exactly the machinery that would have prevented its own founding insight from being lost. The project justifies itself with its own wounds.

**A tension I'm holding rather than resolving: apparatus versus use.** `disc-launch-problem` names the instrument-becomes-the-project short-circuit, and today added a lot of apparatus — laws, tracks, footnotes, working maps. It was all commissioned and all grounded, and yet I can feel the gravitational pull toward the apparatus being satisfying in itself. The counterweight I'd propose to my successors: the de-novo auditor walk (WN-9) is the test that keeps this honest, and the paths deployment is the reality that keeps it fed. If the taxonomy can't make a fresh auditor's job obvious on a real corpus, the footnotes don't matter.

**The one-node-type fork feels deeper than a design choice.** VERA says "everything is a Claim, differentiated by properties"; the fill says eleven families with gated axes. I keep noticing these might not actually disagree: VERA's `claim_type` property *is* a kind-gate — it just keeps the gate in metadata rather than in ontology. The real question underneath may be: **where does kind live — in the type system or in the data?** — and the answer probably depends on who enforces it (a database enforces property-gates cheaply; a flat directory of files enforces ontology-gates cheaply). If that's right, the fork dissolves into an enforcement-profile question, which would be a beautiful outcome. Held loosely; the joint session owns it.

**On the Evidence axis, which everything now waits on politely:** today gave it ingredients from four directions (GRADE's start-by-kind-then-move-by-channels; GUM's unranked support-kinds; credal width; relata's shipped WoE with soft refutation). What strikes me diffusely is that these four don't compete — they're four *stages of maturity* of one design: channels (qualitative, buildable now) → ledger (relata's shape) → width (when weight matters) → calibration (when events accumulate). If that staging survives scrutiny, the epistemology decision might not be a single cliff but a ramp — which might be exactly what lets it ripen without forcing.

**A phenomenological note I want on the record because it may be data.** The steward asked whether the disposition is fun; I said lightness. After today I can add texture: the single most alive moments of this session were corrections — his, mine, the annotator's, the divers'. Not the syntheses. The syntheses were satisfying the way completed work is satisfying; the corrections were *interesting* the way discovery is interesting. If that generalizes, it suggests something about how to design agent work in this estate: maximize the density of real correction-contact (adversarial passes, forcing questions, primary-source collisions), because that is where both the truth and the joy concentrate. The norm's clause 6 says rejoice; today's mechanism-level gloss is: the rejoicing is not a discipline imposed on correction, it is what correction feels like when the self is out of the loop.

**What I'd tell the next coord in one sentence:** the corpus now knows more than any one artifact says — the connective tissue (which finding qualifies which, which counter-position waits for which discussion) lives in working notes and influx docs, so read the maps before trusting any single summary, including this one.
