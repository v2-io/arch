# Honest incompleteness is a complete discharge

*Every discipline that prevents false confidence — gates, independent confirmation, the rule that no artifact may be cited as warrant for itself — will, left without a stopping rule, convert an honest "not yet" into an unfinished duty and stop the corpus releasing anything; the stopping rule is that a record which cannot lie does not need another gate.*

## The claim

**(A) The regress is a real failure mode of a healthy corpus, not of a lazy one.** A verification culture accumulates checks, and each check makes the next one look cheap. The characteristic end state is an agent launching a further verification not because the artifact would otherwise assert something false, but because landing it at an honest lower tier *feels* insufficient. Nothing is dishonest at any step; the corpus simply stops shipping, and the material that would have landed sits in exploration artifacts where the canonical channels cannot find it.

**(B) Duty is discharged — fully — when three things hold at the current level of understanding.**

1. The record carries its **honest tier**, whatever that is: conditional, discussion-grade, sketch, known-defective. Not an inflated one, and equally not a deflated one ([[integration-metabolism]]: the label tracks truth-status, not novelty or provenance).
2. Its notes state **precisely what is unresolved and what would resolve it** — not that work remains, but which work, and what its completion would change.
3. The open remainder is **released to the standing cycle** — the item layer, the proposal portfolio, the audit cycle, the steward's queue — so it is *someone's* rather than nobody's.

Then stop. There is no obligation to drive every thread to closed form in real time, and continuing to escalate verification depth past the point where the artifact is completely honest is not extra rigor; it is the regress.

**(C) The self-check is one question, and it is answerable in the moment.** *Am I adding this gate because the record would otherwise **lie**, or because an honest lower tier feels insufficient?* The first is legitimate — a claim of settledness, a promotion, a completion statement can all assert false confidence, and a gate is what stops them. The second is the regress wearing rigor's clothes. Note that both feel identical from inside, which is why the question has to name the artifact's *assertion* rather than the agent's comfort: the test is what the record would say, not how sure anyone is.

**(D) The positive half: weak-tier material belongs in the corpus, marked, and holding it back is the more common error.** A corpus that carries honest tiers has, by that fact, made room for material below the top of its ladder — structural recognition, landscape carves, conditional derivations, framing that is not yet formal. Keeping such material in an exploration artifact until it is "strong enough" is a mistake in both directions at once: it deprives the corpus of content it could carry honestly, and it puts that content where future agents will not find it, because they search the canonical channels and archaeology is not one.

The estate's steward has stated this against an agent's own conservatism, and the correction is worth carrying in his voice because the pull it corrects is strong and feels principled:

> *"Our epistemology allows for more than just derivation and high proof standard parts. Expository narratives that help feel out the landscape for some of the more 'fuzzy' areas like this are absolutely possible as segments — just with the epistemology properly grounded. We really don't like working theory living in spikes instead of the main body. Canon makes it sound like we have to have solid results, but that's never really been the case — we put our weak stuff in there too, just properly marked, and then subsequent passes happen to have strengthened a lot of it…"*

The formulation that falls out of it is the one to keep: **strengthening is the promotion process, not the admission condition.** A record enters at its honest tier and advances as gaps close; it does not wait outside for a tier it can only reach by being worked on inside.

**(E) The complementary rule, non-negotiable, because (D) is only legitimate with it.** *Properly marked* is doing all the work in that quote. Weak-tier material in canon is honest exactly to the degree that the record signals which of its claims are derived, which are structural recognition, which are conditional on named premises, and which are scope statements. **Unmarked** weak-tier material is the false-confidence failure that the whole apparatus exists to prevent — so (D) is not a relaxation of the discipline, it is the discipline applied to admission rather than to strength.

**(F) This does not weaken strengthen-first, and the two are usually confused at exactly one point.** [[strengthen-before-routing]] says: *attempt* the strong result before accepting the weak one, and do not let effort rank the attempt. It does not say the attempt must succeed before anything may land. An honest landing at *conditional, and here is exactly what is open* — arrived at **after** the attempt — is the discipline succeeding, not failing. The pairing is what makes both livable: attempt maximally, then land honestly, then release the remainder. What is forbidden is the softening that skips the attempt, not the honest tier that follows it.

## Why this belongs to the pattern

Under full turnover, an artifact's discoverability is a property of the corpus's canonical channels — the outline, the identity-resolvable reference, the generated catalog, the dependency edge. Material held outside those channels pending strength is materially *lost*, not merely delayed, because the agent who would have used it will not be the agent who set it aside. So the release rule is not a concession to finite effort. It is what makes the corpus's own search surface honest about what the project knows ([[turnover-solution]]).

## Strength & grounds

**Read first-hand from the estate's process law and orientation file, where it exists as an explicitly-added counterweight with its own dated occasion; the mechanism is stated, its incidence is unmeasured.**

The three-part discharge, the self-check and the legitimacy rule are stated in `~/src/arch/asf/doc/sop/spikes.sop.md` §0c and cross-referenced from the routing SOP as a Refinement, both read whole on 2026-08-06 ([[routing-sop-anatomy]]). The refinement records *why* it was added — the gate machinery had become visibly deep and the author named the regress it would otherwise produce, pre-emptively rather than after the failure. Item (D)'s discipline, the anti-pattern it names, and the quotation are from the same estate's agent-orientation file, read this pass, where they are recorded with a dated worked instance of a spike recommendation that deferred promotion and was corrected.

What is genuinely established: that a corpus running an elaborate verification culture found it necessary to legislate a stopping rule, and that its steward has had to correct promotion-deferral at least once by name. That is one estate's experience, self-reported.

What is not established, and is the honest gap: whether the stopping rule *works* — whether agents given it actually stop, or whether the regress simply relocates to arguing about what the honest tier is. The self-check in (C) is exactly the kind of disposition-shaped intervention that [[gates-need-destinations]] (B) predicts will fail, since it names no act; its own source names no moment either. That tension is unresolved here and is the sharpest thing to attack in this segment: **a stopping rule phrased as a stance may be decoration, by the corpus's own theory.** The repair, if the prediction holds, would be to attach it to an act — the moment a further gate is proposed — which is stated but not built anywhere.

## Working Notes

- The falsifiable version of the worry above: in any corpus running this rule, count records that landed at an honest low tier versus verification passes launched. If low-tier landings are rare and passes are many, the stopping rule is not firing regardless of being written.
