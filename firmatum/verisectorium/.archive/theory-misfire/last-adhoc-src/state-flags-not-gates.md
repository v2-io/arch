# State flags are not promotion gates

*Progress on a segment is recorded as independent, resettable flags about what is true of the work *now* — not as rungs on a one-way ladder that must be climbed to “finish.”*

## The claim

A **gate** (in the sense that matters here) is a check that must pass before a *destination* is allowed: before a package is submitted, before a file may be committed, before a claim is treated as externally challengeable. Gates have stakes and a place the work is *going*.

A **state flag** answers a different question: *what is currently known about this atom?* Explored? Drafted? Dependencies checked? Citations checked? Those flags can be true or false independently, can reset when the body is edited, and do not imply a single next destination. Editing a carefully checked segment is advancement of truth, not demotion on a ladder — so a check flag that *resets on edit* is honest; a rung that forbids going “backwards” is not.

The failure mode of treating flags as gates is estate-visible: a multi-rung promotion ladder looks like process rigor, but when nobody needs the terminus, the middle rungs become ceremonial and the real work happens in landing, refining, and flag-shaped stage values that never complete the climb. The specimen for that pattern — vivarium entirely at `draft`, ASF mid-ladder without ever reaching the documented top — is [[ladder-never-fired]].

**What follows for this corpus (and recommended for the pattern):** prefer a small vocabulary of **independent state flags** that reset when the claim text changes, in service of honesty about the current atom. Reserve **gates** for places with a real destination (publish, merge across a membrane, orientation before write-access). Do not design a long one-way stage ladder and expect it to be the metabolism of daily work.

This is a formulation about process design, not a proof that gates are useless. Gates with destinations remain load-bearing (see orientation before commit, anonymization before submission).

## Strength & grounds

**Heuristic, from process design plus one clear occupancy specimen.** The distinction gate-vs-flag matches how enforcement profiles already split (stakes × reversibility elsewhere in the estate). The specimen that the *long ladder as lived process* fails is [[ladder-never-fired]] (counts and method there). Shared authorship of the observation and the formulation is acknowledged: this is not multi-author corroboration of a social fact, only a clean measurement of stage fields on two trees on one day, used to license a design preference.
