# Respectful pedagogy: mental model first, precision second — and the analog must be isomorphic

*Framing-level prose leads with a scaffold a reader can think with, then gives the precise structure as a second layer; the scaffold is owed to the reader, and a scaffold that overclaims is worse than none.*

## The claim

Where the material is dense but the findings matter, the reader is owed a **mental model before the formalism**: an opening layer, in plain language, from which a thoughtful non-specialist could re-derive the qualitative claim without the symbols. The precise structure follows as its own layer. Three disciplines keep this from decaying into ornament:

- **The scaffold comes first and stands alone.** It is not a summary written after, bolted on top; it is the first thing framing-level prose does (outline preambles, introductions, plain-language briefs), and it must survive being read by itself.
- **The analog must be isomorphic, not merely evocative.** A reader perturbing the analog must get predictions that hold against the formalism — every joint of the picture maps to a named piece of the real structure. An analog that is vivid but wrong-jointed teaches confidently false intuitions, which is why *scaffolding that overclaims is worse than none*.
- **One tight layer, not everything twice.** The scaffold is a paragraph, then precision; "explain everything twice" doubles maintenance and lets the two drift.

"Respectful" is load-bearing: the posture treats the reader's attention and prior knowledge as real constraints to serve, not obstacles to survive — and its origin evidence is a sympathetic expert reader whose attention academic register lost, and who, given a well-jointed analog, worked the qualitative result out unprompted on first encounter. That unprompted re-derivation is the diagnostic to aim for.
