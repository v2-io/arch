## 6. Corrections, and things found on the way

These are the items I would flag as most likely to change a decision rather than fill in a body.

**(a) A strengthening this project's own law wants.** [[integration-metabolism]] states the delete-test as *"delete it; what is lost?"* — and notes that anyone can run it, which is its virtue. vivarium's fifth graduation rule identifies the hole: ***"'Looks complete' and 'claim homes exist' are not provenance."*** A delete-test evaluated on an artifact's **present state** cannot detect content lost at an *earlier* pass, because a partially-mined file and a fully-mined one look identical when you are checking whether the claims you can see have homes. Under multi-pass integration — which is this project's normal case — that is exactly where loss occurs. The repair is a diff against the last known-good pre-mining parent, and it is cheap wherever version control is present. Carried in [[source-tree-graduation]]; **I did not edit [[integration-metabolism]]**, because it is another chunk's drafted segment and a parallel batch is running. It is the lead's call whether that segment absorbs the refinement or cites it.

**(b) R46's "middle rung is unbuilt" is not quite right.** The entry says gate dispositions and tribunal records are one schema family at three altitudes and that the *meso* rung is the unbuilt one. Having read the SOPs: the meso rung's **vocabulary** is built and heavily exercised — thirteen named per-finding dispositions plus a six-state spike set, each state minted by an occasion where a coarser vocabulary would have recorded something false ([[routing-sop-anatomy]]). What is unbuilt is that these dispositions are **not emitted as events**; they exist as a language and as prose in ledgers. That is a smaller and much more tractable gap than "unbuilt," and it changes the shape of the work: the schema does not need designing, it needs an emitter. I left R46 undischarged and did not override [[observable-crossings]]'s deliberate decision to park the schema until tried once — that judgment still looks right to me, and this correction makes the trial cheaper rather than unnecessary.

**(c) R-T3 re-measured, first-hand, 2026-08-06.** vivarium's council ledger stands at **1,697 lines / 160 decision blocks** — *identical* to the 2026-08-05 figures. The ledger did not grow in a day, so "status quo until it hurts" has not moved. Worth recording because the entry frames it as a commitment with a clock, and the clock is currently stopped.

**(d) A live counter-position [[rule-grounds-and-posture]] has to survive**, found while verifying an N12 quote. comproprium's FORMAT opens: *"Nothing in this document has authority beyond being true. A rule found false is corrected here, not obeyed."* That **refuses the posture axis outright** — a coherent alternative design, not a weaker version. It is now stated in the segment as the position the claim must survive, with the tension named: that corpus inherits its conventions through a three-link chain (comproprium ← udon theory ← asf), so it is saying "correct it here rather than obey it" about rules it did not author and cannot fix upstream.

**(e) Two of my own numbers were wrong and are fixed.** I asserted "43 carryovers co-resident with **72 live** segments" — 72 is the *total*; only 29 are live. And I inherited a "1,405-line changelog" figure from R27 without checking it; the repository that entry names has a **178**-line changelog, and the 1,417-line one I could verify is in a different repository altogether (asf). Both corrected before this report. Recording them because they are the same defect twice — a count read from a register rather than re-run — which is the failure mode `provenance-registers` exists to catch, committed while writing about it.
