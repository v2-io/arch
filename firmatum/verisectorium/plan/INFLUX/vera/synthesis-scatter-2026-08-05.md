## How they relate (provisional)

- Strand 1 is **architecture**: where truths live in a logozoetic mind.  
- Strand 2 is **communal methodology catalog**: precepts that keep *working minds* truthful under task pressure (completion compulsion, caveat-substitutes-for-repair, …) — a verisectorium *instance* whose atom is “precept,” not “claim theorem.”  
- Strand 3 is **design research** that tried to make epistemological structure first-class (ennaos era) — richer than either the thin ontology line or the later comproprium precepts alone.  
- Strand 4 is **calibration infrastructure** for expressing confidence (verbal probabilities, etc.) — feeds the *how we mark uncertainty* problem that VERA and tribunal both care about.

Do not treat comproprium `ver-*` segments as the PROPRIUM VERA store, or ennaos VERA as ratified PROPRIUM law.
