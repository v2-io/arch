# The brief is a per-record field with a pass test, and it is written last

*Every record can carry a plain-language statement of what it establishes, held to one criterion — a thoughtful non-specialist re-derives the qualitative claim from it without the formal apparatus — which makes it checkable rather than decorative, and which most records will fail.*

## The claim

[[pedagogy-layering]] governs framing prose: lead with a mental model, then give the precise structure. That discipline lives in introductions and preambles, where an author chooses to apply it. The complementary move is to make it **a field on every record**, so the question *can this be said plainly* is asked once per claim rather than once per document.

**The field is not a summary.** A summary compresses the record; a brief re-expresses it in a register the record does not use, and the two have different pass conditions. The brief's test is the sharp one: a thoughtful non-specialist, given only the everyday analog, should be able to **re-derive the qualitative claim** — not agree that it sounds right, not restate it, derive it. That is checkable by a person in a minute, which is what separates this from an aspiration.

**The diagnostic to aim for is unprompted re-derivation by an outside reader.** The canonical instance in this estate came from a sympathetic non-specialist working the qualitative result out for himself on first encounter, from a household analog, before seeing the formalism. That is the standard: not that the analog is vivid, but that someone perturbing it gets predictions that hold — the isomorphism requirement [[pedagogy-layering]] states, applied at record grain.

**It is written last, and the ordering is a fact about the work rather than about discipline.** The brief becomes possible when the formalism has stabilized enough for its load-bearing structure to be legible in plain language; attempted earlier, it either fails or fixes a framing the record has not earned. This inverts the usual expectation that the plain statement is the easy part done first.

**Most records will not have one, and the honest posture is to say so.** In the estate's own live instance, the standard is explicitly named as unmet by most records. A field that is aspirational-and-known-empty is more useful than one that is quietly filled with restatements, because the empty case is a visible backlog and the filled case is invisible decay. This is the same argument [[absence-as-structure]] makes generally: marked absence beats false structure.

**The failure mode is specific and worth writing into the convention.** Pressure to produce a tight, unqualified plain statement pushes authors toward **overclaiming for concision** — the qualifications that make the claim true are exactly what the register strips. So the convention has to require *load-bearing but not overclaiming*, and the brief has to be read as a pointer into the record rather than as a standalone assertion. A brief that would be wrong if quoted alone has failed even if it reads beautifully. The general form of this trap: honesty apparatus produces heavy reading load, the layer that relieves the load is the layer with no room for apparatus, and the relief is bought with accuracy unless the convention names the price.

## Strength & grounds

**Heuristic, inherited from a live discipline at a stated remove.** The field and its criterion are ASF's, stated in `~/src/arch/asf/doc/sop/agents.sop.md` (Working Conventions, read first-hand 2026-08-06): the per-record Brief field aspires to the Feynman criterion, the benchmark named as whether *"a thoughtful non-specialist can re-derive the qualitative claim from the everyday analog the Brief reaches for, without seeing the symbols"*; the canonical example is attributed to an outside reader's unprompted first-encounter derivation; the file states plainly that the standard is high, that *"most segments do not yet meet it"*, and that reaching it *"often produces the Brief last, after the formalism stabilizes."* The overclaiming-for-concision risk is **inherited at a further remove** — it is a proposal's self-named risk in the same corpus's architectural portfolio, recorded there as unexamined, and this segment preserves that status rather than promoting it.

Single estate, one implementation, one origin anecdote. **No measurement exists** of whether briefs are re-derivable in practice — the criterion has never been run against readers, which is notable because it is the rare pedagogy claim that is cheaply testable: show a brief to someone outside the field and ask what follows.
