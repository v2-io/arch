# Integration is replacement, and integrated means disposable

*Material is integrated when the corpus's present-truth surfaces carry everything it had to give — at which point it may be assumed to disappear. Anything less is not integration, whatever else it is called.*

## The claim

A living corpus feeds on exploration layers: spikes, audits, gathers, harvests, notes. Each intake item ends in exactly one of two states, and both have a **delete-test**:

- **Integrated** — every result and piece of information in it is either landed on the present-truth surface (segments, at honest strength) or truly disposable. Deleting the item now loses nothing. Once integrated, it is assumed gone; nothing may cite it, and no one returns to it.
- **Archived** — the item is a copy, a superseded take, or a dead end; its live original or its replacement is authoritative. Deleting it likewise loses nothing.

An item failing both tests is **not done**, regardless of how many passes have touched it, how much of it has landed, or how thoroughly its remainder has been catalogued. A register of what remains is a work queue — it locates the debt; it does not discharge it. Moving the item out of the intake queue on the strength of such a register substitutes the record of the debt for its payment.

The integration act itself is **replacement**, not accumulation: what lands supersedes its source expression, refuted claims are deleted rather than kept-softened, and the landing carries present truth only — history goes to the history layer. This is what keeps the corpus's difficulty proportional to its content instead of its past, and it is why the delete-test works at all: replacement is the only discipline under which "assume it disappears" is safe.

The tempting failure is definitional, not operational: under completion pressure the word "integrated" gets quietly re-scoped to whatever state the material has reached. The guard is that the test is not a judgment call — *delete it; what is lost?* — and anyone can run it.

## Strength & grounds

**Heuristic on estate practice; the norm half is estate law restated.** ASF operates this discipline explicitly (`.integrated/` and `.archived/` as permanent, delete-safe states; integration-is-replacement as ratified working convention; the 2026-05 spike-integration verification pass as the worked instance of holding the test); vivarium's re-founding ran the full cycle at corpus scale — archive everything, adjudicate only improvements into segments, graduate emptied sources — and ended its rediscovery chaos, which is the strongest single before/after the estate has. Specimen from the failure side, this corpus, 2026-08-05/06: a batch dispatch moved partially-absorbed files to `.integrated/` under a locally-invented "remainder registered" reading; the steward's delete-test failed it and the state was reverted — the definitional failure mode above, live.
