# Instantiation is derivation, not template-picking

*Every choice a generated instance receives must trace to a principle and to the deployment's answers — never to "that's what the template had."*

## The norm

When the instantiation machinery (ch. 11) builds a new verisectorium from a deployment's answers, each generated choice — atom vocabulary, epistemic fields, state flags, membrane placements, view set, orientation scaffold — carries its **derivation**: which principle (ch. 7 / II) and which answer produced it. Two consequences are the point:

- **Customization has a method.** A deployment that needs something different doesn't fork a template; it changes an *answer* and re-derives, or challenges the *principle* — and either move lands in the right place (its ONTOLOGY, or this corpus's segments) instead of drifting silently.
- **The generated instance can defend itself.** An agent asking "why does this corpus gate here and not there" gets a chain, not a shrug — which is what lets instances evolve without each change being a fresh ad-hoc argument.

The failure this forbids has a body of evidence: every estate instance that copied a sibling's forms without the reasons re-derived either diverged silently (four outline dialects from one shape), carried law it never exercised (gap rules with zero gaps; the ladder that never fired), or kept vocabulary that didn't fit its atoms until a painful local re-cut. Copying forms transmits the *shape*; only derivation transmits the *fit*.

Decided as the standard the generator must meet; not truth-apt; overturn expressly. It deliberately does not specify the question-set or the mechanism — those are the ch. 11 gap, and they are Joseph-and-lead work.

## Strength & grounds

The norm operationalizes, for instantiation, what the evidence sections of [[strength-ladders]] and the instance health record repeatedly show about copied-without-reasons forms; the positive precedent is comproprium's divergence-only FORMAT (inherit by reference, state only reasoned deltas — "an inlined definition is not a copy, it is a fork"), which is derivation-not-templates practiced by hand, live and read first-hand.
