# Absence is marked in structure, without predicting content

*What is missing is written into the outline as a bare, visible discontinuity — never as an inventory of imagined future segments, and never left as a hole nobody can see.*

## The norm

Collision detects contradictions among claims that exist; it is structurally blind to claims nothing makes. Missing structure therefore needs its own mechanism, and the mechanism is the **bare gap**: a row in the outline stating only that a discontinuity exists — what precedes, what follows, why the link is unearned, and the main topic to establish. A gap carries **no identity** (nothing may depend on or cite it), **no count** of the segments that will fill it, and **no typed inventory** of imagined content. If a single next segment is genuinely known well enough to name, it gets a real row instead.

Both halves are load-bearing:

- **Unmarked absence is a hole nobody can see.** A corpus whose outline reads as complete when regions are missing trains every reader to trust a false shape — and rows-with-no-file, the degenerate form, break dependency resolution silently.
- **Over-marked absence is false structure.** Keyed pretend-slots and predicted inventories are a proxy wearing verification's clothes: a wall of named future segments reads as a nearly-finished claim layer and is actually a guess. Visibility without prediction is the whole rule.

The norm extends past outlines: do-not-resurrect residue stores (refuted formulations named so they aren't rediscovered), named "not copied" lists on gathers, and census rows for open problems are the same act — the absence made part of the structure, with only what is actually known about it.

Decided for this corpus (the `--GAP--` row semantics in ONTOLOGY are this norm's local form); recommended for the pattern. Not truth-apt; overturn expressly.

## Supporting observations

The estate's absence handling is where its corpora actually rot, measured on one day across the family: the strongest-worded gap law coexisting with zero gap rows in its own 115-segment corpus; two components expressing absence as 20 dangling outline rows that silently break `depends:`; and the retired possibility-outline whose keyed gap-slots were deleted as false completeness — the over-marking failure, caught and reversed in the udon theory corpus (live: `~/src/arch/firmatum/udon/v2/theory/`, whose `norm-gap-as-discontinuity` is this norm's prior landing under shared authorship).
