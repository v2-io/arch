## What landed

Eight files in `plan/last-adhoc-src/`, six of them new chapter-level claims and two new appendix specimens.

**Chapter 1 — atom**

- **`atom`** (`def`) — the unit, defined by four separable properties: one topic; a stable identity; a declared type from a local vocabulary; a present-truth body with declared companions. Explicitly stipulative, with the substantive claims routed to the segments that carry them. States what an atom is *not* (not a file, not a section, not a fixed grain) and fixes the vocabulary question (segments here, atoms at pattern level, local kinds in deployments).
- **`slug-identity`** (`form`) — identity is the slug and carries nothing else. Three grounding legs of different kinds, plus the honest competitors (order-in-filename; pure-graph-without-a-view) stated as trades rather than errors, plus the scope limit that stability is not survival.
- **`atom-grain-parallelism`** (`form`) — grain is the unit of concurrency, of context, and of truth-attachment, and the pressures conflict. Includes this corpus's own boundary case: fine grain isolates edits to existing records and does nothing for the slug *namespace*, which is where the 2026-08-05 batch actually collided.
- **`rename-survival-and-families`** (`form`, **drafted into the ch. 1 gap region**) — retirement-not-deletion (aliases resolve forever, emission writes under the cited name), siblings-link-never-merge with a *mechanical* duplicate test, non-idempotent merging (the regrowth gate), and the two live strategies (alias vs rewrite-the-world) with the condition that decides between them.
- **`identity-regime-archaeology`** (`obs`, appendix) — ASF's TST component holding three identity regimes physically side by side, with counts and one claim traced across all three.

**Chapter 2 — order**

- **`outline-as-organizing-principle`** (`form`) — index is derived, outline is authored; atomization relocates the exposition layer rather than removing it; the outline is the front door and carries the connective argument; it is one view among possible many but the one that is *maintained*.
- **`outline-as-cognition`** (`form`) — structural judgments about a corpus are made *as* outline edits; absence becomes a statement only in the view; the view is small enough to reason over and the corpus is not; hence authored, not rendered. **The row's proposed phrasing is walked back inside the segment as an overreach** — see §Corrections owed below.
- **`navigation-relocation-specimen`** (`obs`, appendix) — the estate's earliest one-claim-per-file argument, made on navigation grounds and expecting the scaffolding to become unnecessary, against the corpus that resulted.
