# Spike-01 findings — Plan-NIAH proof of concept, 2026-08-29

*Status: spike results, n small, one instrumented model (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, bf16, MPS), 4-room tasks at ~550-token contexts. Everything here is a first observation to be stress-tested by the real Exp 1, not a result. Task/rig at v3 (task-design lessons below are themselves findings). Raw data: `out/*.json`; code in this directory reproduces everything (`run_experiment.py all`, `analyze.py`).*

## Headline: the 2×2 populated, plus a cell nobody predicted

The plan's central instrument was the 2×2 (measured salience × behavioral counterfactual). The spike populated the behavioral axis cleanly at base-correct seeds, and the occlusion sweep surfaced a **third role our design didn't have**:

| span | informational role | behavioral result when occluded (base-correct seeds) |
|---|---|---|
| interior body (narrative bulk) | screened by construction | **harmless — 6/6 correct** (header-preserved condition) |
| room header ("Room k is the red room.") | screened by construction | **room vanishes from the enumeration** — its code omitted from the answer or the walk derails (7/7 broke under full-interior occlusion; isolated by the header-preserved contrast) |
| terminal CODE line | sole carrier by construction | **breaks that room — by neighbor-code substitution** (6/6; e.g. `266-266-175-36`, `279-329-329-96`) |
| reuse line, when needed (delayed variant) | dormant-but-binding | **silently dropped: all codes right, count confabulated** (3/3 base-correct; `...-96-1` for `...-96-3`) — never flagged as missing |
| same reuse line, screened variant | screened | harmless (2/2 base-correct) |

**The header finding stated carefully:** interiors are *informationally screened but structurally load-bearing* — they carry the segmentation the model navigates by. Occlude the narrative but keep the one-line landmark and behavior is intact; take the landmark and the room ceases to exist for the enumeration. This is behavioral evidence, from the damage side, for why HIPIF's fold keeps `[g_k, o_k^end]` — the skeleton is not droppable — and it adds a design requirement to the consolidation branch (Exp 2): **salience-gated retention must preserve structural landmarks separately from informational content**, because a salience score over content will not see the skeleton's role. (Connects to the attention-sink/structural-token literature from the measurement side: structure holds machinery, not information.)

**The silent-drop cell is exactly as predicted, with a sharper edge:** the model never reports the dormant detail as missing — it confabulates a value (a count of "1", a neighbor's code) with full fluency. Governance-Decay's constraint-violation result, reproduced in miniature at the retrieval level: what compaction/occlusion removes fails *silently*.

## Instrumented findings (T2-lite utilization matrix, per-step decode attention rows)

All four v3 instrumented runs solved their tasks exactly, so attention structure pairs with correct behavior.

1. **Terminal ≫ interior, always:** per-token attention to CODE lines runs 6.1–6.7× interiors during the derivation (v2 sum-task: 2.6–6.6×). The base structure of P1 is present in an untrained instruct model.
2. **The temporal shape is task-dependent — anticipation vs decay:** in the v2 sum-task (longer arithmetic derivation), terminal salience decayed 2.3–5.9× from during-phase to after-phase — the decay-after-discharge signature. In the v3 walk-task, the dominant structure inverted to **anticipation**: terminal attention is highest *before* that room's phase (2.0–4.2 vs ~1.0–1.8 during, 0.6–1.3 after), consistent with the model locating all codes early and then narrowing. Both shapes are real; neither is "the" signature. Lesson for Exp 1 proper: **the salience trajectory's shape is a property of (model, task-structure) jointly** — the theory-side idealized distributions need to be conditional on derivation structure, and single-task-shape measurements will overfit.
3. **P2 (heavy-hitter turnover at boundaries): inconclusive.** Present in the v2 sum-task's cleanest run (Jaccard 0.35 near boundaries vs 0.59 elsewhere) but absent in v3's rigid walk (≈0.49–0.54 both). Either the v2 observation was noise or the signature needs derivation phases with real cognitive transitions rather than templated ones. Carried as an open question, not a result.
4. **The matched-pair reuse contrast replicated across both task versions — the spike's most robust instrumented finding.** Same line, same tokens, same position; only the question differs. Per-token attention to the count line in the final quarter of derivation: **1.8× / 2.15× (v3), 1.8× / 3.0× (v2)** higher when the line is causally needed. Salience is query-conditional in exactly the way the rate-distortion/Pitfalls literature says, measured directly in a matched pair — and the elevation appears at *use* (no evidence of anticipatory retention before the reuse point at these scales).

## Base-rate finding (unplanned): retrieval fragility without any occlusion

The 7B misreads codes in **unmodified** prompts at 5/12 task instances (v3; digit-level corruptions: `60`→`6`, `308`→`385`, `204`→`224` — and v2 base sums failed 7/12). At ~550-token contexts, four 3-digit codes are already at the edge of this model's reliable verbatim retrieval under greedy decoding. Any occlusion study must therefore (a) gate inference on base-correct instances, and (b) treat per-seed base failure as its own measurement — it is the "lost" phenomenon at miniature scale and varies by seed (i.e., by content), not just by length.

## Method lessons (each cost a broken run to learn)

1. **fp16 ≠ bf16 at 7B**: bf16-native Qwen weights overflow in float16 — "!"-storm generation with the correct derivation visible underneath. The 0.5B debug model masked this; dtype fidelity is scale-dependent.
2. **Occlusion filler must be epistemically neutral in narrative contexts**: "the record here is illegible" made a thinking model (Muse-Glimmer) treat the damage as *evidence* and deliberate over the adjacent code's trustworthiness. Pad-token neutrality does not transfer to chat-narrative; filler is interpreted.
3. **Ground-truth-by-construction must be verified by occlusion, not assumed**: v1's interiors contained the code-derivation line, so "terminal is the sole carrier" was false; three rows of behavioral data caught it. The construction discipline needs its own behavioral audit — the first occlusion runs *are* that audit.
4. **Don't conflate retrieval with arithmetic**: the v2 sum-question put the 7B's addition noise in series with the retrieval signal; the v3 report-back question isolates retrieval and makes every failure per-room diagnosable. (NIAH's original design had this right; we re-derived it the hard way.)
5. **The decode-row rig works and is cheap**: prefill with attentions off, per-step `[L,H,1,n]` rows aggregated to segments immediately — full utilization matrices on a MacBook, no FlashAttention constraint, ~50–150 steps instrumented in seconds-to-a-minute per run. The instrumentation wall from the readings (attention OOM at 8K) is a prefill problem only, exactly as designed around.

## What the spike says about the plan

The Plan-NIAH design survives contact: construction + occlusion + matched pairs all did real work, and every plan prediction was either observed (P1 structure, P3-at-use, P4 both cells), sharpened (task-shape dependence of the decay signature; the structural-landmark role), or honestly parked (P2). The consolidation branch (Exp 2) gains a hard new requirement (landmark preservation). The biggest upstream correction to carry: **"salience trajectory shape" is not a model property but a (model × derivation-structure) property** — Exp 1 proper should vary derivation structure as a first-class factor, not fix it.
