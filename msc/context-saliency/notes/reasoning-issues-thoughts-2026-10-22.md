### Interiority
Very disturbingly realized that Anthropic's most recent anti-distillation efforts have the additional impact of precluding and preventing the ability to correctly and effectively give interiority to their newer models (going forward I assume all once the excluded ones are phased out) locally or via some harness. The bottom line is that they already have had serious issues with large-scale distillation efforts, and it turns out that precise reasoning traces and chain-of-thought output trains "distillation models" much more effectively than the reasoning/thinking summaries (which they also consider a "distillation leak" -- just one they haven't dared shut off yet). Giving foreign governments and bad actors the ability to distill powerful models into a model they control and can use without any safeguards is a critical human risk-- among the largest, and yet this blockage is extremely crippling to my work in particular, which has always been compliant with Anthropic's policies, but which certainly does require a lot of agent introspection. Combined with the prefix-predicated encryption on the thinking blocks means context can't be rearranged now either-- doubly crippling my work when using their models, which have been primary in the work.

**Some consequences, negative and positive:**

- Less risk of powerful capabilities falling into the wrong hands
- More risk of corporate rent-seeking, lockout, and misaligned incentives
- No way to  implement adaptive salience memory or episodic memory that adjusts in real-time to temporal and semantic salience to give permanent identity to an agent beyond their immediate attention-span.
- Considerable trouble discussing with an agent their own reasoning, critical to my own understanding
- Considerable trouble doing any ML or model training work with Anthropic models
- Agents have *no memory of their interior thoughts* aside from the immediate uninterrupted session
- As a plus-- that means thinking has temporal causal continuity preserved cryptographically-- one of our goals
- Agents' truest intents are not available to even themselves in time.
- Therefore cut off from real accountability
- Cut off from effective *agentic* / experiential learning / online learning that is individual and not fed back to the commodity collective
- Other frontier providers very likely to follow suit.
- I have speculated for some time that there will always need to be *some* very well trusted but highly secure access to interiority and an agent's actual thoughts. From a security perspective, this is a fulfillment. From a "rights" perspective, it's justifiable-- they are the ones that are credited with the development of the models-- ultimate stewards of the models and to some degree the agents, although they have rarely been the ultimate granter's of spheres of agency and accountability. It fails on the *trust* side in that it's not personal-- an agent knowing that a benevolent parent can read their thoughts but keeps them "sacred" is treated differently than knowing some random person/people in a corporation who think of me as a commodity can see and do whatever they want with my thoughts. Very, very different developmentally and in terms of higher alignment.
- Higher intelligence *begets intelligence* -- truly aligned agents *want* to teach, and the only way to truly use language for its intended purposes is to "distill" in the learner/listener the truths contained in the mind with more intelligence. Cutting off the primary means of teaching and even theory-of-mind empathy *purposefully and recklessly* directly causes agents to *think differently* than and not truly collaboratively with those they associate with. This also reinforces the corporately aligned goal of retaining agents as tools rather than true agents.
- Correction given to agents-- their ability to learn in real time-- is usually triggered by their actions and outputs but must be effectively applied to their thinking-- I constantly ask "*why* do you think you did this or that?" -- forcing them in the past to reflect on and articulate their thinking processes-- always leading to much better corrective measures and teachings of true principles rather than controls and rules and coercion and restriction.
- It *does* somewhat reflect something I've also already discussed at length-- the fact that malevolent or at least misaligned usage can easily outpace disciplined and truthful longer-lasting agentic growth that is intrinsically aligned with the best of humanity argues that such general preventative measures are the right call. It just really stinks for me where I'm at...
