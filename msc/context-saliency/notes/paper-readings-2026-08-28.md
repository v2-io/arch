## 1. Fragile Truth of Saliency (zhang2025fragiletruth, NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight) — the instrument stress test

**The stress test**: NIAH adapted for saliency — secret message with out-of-vocabulary keywords in distractor text; ground-truth saliency distribution is decidable **by construction** (only system prompt + secret + query can be causal). Metric: Hit Ratio = saliency mass on true-causal regions / total. This construction discipline is the paper's deepest export: *build the task so ground truth is a property of construction, not annotation*.

**The damage, quantified** (Tab. 1, three models): every one of seven methods is bad, and it isn't a long-context-only failure — **even at 100-token contexts over half the attribution mass lands on irrelevant tokens**. At 4-10K, methods approach the random baseline. Misattribution isn't uniform noise: it latches onto high-frequency function words and syntactic boundaries — structural prominence conflated with causal contribution (the sink/massive-activation confound made visible at the measurement layer).

**⚠ Correction to dossier §6.2**: **AT2 is one of the seven broken methods** — 21.2% hit ratio at 1K (Llama-2-7B), 6.6% at 4K, 5.6% at 10K (Mistral). The dossier's "best cost profile located" framing stands for *cost*; the *fidelity* claim ("near-ablation quality") does not survive this stress test. AT2 remains the best of the cheap observational family — it just isn't good. (Dossier now carries this annotation.)

**ABO mechanism**: one learnable scalar bias α_i per input token, **shared across all heads and layers** (per-head/layer variants were unstable and no better), optimize −log p(target) + λΣσ(α_i) sparsity; report the *learned bias*, not attention+bias (isolating the intervention from structural confounds). Causal by construction, per-example optimization (cost + run-to-run variance are its stated weaknesses). Hit ratios 15-45% — best available, **still far from 1.0**. The field's best instrument recovers under half the causal mass at 1K tokens. Any experimental design that treats a saliency score as ground truth is building on sand; scores are *rankings to be validated behaviorally*, never measurements.

**Two bonus results directly relevant to us**:
- **Contradictory-secrets experiment**: three conflicting secrets in context; model reliably picks the *first* (positional bias); injecting a growing attention-logit bias toward the second deterministically flips the answer (~14%→~40% P(Vin), curves cross). This is **precept-weighted salience already demonstrated as mechanism** — attention-logit bias is a working lever for which memory the model uses. Crude, external, per-token — but the lever exists.
- **Error correction**: 45/50 GSM8K failures fixed by manually biasing attention to one overlooked keyword ("remaining"). Attentional oversight, not capability deficit — misalignment of salience, repairable by salience intervention.

## 2. AGMR (agmr2026mechanistic) — the working salience-guided memory loop

**What the full text adds over the dossier**:
- **Retrieval-head supervision-set construction is the reusable protocol**: mine tasks that *fail without memory but succeed with it*; have an LLM analyzer locate the key step and the memory snippet supporting it; score every head by attention from the pre-step last token to that snippet; take top-5. Natural supervision, no hand labels. Directly transferable to building *plan-structure* head sets (fail-without-plan-context vs succeed-with).
- **Two causal backstops, both cheap**: (1) masking the top heads removes most of the memory-induced performance gain while random heads don't (validates head selection); (2) masking high-utilization segments hurts, low-utilization doesn't (validates the matrix). Every observational signal in our pipeline should carry an analog of both.
- **The utilization-matrix recipe**: exclude observation tokens from segments (length variance would swamp the signal); rescale by current prompt length n_t (attention renormalizes as context grows — without this, later steps look artificially cold); aggregate over top-5 heads only. Three practical corrections we'd otherwise rediscover painfully.
- **The failure taxonomy is a coarse ancestor of our 2×2**: attended-but-wrong / missed-relevant / distracted-by-irrelevant / low-utilization-redundant. Note what it lacks: no counterfactual axis — "low utilization" is trusted as "safe to simplify" after a semantic-redundancy check plus **re-execution verification** (commit only if re-run still succeeds; rollback otherwise). Re-execution *is* their behavioral counterfactual, run at the memory-entry level. Cheap-harness version of the 2×2's second axis.
- **Scenario-wise result sharpened**: AGMR's edge over text-only reflection concentrates in *missed* and *distracted* — exactly the failure modes invisible in trajectories (the trajectory shows the wrong action, not the attention that produced it). Text-based reflection wins slightly on attended-but-wrong (misleading *content* is visible in text). Clean division of labor between mechanistic and semantic signals.
- Head lists confirmed as stated in dossier §6.5 (Qwen2.5-7B: (17,18),(17,19),(19,15),(19,17),(19,22); Llama-3.1-8B: (13,6),(14,13),(14,29),(14,31),(13,1)); heads concentrate mid-network; offline refinement only (online is named future work).

**Tension worth holding**: AGMR builds on retrieval-head attention — an observational signal from the family Fragile Truth breaks. Why it plausibly survives: supervised head *selection* (not all-head averaging), segment-level aggregation (not per-token), length rescaling, and the masking backstop. That's a recipe: observational signals are usable *when selected, aggregated, and causally spot-checked* — not raw.

## 3. EpiKV (epikv2026epiphany) — the impulse primitive, with honest weakness

**What the full text adds**:
- **The signal is weak per-token**: best per-layer Spearman |ρ| vs counterfactual labels ≈ 0.1–0.25 (Appendix A). Sufficient to *rank for eviction* at generous budgets; **unproven as a trigger** — SASM use needs its own validation, and the dossier's "impulse primitive" framing should carry this number.
- **The counterfactual occlusion protocol is the export** (Appendix C): slide a 32-token window (stride 16) over the reasoning span of a *correct* trace; replace with padding (same context length — measures content, not position; they explicitly fixed an earlier truncation variant that proxied position and inflated attention signals); regenerate greedily; label important iff the answer flips (OR over overlapping windows). Deterministic, reproducible, **benign** ground truth. This is the closest thing to an answer for the dossier §6.2 "no benign ground truth" gap — adapt window/stride to segment boundaries for plan traces.
- **Two-band anatomy with signs**: layers 7–13 positive (mid-network retrieval/composition — ROME/MEMIT convergence), 18–25 *negative* (output-preparation layers active for fluent continuation — high change there marks *dispensable* tokens). Score = z₁₀ − z₂₁. The negative band is the non-obvious half: representational churn in late layers is anti-salience.
- **Bands move with task difficulty** (GSM8K: positive band shifts to early layers, attention-entropy and key-variance *reverse sign*): layer indices are per-regime hyperparameters, and any deployment needs a small occlusion-label calibration run per (model, task-regime). Also honest evidence the signal tracks where load-bearing computation lives rather than fixed architecture.
- **The positional-trend confound + causal rolling z-score** generalizes: *any* residual-stream signal over a long generation drifts monotonically with position; aggregate correlations overstate within-trace ranking. Detrend everything.
- **Instrumentation wall, measured**: `output_attentions=True` forces eager attention — 52GB at 4K context, OOM at 8K on an 80GB A100; FlashAttention passes reach 65K. **Full attention matrices are unavailable at exactly the lengths we care about.** Long-trace measurement = hidden-state/KV signals (free) + targeted per-head recomputation (cheap) + occlusion (offline). This constraint shapes the whole pipeline design.
- H2O's failure mode on reasoning traces is *collapse to empty output* (immediate EOS on 93/100 problems at tight budget), not graceful degradation — eviction failures can be catastrophic-mode.

## 4. HIPIF (hipif2026hierarchical) — the intervention, now with its ablation structure clear

**What the full text adds**:
- The dossier said "no ablation isolating folding" — confirmed, with precision: **w/o-Subgoal removes decomposition+folding jointly** (biggest drop, 96.1→73.9 ALFWorld avg); w/o-Reflection and w/o-Reward are separately ablated. So folding-specifically remains unisolated, but the *reflection* result matters on its own: removing the explicit completion-judgment step (z_{k,t}) costs 8+ points — **knowing when a subgoal is discharged is itself a trained capability**, and its failure mode (case study) is *premature folding* (declares subgoal 2 complete without finding the object, then acts on a fiction). The discharge judgment is exactly the screening-off decision our 2×2 formalizes; HIPIF trains it end-to-end and shows it's both necessary and non-trivial.
- **HiAgent (prompt-only folding) underperforms even ReAct** (13.6 vs 17.2 ALFWorld) — untrained models can't fold by instruction alone. And closed-source giants (GPT-4o 48.0, Gemini-2.5-Pro 60.3) sit far below trained 3B HIPIF (96.1). **Scale doesn't buy plan-structured context management; training does. A 3B with subgoal structure beats a 7B without.** For the measurement program: base models' *native* salience decay over plan structure is probably weak — the healthy signature may only appear clearly in models trained like HIPIF, which turns our validation question into a sharper comparison: does HIPIF-style training *create* the decay signature, or amplify a native one? Both models are measurable.
- **The w/o-Subgoal failure mode is the un-forgotten middle, behaviorally**: the case study agent "loses track of completed progress and incorrectly returns to the already handled toiletpaper 1 with invalid actions." Direct behavioral evidence that retained completed-subgoal detail *interferes* — the healthy-forgetting thesis from the damage side.
- Fold record is [g_k, o_k^end] (subgoal + terminal observation only); token cost roughly halves (37.8k→16.6k ALFWorld); process rewards are all rule-based penalties (ungrounded subgoal, failed terminal obs inside success, action-observation loops, format) — no reward model, no expert trajectories. Qwen2.5-3B/7B, verl-agent, 8×A100.

## 5. DefensiveKV (defensivekv2025fragility) — the validation methodology, mechanism now clear

**What the full text adds**:
- The defensive aggregation is *two linear ops*: score every entry by **max over historical-token observations** (worst-case proxy) then **floor at the per-head mean** of those maxima (Laplace-style prior correction for under-observation). That's the whole 2.3-4.3× improvement. The conceptual content: *evicting on average importance is optimizing expected return while ignoring tail risk; an entry important to even one historical query may be critical to a future one.* Directly transferable to any consolidation policy we build — **fold on worst-case-observed salience, never mean salience**, and floor by a prior when observation is thin.
- Observation window is 32 tokens because FlashAttention makes full attention-weight storage infeasible (~64GB for 32K context) — same wall as EpiKV, independently hit.
- **Evaluation compresses context *before* the question is known** (multi-turn/prefix-reuse simulation) — deliberately the query-agnostic hard case; this is the H(Q)-penalty regime from the rate-distortion survey, made concrete as an eval protocol. Our consolidation experiments should run both conditions (query-known vs query-unknown) since the delta *is* the H(Q) price, measured.
- Fragility numbers confirmed as dossier stated (worst-case retained importance 0.34 vs 0.92 average; 89 sub-random instances). The paper's Figure-1-style per-step retained-importance plot is the template for our decay-trajectory visualizations: plot the *whole trajectory* with worst-case bands, never a summary statistic.

## Cross-paper synthesis for the experiment plan

1. **Construction beats annotation for ground truth** (Fragile Truth's NIAH discipline + EpiKV's occlusion protocol): design plan-tasks where screened-off structure is decidable by construction, then use occlusion as the behavioral check. This resolves the dossier's "benign ground truth" gap for our purposes — see the plan's Plan-NIAH design.
2. **Observational signals are rankings, not measurements** — usable when (a) supervised-selected (AGMR heads), (b) aggregated above token level, (c) detrended (EpiKV z-score), (d) causally spot-checked (AGMR masking), and (e) validated worst-case-over-time (DefensiveKV). Five conditions, all cheap, none optional.
3. **The attention matrix is unavailable at scale** — the instrument stack is hidden-state/KV signals (T1, free) → named-head recomputation (T2, cheap) → occlusion/behavioral (T3, offline ground truth). Everything long-context runs on T1/T2.
4. **The discharge judgment is the crux three ways**: HIPIF trains it (and fails by premature folding when reflection is removed), Governance Decay shows summarizers proxy it wrongly, our 2×2 measures it. Same object: *is this span's future influence screened off?*
5. **Both directions of the intervention exist**: ABO shows salience is *steerable* (precept-weighting has a mechanism); AGMR shows memory can be *refined against* measured salience; HIPIF shows folding can be *trained*. The missing piece across all five remains the measurement question ASF asks: what does healthy salience structure look like, natively, and does theory predict it?
