# What of the book's machinery still means something for an agentic council

*Strengthen before soften, applied family by family: for each mechanism, a real attempt at an agentic meaning before any "no meaning" lands. Status values: **keeps** (unchanged under the common-state condition and logical time), **translates** (same purpose, different carrier, named), **instruction** (the parent's charter sets it; the council cannot), **no-go** (demonstrated: the purpose cannot arise), **guess** (a meaning I propose and have not tested). Cites read whole. The walking-skeleton spike's measured runs (coordinator, 2026-11-03) are used where they bear.*

## 1. The main question and the subsidiaries

| Mechanism | Purpose in the book | Agentic meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| main motion [10] | introduce business | special council: in order **within the referred subject** — a committee drafts [51:4]; a subject referral needs a resolution to be put, and the book's device for a choice is a blank [12:92–113]; business outside the referral is out of order [9:15 analog, 36:2]; standing council: within the class of business if the charter grants origination [50:8]. (Revision 1 excluded it; revision 2 bounded it only in prose, which admitted out-of-scope motions silently — feedback-2 §9; revision 3 compiles the bound as an applicability-row patch using the model's `germane` predicate, F17.) | keeps, bounded |
| **amend** [12] | perfect the text before deciding it | the single most load-bearing motion for a council that recommends text. **Evidence:** the walking-skeleton runs had no amend, and in every meeting the members routed around it — (a) yes-votes with dissent notes, (b) withdraw-and-substitute, (c) unanimous rejection of their own motion so the agreed text could be moved. The book has each detour named and each is worse than amend: (a) is 51:71's concur-except, a signature not a decision; (b) is 33:11–19 plus a new motion, losing the original's standing; (c) would be routed by the model's same-question bar to a `substantially-same` ruling and likely refused — a question "finally disposed of earlier in the same session by being rejected" cannot be renewed until progress makes it new [10:26(3), 38:7]. Excluding amend does not remove the need; it converts it into motions the book bars. Germaneness is adjudged → submitted → a round. | keeps (essential) |
| postpone indefinitely [11] | decline without a direct vote | "recommend that it not be adopted" is the committee's own report form [51:37, 51:44]; in-council it is the motion that produces that recommendation | keeps |
| commit [13] | send to a smaller group | sub-subcommittee on the pending question (custody moves; the council waits or recesses). Distinct from `commission-inquiry` (05), which leaves the question pending | keeps |
| postpone to a certain time [14] | defer to a set hour | defer to a round or **an event** ("until S reports") — 03 §4 | translates |
| limit / extend debate [15], previous question [16] | manage scarce meeting time | disabled in committees so the parent gets matured judgment [50:25]; agents' scarce resource is tokens, and the parent sets the budget as an *instruction* [13:8(d)] with the book's deadline-vs-budget distinction intact [15:16]. Exhaustion by a full pass round is the model's own `put-question` rule, not a divergence (01 §3 g) | instruction |
| lay on the table [17] | set aside for something urgent, restore by majority | meaningful only with more than one question before the body: standing council keeps it; special council has nothing to set the one question aside for (an urgent interruption is a question of privilege, which pushes a context [19:10], not tabling) | kind-dependent |

## 6. The larger structures

- **Question lifecycle** (`lifecycle.json`): every state and transition is event-ordered; nothing depends on the clock except the three timers mapped in 03. Keeps whole.
- **Engine** (stack, context stack, bundles): keeps whole; the context stack is exactly how an in-meeting act interrupts and resumes [19:10].
- **Scheduler**: keeps with rounds/events as hours; in a special council it holds one item (*the* special order) and is otherwise idle; in a standing council it is the agenda machinery with the parent's referrals as general orders.
- **Rules hierarchy**: keeps, and gains content. The `procedural-law` class — unsuspendable [25:8] — is where the estate's binding norms sit (truth-honoring; who-decided is never laundered; the moratorium). The `charter` class is the parent's act, above the authority [2:7 n4]. A council adopts special rules only if instructed [50:26]. Custom falls on any point of order [2:25] — and the pedagogy/crystallization path of ROADMAP phase 6 is where a council's repeated practice becomes a standing rule.
- **Sessions and meetings**: sittings and the council's life (03). The one-session rule for special committees [50:23] does most of the simplification for a quick council.
- **Committee machinery** (§§13, 36, 50–52): survives best, because it is *about* the relation the council has to its parent. In particular 13:8(d) is the charter's warrant for every agentic instruction; 13:22 for the parent's live steering; 50:15 for recursion; 51:2 for the polling fallback and for the report being text the council adopts; 52:28 for consultant returns "in the nature of debate" (its breakout groups meet during a recess — the parallel inquiry is a declared departure); 50:15 bars non-member subcommittees for a committee that only reports, which is why the inquiry is a consultant, not a subcommittee (feedback-2 §5).
- **Discipline** [61–63]: a committee can only report misconduct to its parent, except to exclude a member when there is no time [50:28]; for an agent emitting noise every round the charter can grant that exclusion power or leave it to the parent. Keeps, bounded.
- **Executive session** [9:24–27]: the parent is entitled to the record [49:19]; secrecy from Joseph is dropped by design, with its cost named — per-seat votes read by the steward are a channel for voting toward the principal, the ballot's purpose [45:18] — not vacuous (feedback §3.4). **[guess]** An agentic meaning: a sitting's *debate* (not its acts, 9:26) marked closed to other agents' future reading — out of the memorata index, unreadable by later forks — to protect the independence of later councils from prior-sampling convergence. Disposition territory, which Joseph deferred; recorded as a possible meaning, not a design.

## 7. Adversarial checks on the universal claims

- *"All but four timers are event-anchored."* Checked by inventory (03 §1): seven timers — revision 1 counted six and this self-check passed the miscount (feedback §9); `vote-challenge-window`, `renewal-unblock`, `order-exhaustion` have only `expires_on_event`. ✔ after correction
- *"Division has no function for any recorded method."* 01 §5, including the secret-method case. ✔
- *"Exhaustion by a full pass round is the model's rule."* `actions.json put-question`: third disjunct is `count of floor claims with purpose debate = 0`. A round in which no present seat claims the floor satisfies it literally — but so does the instant after stating, before any round (feedback §3.2). The charter's duty-sequencing rule supplies the timeliness the expression lacks (F11). ✔ only with the declared rule
- *"The added motions are incidental."* Checked by the second verifier (feedback-2 §9): class I routes their admission through `guard/legitimately-incidental` (residual default true) and makes each decided first over the question it arises from [6:18]; `co-opt-member`'s book analogy (19:7) would be privileged rank 10 — the charters keep class I deliberately and say the analogy is not relied on for class. And `no-order-precluding` bars only class-S motions plus commit and postpone, so 05 §1's "refused when a debate-closing order precludes it" was wrong and is removed.
- *"The absentee-protection thresholds collapse to majority when every seat is present."* 35:2(7) and 37:35(3) both say "unless all the members … who voted with the prevailing side are present or have been given reasonable notice". A seat marked absent by the backstop breaks the condition — so the collapse holds *per vote*, computed from presence, not assumed. The charter must not hard-code majority; the engine computes it. ✔ (and a correction to my own first draft of this line, which said "always")
