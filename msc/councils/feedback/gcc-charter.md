## Where it is sound

- **Sealed first round, recorded shuffle, last-in-shuffle reporter.** These are the three things two verifiers and the contention spike converged on: the sealed round is the record's only evidence that convergence was earned (hollow unanimity is the agent hazard, not contention); a recorded shuffle is a declared fair device that replays; rotating the summarizing pen keeps one reading from accumulating. It also drops the serial floor entirely, which dissolves most of what the floor-contention work had to solve.
- **The leader's sealed expected solution** is AGENTIC-DELEGATION's extrusion defense made mechanical, and it makes the 2/4/3/1 rates measurable. Keep it.
- **Proposition last.** The unity report, then the leader's record at finish, is the shape of the book's *committee* (deliberate, then adopt a recommendation, 51:36–44) and of the higher councils. The book's committee chapters fit this loop better than its assembly chapters do.
- **Hold with "what would change each dissenting seat's view."** That is the exit a hold needs; without it a hold is a veto by silence. Good that it is in.

## What a charter for this loop must declare

The spike's rule: declare only what the engine cannot compute, and reference what the server owns. For this loop that leaves:

1. **The leader seat, its grant, and what its close is.** If the leader is an agent, the record lands `deciders: leader under grant; decided-by: delegated` and nothing takes effect until the parent acts; if the leader is Joseph, the close is his act. Say which. Also whether the leader may close without unanimity at all, or only hold/escalate.
2. **Per seat: representative or perspective; for representative seats the line that re-seats it; for perspective seats what the vantage *looks at*, never what it concludes.** The first verifier caught my charters writing conclusions as perspectives, and the walkthrough's seats then argued their strings.
3. **The decision rule and the non-response rule, with the backstop.** "The council waits" for a representative seat is the whole-roll quorum that gave every seat a veto by silence in my first charter. Declare the wait's remedy (re-seat from the line after N rounds; escalate) and how many representative seats must answer for a close to stand (that is quorum, 40:5).
4. **What each sustaining level does.** Does *object* hold the item? Does *strongly object* escalate? Without a mapping the scale is a survey. Under 51:2 a committee's report contains only what every member agreed to; the scale needs a line that says which levels count as agreed.
5. **The item's scope** (the referral, or the council's purpose), so an out-of-scope first response can be named as such — the book's germaneness, the higher councils' agenda test.
6. **A report-by bound**, so hold cannot loop forever: the parent's remedy for a committee that misses its reporting time is discharge by bare majority (36:4(7)); the loop's version is "escalate to the parent with the urgency stated."
7. **The nonmember and sealing rules.** Subagents a member sends out enter only through that member's response; no nonmember writes in a round (9:36's last item). Sealing is honor-system today (the walking-skeleton found members with file tools can read sealed intents); declare where the store lives and who can read it.
8. **Pins:** the loop's version and the model's, so a run replays.
9. **The record's audience** (council and parent; never public by default) and what the decision record must carry to land in the estate's stores: deciders, decided-by, importance, reopen-when — the template has most of these.

## What the book knows that this loop would regret dropping

- **A point of order at the moment** (23:5). The loop has no way to say, in-round, "the leader just opined," "that seat is out of scope," "this round is being run wrong." Everything flows into the next sealed response, which is one round late. The book keeps this one interrupt even where it keeps nothing else; cheap to add as a preemptive intent in any round, ruled by the leader — or by the members when the breach is the leader's.
- **A recorded disposition for every recommended change.** "Leader needs to allow at least one loop … after that can do the final finish" means changes can be ignored after one loop. The book disposes of pending amendments before the main question (51:42–43 for committee reports); the walking-skeleton's runs showed what members do when there is no amend — withdraw-and-substitute, reject-your-own-motion. Require that each recommended change is accepted or declined with a reason before close.
- **Who may reopen, and at what threshold.** "What would reopen it" is in the record; the mechanism is not. The book's committee rule: no time limit, anyone not on the losing side, two-thirds unless everyone who prevailed is present (37:35). Declare something.
- **The leader does not debate** (43:29) — the template says "frame, ask, supply context," and "supply context" is the door opinion walks through. The book's answer is that the chair who must argue steps down to do it. The loop's analog: a leader's context goes into the *sealed* round as one more revealed response, marked as the leader's, so it is contestable like any other and cannot be timed to steer.
- **Obstruction.** An agent that loops holds or passes nothing useful: a committee's only remedy is to report it to the parent (50:28). Declare the escalation.

## Two places I would push back

- **The last seat in the shuffle has the most information and the summarizing pen.** For an item that goes straight to finish, that seat's reading becomes the record. Draw the reporter separately, or make it the first seat of the *next* round's shuffle.
- **Anonymous sustaining is a projection, not a fact.** Agent identity is recorded on every event by the harness; a representative seat's level is part of the decision's standing (the udon decision-authority ADR needs who decided). Anonymity is coherent only for perspective seats, and only as a view of the record.

One more, on who owns the record. The sustaining round sustains the *decision*; the record's assumptions and findings are the leader's text, which no seat adopted. Either the sustain round sustains the record as a whole, or the record is declared the leader's act (which matches the higher councils, where the presider decides and members sustain). Both are honest; the template should pick one.
