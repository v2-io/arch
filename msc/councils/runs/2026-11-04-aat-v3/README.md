Also — it's an example of a decision where both lines can proceed for a bit first, and often then the answer is apparent and obvious to all." So the item is held, with assignments. The charter and framing below are kept for that, and the framing gets a "what the pilots showed" section first. See `log.md`.

## Charter (for this run)

- **Grant and close.** The council makes a **recommendation to Joseph**, who decides. In the decision-authority vocabulary, its close is *delegated / council-accepted*, never *ratified*.
- **Importance.** High. The decision is hard to reverse and affects all of ASF. The decision rule is **unanimity sought**. An *object* at sustaining keeps the recommendation from closing as unanimous; the item then either holds or goes to Joseph with the split stated.
- **Seats.**

  | Seat | Kind | What it looks at |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1, canon | perspective | the truth of what is in ASF now: what must survive, which corrections on main matter, what a crossing could damage |
  | S2, record | perspective | the evidence history: the vsect trail and what it is worth in each path |
  | S3, founding | perspective | the system definition: record types, transitions, kinds, roles, what founding by charter would buy |
  | S4, crossing and throughput | perspective | the cost of moving work from ASF, and who actually drains each queue. Today's stall is idle stations |
  | S5, reader | perspective | the cold reader and pedagogy: findability and restatements, and what a newcomer meets in each path |
  | S6, ASF lead | representative | the session leading ASF's next phase (0903e35e). It is accountable for the work and also an author of the current plan, so it counts as an advocate on that plan, not as independent of it |

- **Tiers.** All seats are full members.
- **Members.** S1–S5 are fresh agents with no part in the councils work. None is a fork of the leader.
- **Sealing.** Delivery sealing by norm. Members write their first responses here and do not open each other's until the leader releases them.
- **Non-response.** The council waits. The backstop is the leader's judgment.
- **Record.** This directory. It is local to arch and not pushed until Joseph says so.
