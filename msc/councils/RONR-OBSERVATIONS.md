# Where the model interrogates the book

*Discussion-grade observations about RONR 12th ed. itself, surfaced by compiling it: places where formalization shows the text is patched, overloaded, or unenforceable as written. Each is defensible from the cited paragraphs; none is steward-ratified doctrine. Sources: the stage-4 audit cycle (two sampled-unprimed passes and one whole-book pass, 2026-08-27); primaries read whole for every citation below.*

1. **The "order of precedence" is not an order, and SDC-1 is not a rule.** 5:11 announces a "definite order of precedence," and each motion's SDC-1 states general takes-precedence/yields claims — which SDC-2, 6:7, 6:19–6:21, and 24:3(1) then falsify case by case (amend's positional rank; lay-on-table "takes precedence over any incidental motions pending" [17:3(1)] against the undebatable non-adhering appeal that yields to no subsidiary at all [24:3(1)]; the incidental yield rule needing a four-case matrix). The true structure is a *partial order with per-pair overrides*, and SDC-1 is a lossy summary of SDC-2. The book possesses the meta-rule that resolves this — specificity governs [3:2, 56:68(3)] — but never turns it on its own precedence layer or concedes the summary is lossy; the reader must induce the override ordering (matrix ≻ per-motion conditions ≻ rank test) from the contradictions. The model states that ordering explicitly.

2. **"Takes precedence over" is two relations wearing one name** — *may-be-moved-while-pending* and *is-decided-first*. They coincide often enough that the book never separates them, until Reconsider forces the confession: the making/consideration rank split [37:9(1a/1b)] is exactly the admission that the underlying relation was compound. The model's `applied_to` / `adheres_to` / `effective_rank` triple is the repaired vocabulary.

3. **The prevailing-side rule is honor-system in its common case.** For voice and rising votes no record exists of who prevailed; eligibility to move Reconsider rests on unverifiable assertion, and the book's own patches show it knows — the ballot-secrecy waiver, and everyone-present qualifying under unanimous consent [37:10(a)]. The formalization renders it honestly as an adjudicated claim. Notably: a logogenic council with a full event log could make this rule *actually enforceable* for the first time in its history.

4. **The renewal boundary rests on an undefined quantity.** "Progress in business or debate… sufficient to make it essentially a new question" [38:7; also 13:7(8), 14:4(8), 15:5(8), 16:5(8)] is load-bearing in five SDCs and has no criteria anywhere — the fuzziest predicate in the book at one of its highest-stakes gates (whether a defeated minority may try again). Irreducibly judgment-shaped; the book could not have compiled itself here either.

5. **Two inverted-polarity suppression votes.** Refuse-to-proceed-to-orders [18:8(a)] and objection-sustained [26:7] both implement "two-thirds to suppress consideration," each through a differently-inverted put — and 26:9 shows the book knows the polarity confuses (the chair is warned never to put the objection in sustain-form). The model's threshold vocabulary states the underlying symmetric rule once.

6. **40:7's inquorate remainder is an open class by design** — "other motions may also be considered if they are related to… the conduct of the meeting" hands the chair an unbounded relatedness test with no criteria, in the one situation (no quorum) where the assembly's power to check the chair is weakest. Faithfully routed as judgment; the observation stands as critique of the rule.

7. **One place the book skips a vote because arithmetic settles it.** If reconsideration of an adopted Previous Question prevails, the PQ is "presumed to be rejected and is not voted on again" — because only opponents would vote to reconsider, so a majority for reconsideration makes two-thirds for the PQ unattainable [16:16]. RONR doing threshold arithmetic in place of a ballot: a native precedent for an executable model.

8. **The precision is real, and it is unevenly placed.** Everywhere the audits hunted for looseness in arithmetic and timing — the quarterly-interval month-boundary rule [9:7], the two-thirds and membership arithmetic [44], the enter-minutes applicability list [37:47(3)], the four-case appeal-yield matrix [24:3(1-2)], the scheduling worked examples [41:50–55], Table VIII cell-for-cell — the text held exactly. The strain concentrates entirely in the expository precedence layer (observation 1) and the judgment predicates (observations 4, 6). A century and a half of case-law hardening went into thresholds and windows; the summary layer never got refactored against its own refinements.
