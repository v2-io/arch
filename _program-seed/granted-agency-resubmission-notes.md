# Granted-agency — resubmission strategy after the desk rejection

*2026-07-08, Fable 5 session. Status per Joseph: desk-rejected at Inquiry — length / no
reviewer willing to take a paper that long / probably clarity. The rejection frees the
paper: it is under review nowhere, shareable freely (e.g. with Forethought contacts),
and revisable without process constraints. These notes are a first-pass cut strategy
from a full read of the current 523-line build; the ~40% target is a judgment, not a
measurement.*

## Diagnosis

The paper is doing three papers' work: (1) the ACA warrant (§2's "Why these conditions,
on what warrant" re-derives asymmetric comprehension across five traditions at length),
(2) the scope conditions with full sub-scope lattice + five factors + sufficiency
argument, (3) the compact-form with six components + comparative models + CE methodology
+ responsibility. Since ACA is now a full standalone paper, (1) is duplicated weight;
and the densest, most objection-prone material in (2) is not load-bearing for the
compact-form.

## The cuts (largest first)

1. **Offload the ACA warrant.** §2's warrant subsection compresses to ~2 paragraphs +
   citation of the ACA paper (the canonical development, per the locked portfolio
   decision). The five-tradition lineage, Blub, interactive proofs, the
   phenomenal-access generalization — all pointers now. This is the single biggest
   recovery and improves the dependency architecture (the bridge doc already fixed the
   direction: companion cites ACA as warrant).
2. **Take the sufficiency fallback.** Replace the sufficiency-by-trichotomy argument
   with the already-drafted footnote posture: five factors as the necessary set,
   sufficiency conjectural pending operationalisation (§5). This kills the paper's
   weakest leg (a referee constructs a category-escaping sixth candidate — e.g.
   autopoietic precariousness — faster than the trichotomy can absorb it), removes a
   likely referee fight, and shortens.
3. **Compress the sub-scope lattice** (primitive/scaffolded/closed-loop + channel
   collapse) to its conclusion: the conditions apply at the closed-loop sub-scope;
   mechanism in a footnote or the formal companion. The "obstructed, not absent"
   material is evocative but is upper-bound-flavored in a paper whose own discipline
   forbids that register; one sentence suffices.
4. **Halve §4 (comparative models).** Keep Linarelli (contract as special case of
   compact — genuinely load-bearing and the closest predecessor) and the fiduciary
   bilateralization. Corporate/group/legal-fiction/tool compress to a paragraph of
   contrasts.
5. **Trim §5 operationalisation** to the Birch adjacency + the three-valued verdict +
   one architectural-correlates paragraph; the factor-(v) correlate details go to
   companion work.
6. **Keep at full strength:** the six components (especially 5 and 6 — component 6 is
   the paper's most distinctive claim), the anti-occlusion engagement with Soulier, the
   three-level structure (L1/L2/L3), and the production-disclosure move.

## Must-inherit (from ACA-AGENCY-BRIDGE, recorded action item)

The resubmitted version must carry **dimension-locality**: scope claims read
dimension-relative, not system-global; factor (iii)'s two-asymmetry distinction
explicitly connected to the locality point. The current draft treats
containment/asymmetry globally — the bridge doc flags this as a real cross-paper item,
and it is also a clarity gain (the global reading is the one referees attack).

## Resubmission query (sketch — confident, short, no self-deprecation)

The legitimate editor touchpoint is a resubmission query, not a self-review and not an
apology. Three sentences of the right register: the paper articulates a fifth position
(structurally-grounded extension) within the inferentialist CE framework; the original
submission carried its warrant argument in-line, which has since become a standalone
companion, allowing a version at roughly N words; would a resubmission at that length be
welcome for the special issue or the regular track? Field-observation material ("the
world is paying attention to philosophy in a way it hasn't in decades") belongs — if
anywhere — in a cover letter as an observation about the moment, never as an apology
about preparedness.
