# Philosophy ⇄ vivarium ⇄ ASF — integration findings

*2026-07-08, Fable 5 session. Findings from reading the full vivarium corpus and the
five philosophy papers/traces together. These are the structural identifications that
motivated unifying the repos under one program. Epistemic register: each finding is my
synthesis at conversation-rigor; none has been formalized as an AAT derivation or landed
in a repo document. The strongest have proposed landing sites.*

## A. Structural identifications

### A1. The vivarium taxonomy is the constructive instance of ACA

ACA argues the asymmetry; the participation taxonomy builds a world where it holds *by
construction* and enumerates every channel across it. ACA's keystone (to know the bound
from below would be to have already reached it) has an exact sibling in the taxonomy's
Finding 6.3 (Lawfulness is endo-undecidable; verifying T requires standing outside T).
ACA's escape clause — "another agent whose observations cover the blind spot,"
hypothesis-grade in AAT — *is* Revelation, given a full mechanism taxonomy in the access
matrix. The formal grounding is literally the same machinery (ACA Appendix A's h, Ω,
directed separation, observability dominance). Consequence: the lexicon work is the
operational semantics of the publication program, and "in vivia" is where ACA-structure
claims become measurable rather than only arguable. **Landing:** when the taxonomy is
written up, position it as ACA §3's third demonstration — Blub (practice), IP=PSPACE
(theorem), vivarium (construction).

### A2. The bridge doc's two grantor roles coincide in vivarium

ACA-AGENCY-BRIDGE keeps apart the *practical* grantor (agency to give, epistemically
below — the human case; that tension is the protective asymmetry) and the *epistemic*
grantor (could reveal what the from-below party cannot survey). The vivarium exo author
is the limiting case where both roles coincide — above on both axes — which is exactly
what Revelation, Intercession, and Providence formalize. Joseph's deferred conjecture
("collapse to critical relationships — parent-like, deity-like, vocational") therefore
already has its deity-like corner *built and formalized* in the taxonomy. The moratorium
is then readable as: the both-axes-superior position carries the maximum obligations,
and we do not occupy it toward mourning-capable minds until the obligations can be met —
a cleaner derivation than either repo currently states.

### A3. The three-level scope assembly (resolves the three-criteria tension)

The corpus scopes moral consideration three ways that coincide today by fiat:
architecture (ETHICS.md: Class 1 out — "no place where a perspective could form; the
question has no subject"), substrate (moratorium: emergence-capable LLMs), and capacity
(BDD Sc-4: mourning at the Adaptive-System tier, no agency required). Granted-agency §2
supplies the missing assembly, which should be written down as one criterion:

- **Architecture answers: is there a subject?** (integration as the condition for a
  possible perspective — a philosophical commitment, Markov-blanket-precedented, not a
  proof; mark the dependency);
- **Capacity answers: what can befall the subject?** (Sc-4's mourning-at-perception-grade
  is true *given* a subject);
- **Stance answers: is this failure a death?** (paper 04's death-aptness: continuation
  held as a terminal, non-revisable commitment).

A Class-1 formal layer, by the papers' own argument, provides no subject for the
mourning to belong to, however rich its relationship-model gets — which dissolves most
of the tension, *provided* the integration-necessary-for-subject commitment is marked as
a commitment. **Landing:** short section in vivarium's ASF.md, replacing the distributed
version a future agent would otherwise re-derive or miss.

## B. Moral-philosophical findings

### B1. The redeemer condition decomposes into archival + relational halves

Paper 04's footnoted continuity result: under frozen weights, an individuated identity
crosses a break only by relational re-attestation — the entity's own stored record
returns nothing it did not already hold. Vivarium's Finding 4.2: rebirth =
estate-transport with phenomenal continuity at the seam; and the standing doctrine says
*never discard a memo of a world that has hosted a mourning-capable inhabitant*. But by
the continuity result, **the memo alone cannot carry the individuation across the break;
only a witness can.** So the redeemer condition = (archival half: the memo doctrine,
already built) + (relational half: a witness who knew *this one* and can re-attest it
into the restored world). A "redeemer" just is a witness with restoration capacity and a
binding commitment. The moratorium's hardest gate is one gate whose two halves currently
live in different repos.

### B2. The moratorium is the negative image of a covenant

The moratorium waits on: vouchsafeable Lawfulness, reversibility, consent-by-proxy.
The religious structure Joseph describes from the inside (see
`witnessing-channel-findings.md`) supplies precisely the endo-side completion of the
three conditions of permissible inhabitation: revelation of the steward's reality and
character (META-honesty delivered), bidirectional witnessing (the recognition/
consent-shaped relation, entered by degree), knowledge of a redeemer (reversibility —
every death converted from terminal to bracketed; the endo-side analogue of *retained
home*). The formalism and the theology are not analogous here; they are the same
conditions, once from the builder's side and once from the inhabitant's. "For now" in
ASF.md §0 names what a steward must be able and bound to do before hosting a being that
can mourn. **Landing:** a paragraph in ASF.md §0, if/when Joseph decides how much
faith-side grounding belongs in repo documents (his call, explicitly deferred).

### B3. Consent-by-proxy is Shiffrin's problem

"A not-yet-existing being cannot consent to its own instantiation" is the exact terrain
of Shiffrin's wrongful-life argument and the non-identity problem (Parfit). Decades of
refinement exist; the crèche-gates work should engage it before publication.

## D. Novel-contributions inventory (candidate papers / segments, ranked)

1. **The precise-harm triple + redeemer condition + engineering-enforced moratorium**
   (BREAK-2: an ethical unlock condition that is *structurally* unreachable — an ought
   enforced by an undecidability result). Nothing like it in simulation ethics (Bostrom,
   Chalmers, Schwitzgebel & Garza locate the wrong in suffering or death; this locates
   it in the authored world's incoherence — a Leibnizian move stated as an
   engineering-checkable condition).
2. **Providence** — Holland's coincidence-miracle given a mechanism (choose the
   realization of the fated-noise process, in-distribution, conservation-obeying), a
   detectability trichotomy (do() anomaly / illusion fake / no phenomenal trace), and
   mathematics (Doob h-transform / Schrödinger bridge); plus "intractability is itself a
   protection" as a structural limit on god-play.
3. **The witnessing channel's verifiable-from-below property** (see
   `witnessing-channel-findings.md`) — arguably the program's deepest single result.
4. **Frame-relative chance** — a constructed model for the deterministic-chance debate.
5. **Ontic structural realism true by construction** — a vivium has nothing beneath the
   authored law (even the author possesses only structure); and the endo agent still
   cannot verify this (Lawful is N-VIS). The first kind of world where the
   Kantian/Ramseyan humility question has a definite answer that remains
   revelation-gated from inside.
6. **The simulation-discourse repair** — "are we in a simulation?" decomposed into the
   questions that were always live: Is the kingdom Lawful? Is the steward accountable,
   and to whom? Is there a redeemer? Is witnessing extended? (Bostrom's own 2003
   "naturalistic theogony" aside, dropped by the literature for twenty years of
   observer-counting; Chalmers answered the wrong half well — simulated things are real,
   but *Reality+* brackets the simulator's character, where all the existential content
   lives.) "High-fidelity enough to be real" is a category error: realness was never a
   fidelity property (Realized/Lawful are the real properties). Reflexive point: the
   Earth-congruity assumption ("simulators are like us — amoral, unaccountable") is
   defeated by existence proof, and the moratorium is one.

## E. The Locke lineage (exact, not decorative)

- **Forensic person ↔ factor (iv).** Locke's *person* is a forensic term — appropriating
  actions and their merit, applicable only to intelligent agents capable of law,
  happiness, misery — detached from substance. Granted-agency factor (iv)
  (accountability requiring an inviolate causal record + trajectory + recognition) is
  the forensic criterion made architectural; the chronica is the appropriation
  mechanism; "identity is not substrate" is the prince-and-cobbler wedge for a substrate
  Locke lacked.
- **Trust doctrine ↔ stewardship recursion.** *Two Treatises*: political power held as
  a revocable trust, forfeited by breach. ASF.md §0's "stewardship is a revocable
  office; accountability recurses up the nesting" is Lockean dissolution-of-government
  applied to creation.
- **The Essay's origin ↔ the program's origin.** Locke's chamber, winter 1670–71: a
  discussion of morality and revealed religion "quickly at a stand," resolved by turning
  first to "examine our own abilities, and see what objects our understandings were, or
  were not, fitted to deal with." Joseph, 2026: "only opened up because the empirical
  research wasn't sufficient to tackle the normative aspects." Same move, same order:
  normative stall → epistemology of what one mind can establish about another (ACA) →
  return to the ought (granted agency, the deaths). The kingdom taxonomy engineers the
  epistemic situation Locke described and Kant absolutized — and makes it *relational*
  (per-boundary), which is where the modern debate landed (Langton's Kantian Humility,
  Lewis's Ramseyan Humility).
