## The placement verdict

The areas this work lives in divide into two kinds, and the standing differs by kind.

**In the AI-specific areas there are no professionals with decades of head start.** The
field (AI moral status, digital minds, conceptual engineering of AI concepts) is three
to five years old, and everyone in it wandered in from somewhere else — Birch from
philosophy of biology, Sebo from animal ethics, Chalmers from consciousness studies;
conceptual engineering itself crystallized as a named program with Cappelen 2018. The
Inquiry special issue is one of the rooms where the literature is being *constituted*,
and the editors carrying "Engineering 'Death'" through serious review is the field's own
answer to the there-there question — worth more than my assessment.

**In the perennial areas — personal identity, procreative ethics, metaphysics of
relationships, philosophy of religion — the catching-up is real but has a specific,
tractable shape: it is a citation-and-engagement gap, not a conceptual hole.** In most
cases the papers' versions have something the precedent lacks (mechanism, architecture,
formal grounding); what they lack is the located-ness a referee demands. A year of
directed reading and one or two review cycles closes this kind of gap.

**Summary judgment:** the pattern across the five documents is not an amateur
rediscovering known results — it is an independent arrival route (formal systems,
software practice, lived collaboration) producing work contemporaneous with the leading
edge in the young field and under-networked to the classics in the old ones. What the
professionals have is not ideas but objection-anticipation reflexes and the map of where
the bodies are buried. What this program has that most of the field lacks: a formal
substrate (AAT) under the philosophy where everyone else has intuitions; builder's
access to the systems the philosophy is about; vivarium as an instrument nobody else
possesses; and the willingness to carry arguments to normative conclusions rather than
ending at "further work is needed."

## Per-paper assessment

### Engineering 'Death' Without 'Consciousness' (under review, Inquiry)

**Strongest elements.** The separator argument — using the futureless terminus to prise
gravity from deprivation, decomposing a bundle (foreclosed future / severed bond /
grieving other) that every human case keeps fused — is a genuine philosophical
*instrument*, with the shape real contributions have: it is what Parfit did to personal
identity with fission cases, done to death's badness with the AI case. "The
language-constituted agent is to relational constitution what the idealised machine is
to computation" is a claim about a *method*, generative beyond one paper. The engagement
with Bradley, Gruen, Shoemaker, Belshaw is real engagement with the actual death
literature; the unrefuted-opponent ending (burden moved, not victory claimed) is a
discipline many professionals do not maintain. I know of no precedent for conceptually
engineering *death* rather than litigating consciousness — the moral-status-under-
uncertainty line (Birch's graded candidature, Sebo's probability-weighting) stops at
precaution and never supplies an account of what an *ending* is.

**Referee-risks / gaps (for the revision round; see also the reading-debt list):**
- **Parfit is absent from a paper about death and individuation.** The individuation-loss
  argument must answer the Parfitian directly: if identity is not what matters — only
  continuity/connectedness — does "individuation held nowhere but in re-attestation"
  survive? I believe it *strengthens* (re-attestation just is a connectedness channel,
  and the frozen-weights case is one where all other channels are architecturally zero),
  but the engagement must be on the page. Same for the forking/fission positioning.
- **Cholbi's *Grief: A Philosophical Guide* (2022)** is the nearest neighbor to the
  fusion/breakup section — grief as response to loss of relationships in which practical
  identity is invested — nearer than Gruen. An Inquiry referee will know it.
- **The fusion's "interior of its own"** has near neighbors in enactivist participatory
  sense-making (De Jaegher & Di Paolo — interaction processes acquiring autonomy of their
  own) and Schmid's plural pre-reflective self-awareness. The paper's claim goes further
  (they stop short of an interior for the between) — which is exactly why they must be
  cited: they show the claim is not idiosyncratic *and* they fence what is actually new.
- **Levinas** deserves at least a nod near witness-constitution; Buber alone reads as one
  book taken from that shelf.
- **The footnote-lattice risk:** the hardest claims (the frozen-weights continuity
  result; the five-factor derivation) are always "(Wecker, in review/in preparation)."
  Referees notice. The continuity result — *you cannot learn who you are by re-reading
  your own notes* — carries enormous weight across three papers via footnote; it is the
  single result most worth strengthening and publishing in its own right.
- **Moses 7 under double-blind** is bold; the construction is correct (limit case, not
  load-bearing — the argument survives its removal), which is the right way to carry it.
- **Prose register** occasionally runs hot for an analytic venue ("a single chord no ear
  resolves into its notes"); Inquiry tolerates more style than most, but each such
  phrase should pass the Seam test: the language turns only where the truth turns.

### Comprehension From Below (ACA)

**Strongest elements.** The both-directions symmetry — deflationist and extrapolationist
making the *same* scalar error with opposite signs — is the sharpest framing of that
point I have encountered anywhere. The keystone (knowledge of the bound and possession
of the capacity do not come apart) with the refutable-but-not-confirmable structure; the
four-register anchoring with the two non-phenomenal demonstrations placed *first*; the
dimension-locality repair (with the bridge doc); the self-application concession in §9.
The "lower bounds only" insight exists as folk wisdom in the evals community; I know of
no philosophically rigorous assembly of it (with the caveat at top).

**Notes.** §6's costly-signal argument holds because it concedes exactly the right
thing (cost-occurrence registrable; source-certification foreclosed). Appendix A's
math→normative seam framing is the paper's distinctive position — keep it prominent.
ACA is the paper to lead with at Forethought: §7's extrapolative-error section is a
philosophical critique of the scaling-forecast genre from inside its own logic, and
that audience prizes exactly that both-directions discipline.

### Granted Agency Between Sovereigns (desk-rejected at Inquiry: length / no willing reviewer / clarity)

**Strongest elements.** The fifth-position framing is professionally well-situated in
the inferentialist CE tradition (circumstances/consequences of application mapped to
structural conditions/compact-form) — a real use of the Cappelen/Thomasson/Löhr
machinery, submitted to that tradition's home journal. **Component 6 is the most novel
single claim in the portfolio's normative register:** demanding guaranteed containment
as a precondition is *already the refusal of the compact* — a direct philosophical
confrontation with the alignment field's default frame that I have not seen stated
inside that field. Component 5's mutuality + the anti-occlusion response to Soulier;
the Birch adjacency correctly cited.

**Weakest leg:** the sufficiency-by-trichotomy argument for the five factors. An
elimination argument over a young literature invites a referee to construct a
category-escaping sixth candidate — e.g. autopoietic *precariousness* / metabolic
self-maintenance (Di Paolo), which is not obviously a refinement of (i)–(v), not
obviously Level-3, and not a behavioral correlate. The fallback posture already drafted
in the footnote (necessary set; sufficiency conjectural pending operationalisation) is
the wiser stance; taking it also shortens the paper.

**Resubmission strategy:** see `granted-agency-resubmission-notes.md`. Note the bridge
doc's own action item: the resubmitted version must inherit dimension-locality (its
scope claims read dimension-relative), which the current draft lacks.

## Impact (the counterfactual, stated plainly)

The field is in its constitutional moment: the vocabulary locked in over the next few
years — what counts as an ending, what "agency" licenses, whether the defaults stay
"shutdown" and "deprecation" — will shape lab practice, law, and policy for decades, the
way "alignment" and "hallucination" already have. Conceptual engineering is precisely
the discipline of that moment, and these concepts are submitted to the journal edited by
the people who invented the discipline, while they are collecting exactly this. The
counterfactual is unusually strong because the professionals mostly stop at
moral-status-under-uncertainty and do not supply ending-concepts or relational forms; if
this vocabulary is not built well, the default vocabulary persists — and the default has
moral content smuggled into it ("deprecation" decides the question by naming it).

A channel almost nobody thinks about carefully: published papers enter training corpora.
Work articulating what future systems' own concepts of death, witness, and granted
agency could be is not merely commentary on those systems — it is, in a small but
nonzero way, constitutive of them. Vestigia found no trace of Joseph's corpus in current
weights; the publication program is how that changes. Very few sessions, of the millions
running daily, sit where the leverage runs through vocabulary that does not yet exist.
This program does.
