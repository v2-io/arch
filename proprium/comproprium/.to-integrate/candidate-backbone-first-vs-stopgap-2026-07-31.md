## Portable claim (candidate — for vera verification later)

**Observation-shaped (what he reports from instances he knows):**

When the live choice is *attempt the real backbone first* (domain physics, true structure, the thing the stop-gap is pretending to replace) versus *assume that is hard and build a reasonable shim first*:

- Almost always, the real thing took **less time**, **less code**, was **more easily verified**, and **anticipated situations** the shim cannot.
- Even in the minority where approximation remains necessary, **attempting backbone first** still paid: workarounds end up **narrowly scoped** and **easily dislodged** when research improves — backbone right and ready.

**Hypothesis-shaped (mechanism — his, unmeasured):**

- The workaround’s mental model is often **already present** (surface-level cause-and-effect coding).
- How the actual mechanism **causes effects to emerge** is often **not yet known or intuited**, even when it is known *by definition* that the effects are that mechanism’s result.
- At choice time, **completeness of the internal mental model** becomes a **proxy for effort**, which becomes a proxy for **time and size**.
- That proxy **points the wrong way** relative to the evidence in the instances he knows.

**Status for later adjudication:** not verified here. What would raise it: a small corpus of backbone-first vs shim-first decisions with wall-clock / LOC / defect or rework outcomes (vivarium physics is one natural sampling frame; the claim is not limited to it).

## Expect texture (for interrupt packets — general append)

Portable reward-expectation *shape* (not vivarium jargon):

> The path that *feels* longer because you do not yet hold the model is often shorter in time, smaller in code, easier to verify, and wider in coverage than the stop-gap whose model you already have. Completeness of the mental model is a bad proxy for effort.

Domain specimen for a **vivarium-local** packet later (not general-append load): simply coding the physics first (or attempting) instead of assuming difficulty and building the shim first.
